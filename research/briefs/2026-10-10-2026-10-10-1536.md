# Brief — run 2026-10-10-1536: cheap acquisition on the new family's protected tasks

**What was tested and why.** Before this run, every success of the cheap A8 recipe came from families with
addition in the output. A8 means: four searches per source task, fit a decoder and fragment library, four
more searches under it, then refit. Last cycle's probe built a family with addition *inside* the
condition, `(Xa+Xb)>(Xc+Xd) ? Xe:Xf`. On four development tasks it looked very promising, but those
numbers were observations only. This run tested the claim properly. We made 24 fresh, independent A8
builds from the family's four source tasks and froze them. Then we scored them against the fixed
generic prior G4 on the eight protected tasks, which had never been searched. Three of those tasks use
an input pairing in the condition that no source task has. The pre-set bar was a 1.5× lower bound.

**What happened.**
- G4/A8 cost **10.1× [7.6, 13.1]**. This charges failed searches at twice the cap; at 1× the cap it is
  **6.4× [5.0, 8.0]**. Both bounds are far above 1.5×.
- A8 solved 317/384 searches and G4 63/384. A8 was cheaper on all 8 tasks. Every one of the 24 builds
  beat G4 by more than 1.5× (the weakest build sits at 1.5× under the gentler charge).
- On the three tasks with the unseen pairing the advantage was smaller but still large: 6.8× [4.9, 9.2]
  (descriptive).
- Old-family builds reused on this alphabet (O) fell between G4 and fresh builds. Fresh builds were
  4.3× [2.7, 6.6] cheaper than O.
- Source discovery was sparse again (64/384 first attempts solved). Eight builds had nothing to fit
  halfway through. That made some builds worse, but every build still cleared the bar.
- A build repays its acquisition cost after about 40 searches, counted in evaluations.
- Queue time was 63 min of 120 allowed. Data was complete and the code review passed.

**What we learned.** The cheap recipe is not tied to output-addition families. Rebuilt from sparse
sources of a family with addition in the predicate, it gave fresh search a large advantage on that
family's protected tasks, including a pairing its sources never showed. The size is inflated because G4
is weak here (84% of its searches hit the cap), and this is one family within its own bank.
