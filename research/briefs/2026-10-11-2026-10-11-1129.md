# Brief: run 2026-10-11-1129: can the library recipe learn new content (min/max helpers)?

**What was tested and why.** Every library success so far involved small sums, and a free hand-written
sum prior matched or beat them. This probe asked whether the same subtree-acquisition recipe can learn a
different kind of helper. The new helpers are pairwise min and max (`lo(a,b)`, `hi(a,b)`), each a 6-node GT/IF_GT
tree. We used a new bank: 24 source tasks `lo/hi(a,b)+Xc` and 30 destinations `lo(a,b)+hi(c,d)`, of which 6 were
searched and 24 are frozen as a protected roster. Five arms were run in restarted tree GP, 96 paired jobs each:
plain R-tree, the 1019 sum prior, a supplied 12-helper order prior (the ceiling), the unchanged literal extractor
(R-L), and a new behaviour-keyed extractor (R-S) that pools subtrees by their outputs rather than their syntax.
This is a `kind: probe`, so the results are descriptive. Commit `db30b15`; the queue took 7.5 of the 50 minutes
allowed.

**What happened** (geometric cost ÷ R-tree).
- Order prior **0.048× [0.04, 0.06]**, about 20× cheaper.
- R-L **1.55× [1.09, 2.17]**, with a helper in **0/8** builds.
- R-S **1.00× [0.68, 1.46]**, with a helper in 3/8 builds.
- The sum prior cost **2.12× [1.54, 2.91]**.
- Neither learned arm recovered any of the gap to the order prior, so the pre-stated rule for pricing a
  protected comparison was not met.
- Evolution does build the helpers: 63% of the 384 source solvers contain their own. But they come in varied,
  often bloated forms:
  - Literal keys pool a helper in only 5/96 helper × build cases.
  - Behaviour keys pool one in 49/96, but 44 of those lose to the 16-entry cap. The ranking counts source cells
    first; each helper can appear in at most 2 cells, while 3-node sums, comparators and a constant class
    appear in 3–8.
- Source search was cheap: 384/384 solved, about 4 k evaluations each.

**What we learned.** The unchanged recipe does not acquire min/max helpers here. The limit is in the extractor
(first literal keying, then cap crowding), not in what evolution builds. A library of content the target does not
need is not neutral: it costs 1.5–2× even on targets that plain tree GP finds hard. This is one probe on six
development cells, so it does not show that a fixed extractor could not help.
