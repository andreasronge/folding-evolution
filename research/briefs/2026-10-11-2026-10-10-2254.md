# Brief: 2026-10-11, run 2254: does frozen A8 beat ordinary tree GP?

**What was tested and why.** Every A8 result so far was measured against tape search, mostly the weak G4
prior. Strategy 2214 asked whether A8 is still worth acquiring when the alternative is conventional
closure-based subtree GP: well-formed expressions, whole-subtree crossover, lexicase, no learned prior. The
design was frozen and untuned. It compared 24 saved A8 builds × 2 seeds × 8 cells against tree GP on the same
(cell, seed) pairs, plus a G4 reference, on the DG (primary) and TS (descriptive) development rosters.
1 792 searches, evaluations as the unit. This was the third attempt. The first two stopped before scoring:
one on an impractical initializer, one on a seed collision in the plan. Commit `c32ff04`.

**What happened** (cost(tree) ÷ cost(A8); below 1 means tree GP is cheaper; 95% intervals):
- **DG primary: 0.96 [0.68, 1.35]** at 2 × cap. The whole interval is inside the 0.67–1.5 margin, with its
  lower end only 0.013 above it. At 1 × cap: 0.79 [0.58, 1.06], unresolved.
- The ratio near 1 hides two opposite effects. A8 solved 314/384 and tree GP 202/384. When tree GP solved,
  it was about 8× faster (median 6.8k against 57k evaluations). Its failures crowded the 32-token cap: half
  of their programs were at the cap, and 35–42% of offspring were rejected as oversize.
- Arithmetic: A8 saves about 86k [47k, 124k] evaluations per DG search, repaying its acquisition after about
  146 [99, 269] searches per build. Tree GP is 1.6× slower per evaluation, so it costs more worker time per
  DG search.
- **TS: 0.175 [0.142, 0.217]**, so tree GP is about 5.7× cheaper. It solved 382/384 against A8's 372/384.
- Both arms beat G4 by about 10× on DG and 40× on TS. All gates passed, and the queue took 48 min.

**What we learned.** A8's large gains over G4 largely reflected how weak G4 is: tree GP matches them on DG and exceeds them on TS. Against plain tree GP, A8 has
no worthwhile search-cost advantage on DG and is clearly worse on TS (descriptive). What A8 still offers on DG is
reliability: it solves more often, while tree GP is faster when it succeeds.
