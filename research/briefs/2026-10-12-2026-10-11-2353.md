# Brief: run 2026-10-11-2353: does input rebinding make the saved libraries more useful?

**What was tested and why.** The saved signed-gate libraries cut cost on the hard signed-chain cells to 0.78× of
plain tree GP. Their saving tracked whether a library held the needed helpers *with the right inputs*. On average,
each library held 8.6 of the 24 helper bindings. The strategist funded one test of whether literal input binding is
the limit.
- **R-bind:** every library entry is expanded into its 24 consistent X0–X3 permutations. Original weights are kept
  and nothing is deduplicated. The symmetry is supplied, not learned.
- **Comparison:** R-bind against the literal libraries (R-all), with R-tree and the supplied 24-helper prior
  (R-gate) as references.
- **Scale:** 24 builds × 24 cells × 6 repeats, 8,064 jobs. All cells are now development data.
- **Rule:** "material gain" if the upper bound of R-bind/R-all is below 0.90. "Small or none" if the lower bound
  is above 0.85.

**What happened.**
- **Validity checks passed:** R-tree solved 27% of jobs on the first attempt and capped 7%. R-gate solved every job.
- **R-bind/R-all 0.853 [0.785, 0.927]: unresolved.** The precision was as forecast (half-width 0.084), but the point
  estimate fell in the 0.85–0.90 gap. Harm is excluded. A saving above 10% is not established, and one above 15% is
  not excluded.
- **R-bind solved more:** 97.7% of jobs against 95.9%.
- **References:** R-bind/R-tree 0.66 [0.55, 0.80]. With acquisition charged, it repays within ten searches.
- **R-gate is still 2.5× cheaper than R-bind:** 0.40 [0.32, 0.51].
- **Where the gain came from (descriptive):** by how many of a cell's three needed helpers the literal library
  held, R-bind/R-all was 0.69 / 0.87 / 1.05 / 1.24 for 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 helpers. This survives build and cell fixed
  effects.

**What we learned.** Supplying input symmetry helps somewhat, but it trades dose for reach. It wins where the
literal library lacked a binding and loses where it already had one. Missing bindings do not explain the 2.5× gap
to the supplied prior, because R-bind holds every binding in 23 of 24 builds.
