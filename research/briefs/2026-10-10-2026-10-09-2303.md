# Brief: run 2026-10-09-2303. Does the cheap acquired bias (A8) hold up on a fresh bank? (root 10, question 37) — yes on one fresh shape: no material loss to the full pipeline, clearly useful

**What was tested and why.** A8 is the cheap pipeline from run 2033. It uses eight source attempts
per cell, half of them collected under its own bias, and costs 6.5 M evaluations against the full
pipeline's 61 M. On then-addition it was not resolved from the full pipeline. But every
then-addition result was on a development bank. Here the saved A8, S8 (static eight attempts) and
full-F builds, plus G4 with no fit, were frozen and scored on a fresh bank they had never seen.
The strategist's planned shape, addition in the predicate, failed the ≤ 9-token screen (4 of 124
behaviours survived). So the one pre-specified replacement was used: **two-sum-v1**,
`A>B ? C+D : E+F`, with 16 cells picked on labels before any search.

**What happened.** The run is complete and valid: 3 328 searches, 104 min of scoring, every gate
passed. Stage 0 chose the pre-registered 4-seed fallback. Intervals are 95% over 16 corpora.
- **Primary: cost(A8)/cost(full F) 0.945× [0.819, 1.092].** A 1.20× loss is excluded; the sign
  is unresolved.
- **Guards pass.** G4/A8 2.73× [2.36, 3.17] and G4/full F 2.58× [2.28, 2.90]. Solve rates: A8 51%,
  full F 49%, S8 47%, G4 20%.
- **Static collection is slower again: S8/A8 1.173× [1.036, 1.327]** (2033: 1.126×).
- **Harder bank.** G4 solved 20% here against 63% on then-addition. The fitted arms' lead over G4
  grew.
- **Economics.** With acquisition charged, A8 has the lowest estimated total cost at every
  reported horizon up to 4 096 searches. Unlike 2033, full F does not overtake it in worker time within that range.

**What we learned.** The cheap adaptive acquisition is not something that only works on its
development bank. On one new composition it costs about the same per search as the full pipeline,
at a tenth of the acquisition, and is still 2–3× better than no fit. That makes A8 the procedure
to carry forward. Our evidence covers one output shape and the existing builds, not general
transfer.
