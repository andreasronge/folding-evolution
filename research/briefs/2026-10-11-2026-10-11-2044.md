# Brief: run 2026-10-11-2044: do the signed-gate libraries still help when assembly is hard?

**What was tested and why.** Run 1938 showed that the R-all acquisition recipe cut tree-GP cost to 0.58× on a fresh
signed-gate bank. But those targets were easy: plain restarted tree GP solved 98% on its first attempt. The
strategist funded one cheap probe of a harder target from the same helpers: three linked conditionals,
`p(a,b)+n(b,c)+p(c,d)` (bank `x4-signed-chain-v1`).
- **Arms:**
  - R-tree: no library.
  - R-all: the 24 saved 1938 libraries, unchanged.
  - R-gate: all 24 helpers supplied by hand.
- **Design:** six development cells × 24 builds × 2 repeats, 288 paired jobs per arm. 18 protected cells were frozen
  and never searched.
- **Code and runtime:** commit `8a621df`. The queue took 15 min of the 55 allowed.

**What happened** (a probe, so these are observations):
- **This is a hard tier, not a ceiling.**
  - R-tree solved 30% of jobs on the first attempt and capped 5.6%.
  - Its mean cost was 159 k evaluations, about 20× the easy family.
  - R-gate solved 288/288.
- **The acquired libraries gave a small saving that is not resolved: R-all/R-tree 0.79 [0.60, 1.03].**
  - Below 1 in 6/6 cells but only 14/24 builds.
  - A 40% saving is excluded. Neither "any saving" nor the 0.80 usefulness bar is resolved.
- **The supplied helpers are far ahead:** R-gate/R-tree 0.30 [0.23, 0.38], R-gate/R-all 0.38.
- **The saving appears to follow helper coverage** (descriptive, confounded): 1.02 / 0.81 / 0.64 / 0.37 with
  0 / 1 / 2 / 3 needed helpers in the library. Only 3% of build × cell pairs have all three helpers.
- **Pricing:** a protected confirmation at r = 4 costs about 76–152 min of queue, with summed timeouts under 4 h.

**What we learned.** Harder assembly did not visibly raise the acquired libraries' relative value. The point saving
(0.79) is smaller than on easy targets (0.58) and unresolved; that comparison across experiments is not tested.
The absolute saving per search would repay acquisition in about 7 searches rather than 66, with an interval that
runs to "never". Hand-supplied helpers keep a 3× advantage. The limit looks like
acquisition coverage (libraries keep about a third of the helpers), not the difficulty of linking pieces. That is
a hint only.
