# Brief: run 2026-10-07-1707 (question 20; root 10 slot 13 of 13)

**What was tested and why.** Four procedures that selected decoder changes by noisy search cost
found no resolved gain from context. This run used a different signal: the token tapes of exact
solvers. Each corpus came from 48 G4 searches per training cell. From each corpus we fitted three
tables:
- **T**, token-only multipliers on G4, fitted by maximum likelihood.
- **C**, previous-token counts shrunk toward G4 (α 50).
- **K**, a token-only table matched to C's pooled emitted frequencies.

The run had 16 independent corpora per family. Each table was scored on fresh training seeds,
then on the three withheld cells.

**What happened.** The run was clean. It made 35 240 searches in 81 min. The replay was
bit-identical, all checks passed, there were no exclusions, and every collection cell solved at
least 40/48.
- **C beats T: 1.365× [1.288, 1.446]** on training cells. BE gave 1.46× and PA 1.28×, both
  resolved. C was faster in 31/32 corpora.
- **The gain transfers:** C/T is **1.293× [1.213, 1.378]** on the withheld cells, faster in
  30/32 corpora.
- C/K is 1.65×, but K is slower than T (0.83×). So matching frequencies does not reproduce C's
  gain, and C/T is the honest size of the gain from context.
- Against G4 (unpaired): T is 2.4× faster and C is 3.3× on training cells. T is level with the
  1723 search-selected maps (unresolved).
- **No family advantage was resolved.** Matched over mismatched C is 1.02× on the one BE
  withheld cell. On the PA cells it is 0.75× [0.63, 0.89] and 0.99×, so on one PA cell the
  BE-fitted tables are faster.

**What we learned.** This system's own solvers carry transferable assembly information beyond
token frequency, about 1.3× on unseen compositions. A one-shot fit extracts it at about a tenth
of the cost of the nested learners. None of the selection-based learners found it, and it is not
family-specific on this bank.
