# Brief: run 2026-10-08-2116, does feedback improve partial-program acquisition? (root 10, question 28)

**What was tested and why.** Run 1831 showed that a context decoder fitted to tapes from G4
searches that had not yet solved (C1) beats G4 1.62×. The question was whether to spend further
collection under that updated decoder (feedback), or under G4 followed by one fit (one-shot), at
the same source allocation. The answer picks an acquisition procedure. On the comparison-gate
training cells, 16 lineages (one 1831 corpus each, replayed bit-exactly) each got 96 sources per
cell per arm. F collected rounds 2–3 under C1, then under its refit C2, and froze C3. O collected
both rounds under G4 and fitted once. TF ran the same loop with token-only fits. R kept C1. G4 and
the exact-solver fit (C_exact) were references. This is iterated EDA-style external fitting (cf.
PIPE, cross-entropy method), not inheritance.

**What happened.** The run was complete and clean: 6 144 paired scored searches, 200 min, every
replay and hash check passed, no starvation.
- Primary F/O **1.18× [1.01, 1.37]**, 11/16 lineages. The rule says adopt feedback
  provisionally. A worthwhile 1.15× is neither shown nor excluded; showing it would take about 600
  lineages.
- **Resolved on BE only:** BE 1.42× [1.17, 1.72] (8/8), PA 0.98× [0.83, 1.16], unresolved. PA
  collection improved as much as BE's (sources under C1/C2 solved before the cap 27–30% vs 16%),
  so yield does not explain why PA shows no resolved gain.
- More G4 data bought nothing measurable: O/R 0.99× [0.89, 1.10]. Feedback over keeping the first fit is
  unresolved: F/R 1.16× [0.99, 1.36].
- Context feedback beat token feedback: F/TF 1.46× [1.23, 1.75].
- F reached only 0.31× [0.25, 0.38] of the exact-solver fit's speed, closing about a tenth of
  the gap. 1831's C1/G4 replicated on fresh seeds (1.62×).
- I expected F/O 1.1–1.3× (it landed inside) and O/R 1.05–1.15× (it landed below).

**What we learned.** Collecting under the updated partial decoder teaches a slightly better
decoder than collecting under G4. The gain is resolved only on BE, by a margin whose lower bound sits at 1.
Once the first partial fit exists, more G4 tapes add nothing measurable (a gain above 1.10× is excluded). The large gap to the exact-solver fit
is not closed by a couple of feedback rounds.

evaluations than O, so this compares equal allocations, not equal costs. Everything was measured
on the training cells of a development bank: no transfer claim, and order versus frequency is not
isolated. The BE/PA difference was not tested as a contrast.
