# Brief: run 2026-10-11-2150: do the signed-gate libraries pay off on hard, never-searched cells?

**What was tested and why.** The 2044 probe found a hard tier on the signed-chain bank (`p(a,b)+n(b,c)+p(c,d)`).
On six development cells the saved 1938 libraries gave a small saving that did not resolve: 0.79 [0.60, 1.03].
The strategist funded one confirmation on the bank's 18 protected cells. Those cells were frozen in 2044 and
never searched.
- **Arms:** R-tree (no library), R-all (the 24 saved libraries, unchanged), R-gate (all 24 helpers supplied).
- **Design:** 24 builds × 18 cells × 4 repeats = 1,728 paired jobs per arm.
- **Rule, fixed before scoring:** "saving" if the 95% upper bound of R-all/R-tree is below 1.00. A 0.80 bar was
  reported as a label only.
- **Code and runtime:** commit `83c7c5b`. Scoring took 85 min of the 192 min allowed. The grid was complete.

**What happened.**
- **Validity checks passed.** R-tree solved 27% of jobs on the first attempt and capped 6.8%. R-gate solved every
  job.
- **Saving resolved: R-all/R-tree 0.778 [0.696, 0.870].** Worker seconds gave 0.76. The ratio was below 1 in all
  18 cells and in 20 of 24 builds.
- **The 0.80 bar is unresolved.** The saving is 13–30%; whether it exceeds 20% is open.
- **Acquisition repays after 5.4 [3.8, 9.7] searches** (66 on the easy 1938 cells). Each search saves about 40 k
  evaluations against 216 k spent on acquisition.
- **The supplied helpers are still about 3× cheaper than the libraries:** R-gate/R-all 0.34 [0.30, 0.38].
- **The saving follows helper coverage** (descriptive): R-all/R-tree was 0.98, 0.78, 0.59 and 0.42 with 0, 1, 2 and
  3 of a cell's three helpers in the library. Most build × cell pairs hold at most one.

**What we learned.** Frozen acquired libraries still pay on harder linked assembly. The saving is modest in
relative terms (about a fifth), but large in absolute terms, so acquisition repays about ten times faster than on
easy targets. What limits the libraries looks like coverage: with no needed helper they save nothing here.

Re-extracting from the saved 1938 corpora under a changed rule needs no new source search. It would be a mechanism

The alternative is to stop root 10's library line here with a clear summary.

**Failures and uncertainty.**

- Two legacy tests in `test_signed_gate.py` fail at baseline as well. They do not touch this run's provenance.
- The coverage gradient is an association, confounded with library size. Only 44 jobs fall in the top stratum.
- Means are capped: 20% of R-tree evaluations come from capped jobs. Solved-only ratios (0.80–0.81) agree.
- Per-build rankings are noisy; R-tree's per-build means alone vary 11% from seed sets.
- Worker-second repayment (3.8 searches) mixes 1938 and current load and is rough.
