# Brief: 2026-10-11, run 0024: does restarting tree GP remove A8's last DG advantage?

**What was tested and why.** In run 2254, frozen A8 kept two advantages on DG over plain tree GP: it solved
more searches (314 vs 202 of 384), and it saved about 86k evaluations per search. Tree GP either solved fast or
stalled. Strategy 0024 asked whether a cheap generic fix, restarting, removes that advantage. Both procedures
ran under the same schedule: up to 8 fresh attempts of 65,536 evaluations each (the same 524k total cap). Each
attempt used a new population, new training cases and a new seed, and A8 kept its build. Full-cap A8 was rerun
as a secondary reference. The runs used the same DG (primary) and TS (descriptive) development rosters, the 24
saved builds, and 768 jobs per arm per family. Commit `1773d11`; queue 36 min; all gates passed.

**What happened** (R = mean evaluations of R-tree ÷ R-A8; below 1 means tree is cheaper; 95% paired-build intervals):
- **DG primary: R = 0.79 [0.68, 0.92].** Solved 765/768 vs 754/768, a difference of +0.014 [0.000, 0.034].
  This is the pre-registered PASS cell. Tree was cheaper in all 8 cells and in 20 of 24 builds.
- **Restarts helped A8 too:** restarted ÷ full-cap A8 was 0.73 [0.65, 0.81], and solve rates rose from 83.6% to
  98.2%. A8's reliability lead in 2254 was mostly an effect of the full-cap schedule.
- **Worker seconds point the other way:** 1.39 [1.20, 1.60], because a tree evaluation costs 1.75× an A8
  evaluation. Measured in seconds, A8 repays its acquisition after about 312 [211, 579] searches per build.
  Measured in evaluations, it never does.
- **TS:** R = 0.18 [0.16, 0.20]; both arms solved every job.

**What we learned.** Under a common restart schedule, an acquisition-free tree GP needs about a fifth fewer
evaluations than A8 on DG, with reliability within three points. A8's remaining edge is cheaper evaluations
per second. That comes from the implementation, not from what it learned. The bar for any learned bias is now
restarted tree GP, which solves 99.6% of DG jobs.
