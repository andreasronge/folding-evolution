the double-gate family (DG) is over the weak G4 prior. The strategist asked whether frozen A8 still beats a
conventional, family-blind structural baseline: closure-based subtree GP with lexicase, on the same DG and TS
rosters, with acquisition charged. The critic approved with notes (question 43).

**What happened.**

  compile to 16 tokens / 10 nodes. They and 1 998 random trees give identical outputs in an independent
  interpreter, the Python VM and the Rust VM. Overflow, conditional order, size reversion and provenance
  checks passed.

  fits the 32-token cap with probability 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ per draw: 0/10 000 accepted, about 3.4 worker-hours of
  initialization per search. The researcher stopped, as required.
- Nothing was searched: no A8 replays, no calibration, no target scoring. No budget slot was used.

comparison. A read-only check today found that depth 1–3 edges fills a population of 256 in 274 draws.
It also found that the frozen grow method would have made about a quarter of the initial population
single terminals, which would have handicapped the baseline.
