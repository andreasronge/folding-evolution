# Brief: run 2026-10-09-2033. Can the cheap four-attempt pipeline collect its own next source batch? (root 10, question 36) — yes, with no lock-in; the 1.10× bar is unresolved

**What was tested and why.** Run 1743 showed that rebuilding decoder C and library F from four G4
attempts per cell (C4+F4) loses about a third of the full pipeline's search speed. Here each of its
64 builds added four more attempts per training cell, in one of two ways. **A8**: the attempts were
run under the build's own frozen C4+F4. **S8**: they were four more G4 attempts. Both pooled eight
attempts and refitted with unchanged code. The question was whether a sparse, gappy bias improves
its own data or locks itself in. The pre-set bar for a worthwhile gain of A8 over S8 was 1.10×.

**What happened.** The run is complete and valid: 8 192 searches, 94 min of queue, every gate
passed. Intervals are 95% over 16 corpora:
- **Collection.** A8 solved 92.5% of its attempts against 57.6% for G4. It filled every empty cell
  and used 0.30× the evaluations of S8's batch.
- **Primary: S8/A8 cost 1.126× [1.039, 1.220].** A8 is resolved faster (14/16 corpora), but the
  interval covers 1.10, so the result is *unresolved* (rule 3). This holds under every sensitivity.
  Solve rates are the same (90.0% vs 89.7%); A8's gain is a cost shift among solved searches.
- **Against the full 48-attempt pipeline: full F/A8 1.031× [0.951, 1.117].** A8 is not resolved
  from full F, and a slowdown above about 5% is excluded. S8 sits at 0.915× [0.839, 0.998]. Both
  beat the four-attempt seed (1.52×, 1.35×).
- **Economics, counting acquisition in evaluations.** A8 costs 6.5 M, S8 10.2 M and full F 61.3 M.
  A8 is cheaper than S8 at every reuse horizon (resolved up to 1 024 searches). It repays its extra
  cost over the seed after about 44 searches.

**What we learned.** A cheap, sparse bias does not lock in. One round of collecting under it buys
better data than G4 at a third of the price. The rebuilt pipeline is not resolved from the full
pipeline's speed, at about a tenth of its acquisition cost. Whether A8's edge over static
collection is "worthwhile" (≥ 1.10×) stays open. That doesn't change the choice: A8 is cheaper and
at least as fast either way.
