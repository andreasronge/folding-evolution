# Brief: run 2026-10-05-1558, family bias on constant thresholds (sampling)

**What was tested and why.** This is part 2 of the README: can the map's bias be fitted to a
task family? It reran 1510's design with a task set that sampling can actually reach. We fitted
`op_weights` on sum>1 and sum>5 (Σ) and on max>1 and max>5 (M), then held out sum>2 and max>2.
The test compared uniform, the matched fit, the other family's fit, and a pruned vector on
held-out P(exact), with pre-registered bounds. Sampling only; no evolution.

**What happened.** The run completed in 38 min with no runtime cut. All six tasks were
eligible (122–288 hits in 200M). The verdict is **A** in both families. Holdout hits per 125M
tapes:

| Arm | sum>2 | max>2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| uniform | 173 | 69 |
| Σ-fit | **852** (4.9×) | 92 |
| M-fit | 176 | **612** (8.9×) |
| prune | 161 | 86 |

- Matched over mismatched was 4.8× and 6.7×. All adjusted lower bounds were above 3.
- Fit tasks gained 18–34×. The holdouts gained about a quarter of that.
- The fits suppressed the unseen constant: CONST_2 fell to 0.37×.

**What we learned.** A fitted frequency bias does transfer to an unseen member of the family,
but only through one op's weight. The reviewer found that multiplying four op folds (INPUT, GT,
the aggregator, the threshold constant) reproduces every rate within 1.5×. Moving the Σ
vector's aggregator weight onto REDUCE_MAX turns it into a better max>2 sampler than the M-fit.
My prior was C (generic gain), and it was wrong: mismatched fits gave the holdouts nothing.

**Failures and uncertainty.**
- This is one seed. The M fit rests on a single trajectory, because cold starts never reached
  10 elites.
- The product model was chosen after seeing the data.
- Specificity could hardly fail on this design, because the families differ only in their
  aggregator. The real risk was CONST_2 cancelling the gain, and it did not at six half-step
  updates. A harder fit might.
- The pruning control floored only NOP-like ops, so it adds little.
- The holdouts differ from the fit members only in a constant: a narrow sense of "family".
