# Brief: run 2026-10-11-1427: can selection carry an inherited switch that turns on a useful library?

**What was tested and why.** The program has shown that an externally acquired library (R-all) cuts
search cost on the order cells (0.40× R-tree). It has never shown that program selection can keep useful
variation bias by inheritance; root 23's token-frequency learner did not. Strategy 1427 opened root 55 to
test the easiest version.
- Each program carries one inherited bit. Bit on: half its mutation donors come from R-all. Bit off:
  ordinary donors. The bit follows the recipient parent, is never mutated, and starts at 25% of programs.
- Arms: L linked, S bits shuffled before each selection, Z linked bits with size-matched random donors,
  N neutral.
- Afterwards each library's mean final frequency was frozen as a global donor law and scored in fresh
  searches.
- Probe, observations only. Six development cells, 24 saved libraries, protected cells untouched. Commit
  `f8f4246`; the queue took 17 of the 55 minutes allowed.

**What happened.**
- The bit fixed in **46/96** linked runs, against 27/96 shuffled, 29/96 size-null and 16/48 neutral.
  L − S **0.20 [0.04, 0.35]**, L − Z **0.18 [0.05, 0.30]**. The controls sit at the neutral 25%.
- The driver is a small edge among selected parents, about **4% relative**. It appears only in L and only
  before the first solve. Extra reverts from oversize library inserts were matched in Z, and they did not
  move Z.
- Drift decided every run within about **15 generations** (328/336 in the first of 12 episodes). The
  other 11 episodes measured nothing.
- In fresh search the evolved law against the starting mix was **0.91× [0.71, 1.20]**, unresolved. Against
  plain R-all it was 1.21× [0.98, 1.46]. The dose curve is steep then flat: 74 k evaluations at
  probability 0, 45 k at 0.125, 32 k at 0.5. So the readout had at most about 0.73× to gain.
- My checks on the saved rows: during the selection episode, linked and shuffled populations solved
  equally often (55/96 each). One population's own law is 0 or 0.5, and that lottery would cost about
  1.2× the starting mix.

**What we learned** (observations, not yet beliefs). Program selection does favour carriers of a known
useful donor law, beyond shuffled and size-matched controls. But the advantage is weak next to drift at
P 256 with lexicase, so the inherited choice is a coin toss with a bias. A gain from it in fresh search
was not resolved.

**Failures and uncertainty.**
- No run failures: grids complete, audits clean, no bookkeeping alarm, 0 protected searches.
- Design flaw: the 12-episode exposure was 11/12 wasted. Per-library means are k/4, which makes the
  readout coarse, and 7/24 builds repeated the starting law exactly.
- Z's random donors are not neutral content, so content and robustness are not fully separated.
- The 1.2× lottery figure combines pooled dose points and has no interval.
