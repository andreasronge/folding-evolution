# Brief: run 2026-10-11-1315: does the uncapped library recipe learn a new kind of helper (rectified sums)?

**What was tested and why.** Run 1228 showed that the uncapped behaviour-keyed library (R-all) turns acquired
min/max helpers into a 2.5× search saving, but only on development cells of the bank it was tuned on. This
probe froze that recipe and pointed it at new content:
- sources `pos(a,b)+Xc` / `neg(a,b)+Xc`, where pos/neg are the positive and negative parts of a pair sum;
- destinations `pos(a,b)+neg(c,d)`.

Four arms ran on six development cells (96 paired jobs each): plain restarted tree GP (R-tree), 12 supplied
rectifier helpers (R-rect), 8 fresh R-all acquisitions, and the old order libraries transported unchanged. It was a
probe: observations only, with an advisory rule for whether to fund a protected confirmation. The 24 protected cells
were never searched. Commit `4a60cc7`. The queue took 19 of the 55 minutes allowed.

**What happened.**
- Sources were easy: 384/384 solved, ~166 k evaluations per build. But evolution mostly solved them *without*
  the helper, via the three-input sum (`a+b+c` in 326/384 solvers). Only 34/384 contained their own helper, and
  9/384 at a usable size.
- No rectifier entered any library: needed-helper coverage **0/48**.
- R-all / R-tree **1.03× [0.96, 1.10]**, so no saving. The old order libraries gave 1.04× [1.00, 1.08].
- Supplied helpers did help: **0.57× [0.50, 0.64]**, 73/96 solved against R-tree's 20/96. That is still below the
  pre-set bar (≥ 90% solved and ≤ 0.5×).
- All three advisory conditions failed. Every prediction in the proposal held, and the off-harness stage 0 was
  reproduced.

**What we learned** (observations, not yet beliefs). On this source design the recipe had nothing to pool:
evolution rarely builds the helper when a helper-free route exists, so the loss happens before extraction, not in
it. This does not show that rectifiers cannot be acquired, and it is not a verdict on R-all's portability.
