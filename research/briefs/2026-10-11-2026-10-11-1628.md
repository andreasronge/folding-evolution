# Brief: run 2026-10-11-1628: do the order libraries need their helper entries intact?

**What was tested and why.** The acquired R-all libraries cut restarted tree GP's cost on order cells to about 0.4×,
on both development and held-out cells. Cost fell with how many of a cell's min/max helpers a library held. But that
pattern was only observed, never caused, and libraries with no needed helper still helped a little. So we asked
whether the saving rides on the helper entries themselves.
- **R-hx:** the same 24 saved libraries with only their 4–10 helper-valued entries scrambled. Labels were permuted
  within each entry until no subtree computed a helper. Material, shape, slots and draw probability stayed fixed.
- **Compared against** intact R-all and R-tree (no library).
- **Design:** six development cells, 24 builds × 8 repeats, 1,152 paired jobs per arm. Commit `46e05cb`; the queue
  took 26 of the 70 minutes allowed.

**What happened.**
- **R-hx / R-all 2.35 [2.07, 2.66].** The pre-set bar was a lower bound above 1.25, so the outcome is *material
  loss*. R-hx was costlier in 24/24 builds, 6/6 cells and all 4 realizations.
- R-hx / R-tree 0.90 [0.83, 0.98], against R-all / R-tree 0.38 [0.34, 0.43]. That 0.38 replicates the earlier 0.40.
  About a tenth of the saving survives (in log terms).
- With the helpers scrambled, the coverage gradient vanished: 0.99 / 0.86 / 0.94 of R-tree, against R-all's 0.78 /
  0.41 / 0.22.
- The scrambled entries were used as often as the originals, so the library was not simply ignored.
- Solve counts were the same across arms (1,146–1,151 of 1,152).

**What we learned.** Most of the order libraries' saving comes from their intact helper entries. Once those are
rearranged into (mostly trivial) same-material expressions, the library is barely better than none. The useful
content of an acquired library here is a few ready-made computations, not its primitive supply. That makes
"do the sources build the helper, and does the extractor keep it?" the key condition for acquisition to work. It
also fits the earlier rectifier probe, where sources routed around the helper and the library did nothing.
