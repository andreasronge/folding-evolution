# Brief: run 2026-10-06-1603, four-reducer FIRST bank (root 10, 15)

**What was tested and why.** Root 10 has shown that learned token weights transfer about 2×
within one family (post-addition, PA). It has not shown that a map learns a *family's*
preferences. Testing that needs two families. This run tried to build them:
- Added FIRST to SUM/MAX/MIN.
- Screened 36 ten-token cells: branch-else (BE) `A?B:(C+D)` and post-addition `(A?B:C)+D`.
- Measured search speed on the survivors under 8 decoders, including hand-set BE and PA
  grammars.
- A learner pilot would run only if both families split and the time fit.

The steward's probe predicted that BE would fail its split. This was the first revision; the
1536 version was sent back for reading a missing contrast as incapacity.

**What happened.** Clean, complete run: 44 min, commit `92ba7c5`, validation passed, 5 200
searches. Outcome **row 1: candidate rejected.**
- 13 of 36 cells survive the alias screen (BE 5, PA 8). **BE has no holdout pair covered by
  training on any of three domains.** Its `then` reducers S, F and M each occur once. PA splits
  (2 holdouts, 6 training).
- G4 solves 45–50/50 on every cell, with medians 8.7k–28.7k, well above the 4 096 line.
  G4 / U is 8–10×.
- Hand-set grammars, matched over swapped (95% interval):

| family | grammar | its marginals | context over marginals | matched grammar / G4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BE | **1.68 [1.39, 2.05]** | 1.11 [0.90, 1.36] | 1.51 [1.09, 2.09] | 1.36 [1.04, 1.75] |
| PA | **1.32 [1.12, 1.57]** | 0.91 [0.76, 1.10] | 1.46 [1.16, 1.82] | 0.87 [0.75, 1.04] |

- The learner pilot was blocked for cost after the first block. On this bank a 4-trajectory
  pilot projects to 2.4–4.7 h, far more than 0132's estimate.

**What we learned.** FIRST does not rescue branch-else, so the symmetric two-family test cannot
run on this bank under the frozen rules. A fixed previous-token decoder *can* express a crossed
family preference here, and it lives in context, not token frequencies. But only the BE grammar
beat G4; the PA "win" is the BE grammar slowing PA.

**Failures and uncertainty.**
- No failures.
- The cost gate that blocked the pilot hinged on a pre-set 2× safety factor. Without it the
  pilot would just have fitted, but it was moot because the split had already failed.
- The grammar contrasts are for two hand-set priors only. They say nothing about whether
  learning finds such preferences; contextual learning was weak in 0132 and 0811.
- The PA contrast is resolved for the family as a whole, but on only 2 of 8 cells individually.
- A BE direction with one holdout cell (option 1) would be a weak test, and it would be chosen
  after seeing data.
