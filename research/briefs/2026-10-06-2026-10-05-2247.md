# Brief: run 2026-10-05-2247, composition bank feasibility (root 10, 11)

**What was tested and why.** Root 10 asks whether a decoder adapted on some operation
combinations helps fresh populations on held-out ones, beyond a token-frequency bias. Before
building the adaptation loop we needed a task bank: 3 reducer pairs ({SUM,MAX}, {SUM,MIN},
{MAX,MIN}) × 3 combiners (X+Y, 2X+Y, "SUM>0 ? X : Y") on the stack tape. The bank had to pass
three checks: no shorter program fakes a cell (exhaustive to 6 tokens); uniform search solves it
(≥ 70% of runs by 524k evaluations); and the held-out cells must not already be easy under fixed
decoders: token bias F, hand-set previous-token grammar G, and G's marginals without context
(G-marg). This was the third attempt; the first two were blocked by a merge conflict.

**What happened.** Clean run, 19 min, commit `0995d33`, 2 400 searches. Outcome **row 2**.

| Uniform U, solved / median evals | F | G | G vs G-marg (paired 95%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADD cells: 50/50, 7.9k–11.5k | 3.1k–3.8k | 768–1 024 | 4.0–4.5× (2.7–7.3) |
| DADD cells: 49–50/50, 15k–30k | 5.4k–8.7k | 1.8k–2.3k | 2.6–4.1× (1.9–5.8) |
| Sm-SEL: **27/50**, > cap | 30k | 4 096 | 19.5× (4.6–36) |
| Mm-SEL: 129/150, 112k | 28k | 3 584 | 9.9× (4.7–28) |

SM-SEL was dropped as an exact alias. With it gone and Sm-SEL at 27/50 (95% 39–68%), 0 of 6
splits are eligible. And even ignoring that, all 4 structurally possible splits lack headroom:
G solves the ADD and DADD holdouts in under 4 096 evaluations.

**What we learned.** The bank fails mainly on headroom. G's rows (INPUT → reducer; number →
INPUT/ADD/DUP/IF_GT) are the syntax of every program in it, so the "generic" control already
does what a learned decoder would have to learn. Context in the decoder is a big lever on this
tape: G beats its own context-free marginals 2.6–19.5×. But supply and mutation structure are
mixed in that figure, and in every arm search gained roughly a tenth of the supply gain or less.

**Failures and uncertainty.**
- Sm-SEL's miss is operational: 54% at the cap with a rising curve (a reviewer probe on
  other seeds reached 36/50 at 2M). A larger cap was not run and would not fix headroom anyway.
- The SEL medians under G sit on the 4 096 line (intervals span it). The verdict does not
  depend on them.
- G/G-marg is not a mechanism. Cells share seeds and training sets, so per-cell intervals are
  correlated. No learned decoder was tested; nothing here bears on transfer itself.
- 43 runs found training-perfect but inexact programs; the 625-input check caught all of them.
