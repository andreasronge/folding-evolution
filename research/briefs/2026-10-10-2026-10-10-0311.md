# Brief — run 2026-10-10-0311: a new task family for the cheap acquisition recipe (stage 1 probe)

**What was tested and why.** Every success of the cheap A8 recipe (four searches per source task,
fit a decoder and fragment library, four more searches under it, refit) came from families where
addition sits in the output. We built a new family with addition *inside* the condition,
`(Xa+Xb)>(Xc+Xd) ? Xe:Xf`, over four independent inputs (new alphabet `v2_x4`, 625 test inputs), and
checked, cheaply, whether it supports a fair transfer test: a valid protected split, whether sources
are discoverable, headroom, and the price of the real test.

**What happened.**
- Bank: 24 clearly different tasks; frozen split 4 source / 4 development / 8 protected (never searched).
  Python, Rust and the reference semantics agree.
- Source discovery is sparse: the generic prior G4 solved 22/128 first attempts (17%); median 2.5 of
  16 per build, below the 4/16 we flagged in advance. Half the builds had nothing to fit mid-way, yet the
  second batch solved 81/128 and all 8 builds ended with full libraries.
- On the 4 development tasks: solved G4 7/64, A8 55/64, old-family A8 builds (O) 22/64.
  G4/A8 cost 12.2× [6.7, 20.7]; most of that size comes from G4 hitting the search cap (57/64);
  at a gentler penalty it is 7.3×. O was 6.7× costlier than fresh A8.
- 448 searches, 17 min of queue (55 min allowed). Builds vary a lot (factor 2.2 SD).

**What we learned.** The new family is usable, and the pilot suggests the recipe learns it even from
sparse sources, with old-family libraries helping only a little. These are development-cell
observations from a probe; nothing entered the digest's beliefs.

**How the tree changed.** Closed 39 (bank question answered). Opened 40 (protected-cell test, awaiting
budget). Root 10 is at 31 of 31 slots. Fixed one overstated sentence in 38's log (critique note).

**Next.** Decision `next: strategy` (root 10 has no budget, and the strategy asked for a review). My
suggestion: 24 fresh builds scored on the 8 protected tasks against G4 (48 shared seeds per task),
useful if the 95% lower bound clears 1.5×; about 2 h of queue, 4.5–5 h in total. It would settle
whether cheap acquisition extends beyond output-addition families.

**Failures and uncertainty.** No run failures. Uncertain: G4's headroom is at the bottom of the band, so
protected ratios may again be cap-driven (solve fractions will be reported alongside); four development
tasks and eight builds give a rough variance estimate; one development task suggests transfer to an
unseen pairing of inputs in the condition, but it is a single task.
