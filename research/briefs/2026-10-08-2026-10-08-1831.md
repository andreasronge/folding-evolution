# Brief: run 2026-10-08-1831, can unfinished searches teach a context decoder? (root 10, question 27)

**What was tested and why.** Every useful fitted context so far was fitted to exact solvers.
That needs a lot of solved searches first, so it says little about how a map could be acquired
during search. The strategist asked whether the population before any exact solve already
carries usable assembly information. On the comparison-gate training cells, 16 corpora each ran
32 G4 searches per cell. Each search stopped at its first exact solve or 65k evaluations. At
generations 64/128/256 the unsolved ones archived 8 lexicase-selected parent tapes (S) and 8
uniform population tapes (P). From these we fitted a previous-token context (C_S), a token-only
fit to the same tapes (T_S) and a context fit to P (C_P). All were frozen and scored on fresh
searches against 1246's exact-solver context (C_exact) and G4. This is external, EDA-style
fitting (cf. PIPE, N-gram GP), not inheritance.

**What happened.** The run was complete and clean: 5 120 scored searches in 120 min, 89 536
archived tapes, all verified non-exact.
- Primary C_S/T_S **1.28× [1.12, 1.45]**, 13/16 corpora. The rule routes to "useful": the gain
  is resolved, and 1.20× is plausible but not established.
- C_S/G4 **1.62× [1.37, 1.90]**, 16/16 corpora; T_S/G4 1.27×.
- Most of the C-over-T gain comes from more searches solving within the cap (73% vs 66%). On pairs
  where both solved it is 1.05× [0.88, 1.24]. It is also BE-carried: BE 1.42×; PA 1.15× [0.96,
  1.38], unresolved.
- Selected parents taught no better than uniform population samples: C_S/C_P 1.04× [0.92, 1.16].
- The exact-solver fit is still 3.7× faster (C_S/C_exact 0.27× [0.24, 0.30]), but it used about
  7.9× more source evaluations per cell.
- I expected 1.3–2× for C/T and 0.5–0.8× of the exact fit. C/T came in at the low edge, and the
  share of the exact fit was well below that range.

**What we learned.** Populations that have not yet solved already carry a context signal that
a token fit to the same tapes misses, at about an eighth of the evaluation cost. It is
much weaker than what complete solvers carry. As far as we can resolve, it comes from the evolved
population at large, not from parent selection. Scope: training cells of a development bank only.
