# Brief: run 2026-10-11-1228: does lifting the library cap make acquired min/max helpers useful?

**What was tested and why.** The 1129 probe found that evolution does build the min/max helpers
(`lo(a,b)`, `hi(a,b)` from GT/IF_GT). The behaviour-keyed extractor (R-S) pooled them, but its 16-entry cap
threw out 44 of 49 admissible cases in favour of 3-node sums and comparators. This run removed only the cap
(R-all: every output class recurring in ≥ 2 source cells, 25–37 entries) and changed nothing else. It used 24
new acquisitions from the 1129 source roster and four arms on the six development cells of `x4-order-v1`: plain
restarted tree GP (R-tree), the supplied order prior (R-order), capped R-S, and R-all. There were 576 paired jobs
per arm. The run was pre-registered with one primary ratio and a fixed rule; the protected cells stayed unsearched.
Commit `60ab766`. The queue took 14.6 of the 110 minutes approved.

**What happened** (ratio of capped arithmetic mean evaluations, 95% interval over builds).
- **R-all / R-tree 0.40 [0.32, 0.50]**, which falls in the pre-set "useful acquisition" branch.
  - R-all was cheaper in 24/24 builds and all six cells (0.35–0.47).
  - All 576 jobs were solved.
  - It repays its acquisition after about 5 searches.
- Capped R-S / R-tree was **1.23 [1.07, 1.41]**: the capped library costs more than no library.
  - R-all / R-S was 0.33 [0.27, 0.39] on the same corpora.
- The supplied order prior is still **7.3× [5.5, 9.8]** cheaper than R-all, so R-all closes about half the gap.
- Descriptive only:
  - A needed helper is in the pool in 126/144 cell × build pairs (capped: 14/144).
  - Cost falls with coverage: 0.77, 0.45 and 0.23 of R-tree for 0, 1 and 2 helpers present.
  - 382/576 R-all solutions contain a needed-helper entry.

**What we learned.** The subtree-acquisition recipe can turn content that the alphabet lacks into a real
search saving. The capped ranking had been hiding it. I had predicted 0.8–1.0×, so dilution by trivia did much
less damage than I expected. This holds on six development cells of one bank. It does not show that the helpers
themselves cause the saving, and it is not a transfer result.

**Failures and uncertainty.**

- Helper causality is confounded: entry count, sizes and trivia share all changed with the cap. The no-helper
  pairs also beat capped R-S.
- R-all was designed after this bank was seen, and only cap = 16 and cap = none were compared.
- Uncapped libraries are untested on easy targets, where libraries have done harm before (question 50).
