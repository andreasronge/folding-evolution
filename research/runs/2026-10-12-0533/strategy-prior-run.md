**What the program has learned.**

- **The map changes supply and the fate of variants.** Random-genome frequency
  predicts easy tasks better than hard discoveries. Folding's tested fixed-target
  advantage largely tracks solver supply; in other settings selection beats
  random sampling substantially. Cheap joins explained the tested compositional
  advantage, while lineage mixing can prevent rare shared forms from establishing.
  There is no general developmental-encoding victory
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's solutions contain reusable information.** External context fits
  beat token fits, including **2.12× [1.86, 2.41]** on one fresh expression shape.
  Pooled/positional frequency replacements and undirected mutation-width recoding
  did not reproduce the gain. Fragments and inexpensive feedback improved the
  complete acquisition recipe. Outer selection learned useful token biases, but
  the contextual learners tried did not add a resolved increment. External fitting,
  outer selection and individual inheritance remain distinct answers
  ([10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md), [digest](../../digest.md)).
- **Stronger structural baselines changed the practical conclusion.** Restarted
  tree GP used fewer DG evaluations than A8, though more worker time. Learned
  tree donors then cut search costs substantially, including on a fresh shape.
  A supplied sum prior removed the average practical case for TS acquisition on
  B/C, and libraries increased cost on nearly solved TS targets. Acquisition is
  useful; necessity of learning and blanket deployment are unshown
  ([44–51 in the digest](../../digest.md)).
- **The acquisition recipe extends to conditional content and harder assembly.**
  Uncapped order libraries cost **0.37 [0.33, 0.42]** of R-tree on held-out cells;
  rearranging their helper entries removed most development saving, against
  replacements that were mostly trivial. Frozen R-all also acquired useful bias
  on a fresh signed-gate bank: **0.577 [0.47, 0.72]** of R-tree, outperforming
  transported order content. On protected cells of the harder signed-chain bank,
  saved libraries cost **0.778 [0.696, 0.870]**, repaying acquisition after about
  **5.4 [3.8, 9.7] searches**. That last test is held-out-cell confirmation,
  not fresh-bank transfer ([1938](../2026-10-11-1938/analysis.md),
  [2150](../2026-10-11-2150/analysis.md)).
- **Binding coverage helps but leaves a large acquisition gap.** Uniform input
  rebinding cost **0.853 [0.785, 0.927]** of literal reuse; a saving is resolved,
  the pre-set 10% materiality label is not. The supplied prior remains about
  **2.5× cheaper**. The coverage-stratum pattern is consistent with a trade-off
  between reaching missing bindings and diluting existing ones; it does not
  isolate that cause or establish harm within the high-coverage strata.
  Relaxing recurrence also admits much non-helper content. Neither change is an
  established solution to the acquisition gap ([2353](../2026-10-11-2353/analysis.md),
  [saved-corpus audit](../2026-10-11-2353/library-planning-audit.json)).
- **Inheritance can select a useful supplied dose; useful content preference is
  still missing.** Root 23's inherited token frequencies did not improve on uniform
  under its tested procedure. In 55, a recurrent selector raised library use and
  its frozen law cost **0.805 [0.704, 0.913]** of the start, unresolved at the
  pre-set 10% bar. The latest content-choice test instead gave **0.991
  [0.926, 1.060]** of the 50/50 start: pooled savings above about 7% are excluded,
  despite 22–28% headroom to the matched pure libraries. Preference moved on order,
  but fresh-search usefulness did not resolve in either family. The traces suggest
  that post-solve redraw erased much of the earlier preference; this is descriptive,
  not an intervention showing that a different schedule would work
  ([1736](../2026-10-11-1736/analysis.md), [0250](../2026-10-12-0250/analysis.md)).

acquired search bias, some family dependence, and indirect selection of a supplied
operator's frequency. They do not yet show a useful inherited content learner or
