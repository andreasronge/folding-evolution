This revision directly tests the remaining practical question with the necessary controls, and it now preserves an unresolved outcome instead of treating insufficient precision as evidence against frequency bias.

The four arms separate useful fitted transfer from generic enrichment and from a simple manual scaffold. The corrected hand-set probabilities, exact-solver checks from generation zero, and continued evolution after shortcuts address the previous critique. More sampling or another fit trajectory would not answer whether evolution benefits; no cheaper replacement separates these explanations as directly. Retain the analytic random-search comparator. A positive result can still be entirely supply-driven, as the proposal now acknowledges; it does not establish an improved search mechanism or isolate one op's causal effect.

**Size is reasonable, but precision is less assured than advertised.** Two families × four arms, initially 50 and at most 100 seeds per cell, is proportionate to the question and remaining budget. The quoted SE(log ratio) ≈ 0.14 is not a general property of 100 solves. For example, even with uncensored exponential waiting times, the asymptotic SE for a ratio of two sample medians at 100 runs per arm is about 0.20; at 50 it is about 0.29. With the proposed correction, a ratio near one would not typically establish equivalence at look 1 in that example. Censoring and different curve shapes can worsen precision. This does not require a larger experiment now: the second look and explicit strategy exit are appropriate safeguards. Treat the pilot as a runtime and censoring check, not a power guarantee; the assumption that evolution beats random search is particularly uncertain given §28.

**Carry three details into the implementation plan.** Choose the estimator before pilot outcomes are inspected, rather than retaining a data-dependent choice between KM medians and log-normal AFT. If using medians, declare comparisons unresolved when medians or their bounds are not estimable; do not rescue them with post-hoc parametric extrapolation. Freeze each comparison's verdict when it stops, even if a shared cell later receives more seeds for another comparison. Also record the crossover rate and mate policy explicitly: “crossover v2” alone does not define the evolutionary setup.

The outcome leaving us no wiser is an interval spanning both negligible and worthwhile effects after the final affordable look, including heavy censoring or indistinguishable early solves. The proposal correctly sends that to strategy. Conversely, median equivalence would support only “no worthwhile median-speed gain on these holdouts”; differing tails or solve rates at the cap must remain visible rather than being compressed into a general claim that frequency bias does not affect evolution.
