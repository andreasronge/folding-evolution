**What the program has learned.**

- **Maps and operators affect different parts of discovery.** Root 01 found
  random-program frequency useful for easy targets, insufficient for hard
  evolutionary discoveries. Cheap joins explained the tested composition gain;
  mixing between lineages blocked rare shared forms even though self-mating
  retained both discovery and establishment. The older folding/chem-tape/CA
  work supplies mechanisms and hypotheses, not general evidence that indirect
  development is superior ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Previously evolved programs teach useful bias.** Outer selection learned
  transferable token weights; the tested contextual selection procedures added
  no resolved increment. External solver fitting did: context beat token
  fitting about 2× on a fresh shape, and the pooled/positional frequency
  replacements and random widening intervention did not reproduce its benefit.
  Literal tape fragments added further speed; the tested pre-solve fragment
  source did not. These are successful external learning procedures, not
  evidence that evolution acquired their contextual structure
  ([digest](../../digest.md)).
- **Acquisition can be economical and depend on the family.** Sparse A8
  acquisition survived a new output shape and replacement source tasks.
  DG-acquired artifacts cost about 3× less to use on DG than TS-acquired ones;
  the reverse preference remains unresolved. Both tables and libraries carry
  useful information, with content, size and source difficulty incompletely
  separated ([41](../2026-10-10-1717/analysis.md),
  [42](../2026-10-10-2001/analysis.md)).
- **A stronger comparator changed the practical answer.** Common restarts made
  acquisition-free tree GP about a fifth cheaper than A8 in DG evaluations.
  A8 remained cheaper in worker seconds; this complete-procedure comparison
  does not isolate why. Gains against weak G4 are therefore insufficient to
  choose the next method ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md)).
- **Learning helps that stronger comparator, and some benefit transfers.**
  Frozen tree-source libraries cut restarted-tree evaluations to
  **0.390 [0.332, 0.463]** on DG and **0.250 [0.217, 0.289]** on the fresh
  gate-sum shape; worker seconds also fell. On gate-sum they solved
  1,728/1,728 jobs versus 1,726/1,728 and repaid this acquisition after about
  **55 [49, 62] searches**. But predicate-matched targets cost **0.055×**,
  versus **0.363×** and **0.338×** on the other two splits. The latter gain
  is substantial and unexplained. Size-matched donors exclude size alone;
  they do **not** exclude primitive supply. Few large entries surviving
  verbatim on unmatched splits does not exclude transformed reuse or
  intermediate effects. This is one acquisition cohort and a new shape built
  from familiar parts, with development looks disclosed
  ([45](../2026-10-11-0150/analysis.md),
  [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inherited learning remains missing.** Root 23's frozen frequency
  vectors were worse than uniform on sum; a max gain above about 1.06× was
  excluded. Ancestry helped versus shuffling on max without establishing
  usefulness. This limits one rule, exposure schedule and bank, not
  self-adaptation generally ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).
