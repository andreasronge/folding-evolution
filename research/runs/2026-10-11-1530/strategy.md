**What we have learned.**

- **The map changes arrival and evolutionary access.** Random-program frequency
  predicts easy tasks better than difficult discoveries. Folding's fixed-target
  advantage was consistent with sampling bias, while other regimes beat random
  search substantially. Cheap joins, rather than modularity itself, explained
  the tested composition advantage. Mixing between lineages obstructed rare
  shared-form establishment. These are scoped map/operator effects, not general
  superiority of developmental encoding
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's own programs contain reusable information.** Externally fitted
  context beat token-only fitting, including about 2.1× on a fresh then-addition
  shape. Pooled and positional frequency replacements and random mutation
  widening did not reproduce that gain. Fragments and cheap feedback acquisition
  added useful reuse. DG acquisition had a resolved preference over TS acquisition
  on DG; the reverse preference remained unresolved. Both tables and libraries
  carried information. Outer selection learned token biases, but the tested
  contextual extensions added no resolved increment
  ([10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- **The comparator determines the practical claim.** With matched restarts,
  ordinary tree GP used about a fifth fewer DG evaluations than A8, although A8
  remained cheaper in worker seconds. Tree-source libraries then reduced cost
  to 0.39× on DG and 0.25× on one fresh gate-sum shape. Yet a supplied sum prior
  beat the acquired TS libraries on gate-sum B/C, and acquisition did not repay
  against it through 200 searches. Libraries also harmed easy native TS targets
  ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md),
  [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md),
  [49](../2026-10-11-0833/analysis.md),
  [51](../2026-10-11-1019/analysis.md)).
- **Acquisition is useful beyond small sums, but its scope is narrow.** Keeping
  all eligible semantic classes made order libraries cost **0.40 [0.32, 0.50]**
  of R-tree across 24 independent acquisitions on six development cells, repaying
  acquisition after about five searches. The supplied order prior remained
  **7.3× cheaper**. Cap removal changed content, size and draw probabilities;
  the order helpers' causal share is unisolated
  ([1228](../2026-10-11-1228/analysis.md)).
- **Two latest probes locate limits, without establishing new beliefs.** The
  rectifier probe acquired no rectifier entries under its source design and
  gave R-all/R-tree **1.03 [0.96, 1.10]**. Supplied rectifiers helped but missed
  the continuation bar; this is a source/acquisition boundary observation, not
  proof of impossibility ([1315](../2026-10-11-1315/analysis.md)). In the inherited
  selector probe, linked R-all carriers fixed **46/96** times versus **27/96**
  shuffled and **29/96** size-matched controls. All trajectories absorbed;
  **328/336 did so in the first episode**. The frozen aggregate law versus its
  starting mixture was **0.91 [0.71, 1.20]**, unresolved. It did not demonstrate
  useful learning beyond the starting law or fixed R-all
  ([1427](../2026-10-11-1427/analysis.md)).

The last result distinguishes carrier selection from useful retained bias. Its
small positive selection differential deserves preservation as an observation.
It does not establish twelve episodes of task-family adaptation: the switch was
usually already fixed or lost. Nor do the shuffled arm's worse readout and the
pooled dose curve establish the cause of every cost difference; the dose groups
have different build composition. The steward's approximately 1.2× cost for
deploying a single absorbed population is a descriptive projection, not a tested
deployment result. More seeds of the same instrument would not repair its scope.

**Current line and mechanistically different candidates.** I searched the
literature for this review; the following papers were found, not inferred from
repository citations. Cost ranges below are local planning judgments, not paper
results or guaranteed runtimes.

| Candidate | Mechanism and decision at its full cost |
|---|---|
| Frozen-library reuse | [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-24855-2_63) transfers acquired functions across GP runs. Our R-all externally pools observed semantic classes and freezes expanded donors. **Pursue one 4–6 h protected-combination test**, including all agent work. It distinguishes useful excluded-combination reuse from a development-only success, without another learner change. |
| Inherited operator choice with recurrent modifier mutation or weaker drift | [Stephens et al., *Self-Adaptation in Evolving Systems* (1998; linked preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/adap-org/9708002) studies indirectly selected encoded variation probabilities. Unlike external extraction, selection here acts through individual descendants. **Defer a redesigned 10–14 h complete study**, including calibration and useful frozen readout, rather than the old 6–8 h confirmation. Mutation can restore lost variants, but does not by itself create deployment headroom. Symmetric mutation from initial f=0.25 also moves the neutral mean toward 0.5, so an improvement over the initial law alone could be mutation pressure. Reconsider a fixed redesign with matched mutation-only/shuffled controls and a credible useful-readout contrast; not just longer exposure or a smaller starting frequency chosen to manufacture a gain. |
| Parameterized abstraction | [Bowers et al., *Top-Down Synthesis for Library Learning* (POPL 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16605) synthesizes corpus abstractions using compression. **Defer 12–18 h** for adaptation, instantiation, validation and a consequential search comparison; runtime remains unmeasured here. It could pool across bindings, but the observed order cap problem already has a simpler fix, and rectifier sources usually do not contain the desired helper. Neither observation yet singles out missing parameterization as the decision bottleneck. Reconsider source content whose useful sharing the fixed representation demonstrably cannot retain, with an explicit search-use plan. |
