**What the program has learned.**

- **Supply matters, but does not fully explain search.** Root 01 found random
  program frequency useful for predicting easy targets, insufficient for hard
  discoveries. Cheap joins explained the tested composition advantage;
  lineage mixing blocked establishment of rare shared forms. These results
  favor concrete map/operator explanations over developmental superiority
  ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Previous discoveries can teach transferable bias.** Outer-selected token
  weights gave roughly twofold transfer. The contextual selection procedures
  tested added no resolved increment, whereas external fitting to evolved
  solvers did. Fitted context beat token fitting **2.12× [1.86, 2.41]** on
  then-addition when that bank was fresh; pooled/positional frequency controls
  and random recoding to match mutation width did not reproduce it. Exact
  fragments added useful search information; the tested pre-solve extractor
  did not recover their increment. These distinguish successful external
  acquisition from the evolutionary acquisition still sought
  ([digest](../../digest.md)).
- **Cheap acquisition learned some family dependence.** A8 reduced acquisition
  cost, transferred to a fresh output shape and survived replacement sources.
  DG-built biases beat TS-built biases about **3×** on DG; the reverse
  preference remains unresolved. Both the fitted table and fragment library
  carry information. Content, library size and source difficulty remain
  partly confounded ([41](../2026-10-10-1717/analysis.md),
  [42](../2026-10-10-2001/analysis.md)).
- **The comparator changed the practical conclusion.** With common restarts,
  ordinary tree GP used about a fifth fewer DG evaluations than A8; A8 remained
  cheaper in worker seconds, repaying acquisition after an estimated 312
  searches per build in that unit. This compares complete implementations;
  it does not isolate an engineering cause of the time reversal
  ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md)).
- **Learning also helps inside that stronger comparator.** In 0150, frozen
  source subtrees reduced R-tree's mean DG evaluations from **87,352 to
  34,109**, ratio **0.390 [0.332, 0.463]**, and beat size-matched random
  donors, **0.414 [0.332, 0.533]**. Worker seconds agreed; 1,536/1,536 learned
  jobs solved versus 1,524/1,536 ordinary jobs. The tested acquisition repays
  after about **54 [45, 65] searches**. Gains occur in all eight cells,
  including unmatched predicate pairings. This is strong evidence for this
  procedure on development data, not fresh-bank transfer or identified
  semantic modularity. Libraries are homogeneous; input/ADD supply, pair sums
  and predicate structure remain competing ingredients. Fifty-four searches
  prices this collector, not the minimum cost of acquiring the information
  ([45 analysis](../2026-10-11-0150/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inherited acquisition remains unshown.** Root 23's tested
  frequency-modifier procedure was worse than uniform on sum and excluded a
  max gain above about 1.06×. Ancestry helped relative to shuffling on max
  without establishing useful frozen bias. This bounds one rule and schedule,
  not self-adaptation generally
  ([root 23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

| Candidate | Mechanism, value and complete-cost judgment |
|---|---|
| **Frozen tree-library transfer: pursue** | External extraction from source solutions changes the complete subexpressions proposed during mutation. [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf) transfers acquired functions between GP runs; our fully expanded literal donors are narrower. [Stephens et al., *Self-Adaptation in Evolving Systems* (1997 preprint; 1998 publication)](https://arxiv.org/abs/adap-org/9708002) studies encoded mutation/crossover probabilities in changing environments. This is mechanistically different and directly relevant to the owner, but does not establish token-frequency learning. The existing plans price a usefulness-plus-transfer route at **10–14 h** before repricing a changed rule. Neither a new rule with measured signal nor evidence beyond the scaffold is available; affordability alone does not justify a retry. |

if it chooses between those procedures, or sooner only if a specific validity
problem makes the complete-method comparison uninterpretable. No such problem
was found in 0150's required inspection.
