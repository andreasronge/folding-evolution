## What I verified

1. **Arm wiring.** `roster()` keeps only F/W rows of the 1036 then-addition schedule, so cheap arms reuse 1036's exact (corpus, cell, seed) triples. `envelope()` gives both new arms the same C4 table and the F4 library of the row's (corpus, block); `jobs()` checks every (corpus, cell, seed) group has exactly one F and one W row with one `initial_tokens_hash` and one case draw. `execute()` is the module-level function (passed directly to `run_jobs`), so `empty_fallback=True` is honoured. `report()` re-checks that the full F/W/C reference rows share the training indices (paired cases, different initial programs, as intended).
2. **Block assignment.** `seed_ordinal` is the index within the historical schedule's seed order (not the numeric seed); block = ordinal mod 4; with 16 seeds every (corpus, cell, arm, block) has 4 rows, with 12 it has 3 (test covers both). Source blocks are `ordered[tid, cid][4b:4b+4]` in 1246 schedule order; I confirmed the collection schedule has 48 consecutive, monotone seeds per (corpus, cell), so block 0 is the audited prefix and blocks 1–3 are the next disjoint quartets. Failures are kept in the block (1024 of 3072 attempts selected, including unsolved).
3. **Estimator and extraction.** `small_source_counts` is per-cell 1600-mass normalisation via the legacy `transition_counts`, with an analytical 32-step G4 expected-transition matrix (mass 1600) for empty cells; `fit_context` is the alpha-50 C formula from `fit()`. Prepare asserts all 16 full-C hashes reproduce and all-empty recovers G4 bit-exactly (passed in smoke4). F4 uses the unchanged `extract_windows` whole-corpus rule (≥2 source cells, padded-solver filter, top 32); no min-8 gate, as the proposal says. Empty libraries route both F and W to W-with-uniform-3..6 lengths and the audit shows identical edits on 2048 forced edits.
4. **Primary metric orientation.** `contrast(rows, "full_F", "cheap_F")` returns exp(mean_corpus(mean_block(log cost_full_F − log cost_cheap_F))) with unsolved = 2 × cap, t interval on 15 df; cheap costing 120 vs 100 gives 0.833 (test asserts this and equal block weighting under unequal counts). Decision rule `lo > 0.833 → retained`, `hi < 0.833 → tight_loss`, else unresolved, matches proposal and plan. Secondary `F4_speed_over_W4` = cost_W4/cost_F4 (>1 = F4 faster), consistent with the repo's F/W convention; `W4_cost_over_full_W` is explicitly a cost ratio.
5. **Economics.** A+N·S in evaluations and in calibrated worker-seconds; cheap methods charge the mean of the four replicate blocks' own attempts (failures included) plus verification/fit/extraction; W4 charges the same (its length law needs extraction); full F charges the full 48-attempt collection once; G4 charges 0. Crossover N is reported with direction in both cases (initially cheaper/eventually costlier and the reverse); bootstrap resamples whole corpus records stratified by family with a fixed seed, G4 resampled independently. Test demonstrates the "initial win, long-run loss" branch.
6. **Gates and stop rules.** Prepare gates are provenance/determinism checks (source hashes, estimator reproduction, 10k-edit audits, 32 F/W + 2 G4 bit-exact replays on scientific fields only) that all passed at this commit; a failure in the queue would mean nondeterminism or environment drift, which is a correct reason to stop. Admission is timing-only: 1.15 × 256 × n × (mean_F + mean_W) / effective_workers ≤ 150 min, 16 then 12 seeds. Smoke4 projects 115 min at 16 seeds, and the score deadline (10 560 s internal) leaves about 1.5× headroom over the margined projection, so an efficacy-driven stop is not possible and a timing fallback still yields a decision (3 seeds per block). The score run replays the 128 smoke rows from the queue's own prepare before scoring. No seed-overlap risk: diagnostic seeds are checked at runtime against the 1036 and 1246/1548 schedules.

## Minor notes (not blocking)

- `build_source` calls `extract_windows`, which also builds the four unused leave-one-out libraries, so the cheap `extraction_seconds` is overstated several-fold. It is ~0.05 s against ~240 s of source time per block, so the economics are unaffected (conservative for the cheap arm).
- The worker-second calibration for G4 (0.626) comes from only two replayed rows; the output is already labelled as qualified. The analysis should lean on the evaluation-unit curves and treat the seconds curves as indicative.
- `prepare()` only checks that two G4 reference cells exist rather than that the 256 G4 rows cover exactly the 16 selected then-addition cells. I verified from the frozen data that they do (16 cells × 16 seeds, all `phase: fresh`).
- The first assignment of `audit_corpora`/`audit_libs` in `prepare()` is dead code, immediately overwritten by the `|`→`_` aliased versions. Harmless.
- Smoke rows (diagnostic seeds, not efficacy): F solved 56/64, W 59/64, mean 6.5 s and 6.2 s per search, max 33 s, close to 1036's F/W means. Three of the 64 libraries hold only 4–6 fragments and 14 of 256 source cells were empty (G4 fallback); the report records these per acquisition, which the analysis should read alongside the per-block intervals.
