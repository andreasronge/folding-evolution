The complete small-source comparison is feasible and worth its cost, provided plan.md makes the retention estimand and acquisition accounting explicit before scoring.

1. **note — Feasibility is credible, but W4 and the other source blocks remain projections.** The source audit reports 150/256 solves and four empty cells; the pilot reports 61/64 C4+F4 solves and 51/64 C4 solves. These support tractability, not a precise efficacy prediction. At those rates, 4,096 searches would yield about 3,904 and 3,264 solves respectively; historical F and W yielded 3,719 and 3,643. W4 has no measured solve rate or runtime. The proposed arithmetic gives 73 minutes before margin, consistent with recent queues, and 210 minutes of timeouts is ample if admission works. In plan.md, make the timing smoke cover both new arms, all four source blocks and both source families; state an adverse solve/runtime scenario, then freeze 16 or 12 seeds using timing alone. Keep the replay gate, scoped to deterministic scientific fields rather than timing metadata.

2. **note — Define the ratio in costs, not arm shorthand.** The decision rule requires `rho = exp(mean(log(cost_full_F) - log(cost_C4F4)))`: 0.833 means the cheap method costs 20% more. The repository normally uses “X/Y” to mean speed of X relative to Y, which would invert the proposal's “F/(C4+F4).” Include a numerical sanity check in plan.md. For four source blocks, specify equal block weights, use the seed's ordinal index for assignment, and retain corpus-level clustering against the shared full-source reference. These are four replicate acquisitions, not an ensemble fitted to sixteen attempts per cell. Report block-level variation as well as the average; average retention does not guarantee a reliable individual acquisition.

3. **note — Performance retention and economic usefulness are different decisions.** Implement the linked plan's arithmetic `A + N*S` curves, with actual capped effort and solve probability alongside the penalized geometric metric. Charge each block's own attempts, including failures, verification, fit and extraction; the 240 worker-s figure measures block 0 only. Charge one acquisition per deployed method, not all four replicate builds, and preserve acquisition/search dependence in the corpus bootstrap. Historical worker times need a common-hardware calibration or an explicitly qualified comparison. A tight loss at 0.833 rejects the chosen retention target; it does not by itself show that the policy is uneconomic at every reuse horizon. Conversely, retention alone does not establish repayment against G4. Report crossover ranges, including cases where the cheaper source wins initially but loses at large N.

4. **note — W4 does not establish “acquiring C alone.”** Its length law comes from F4, so extraction is still part of its acquisition procedure. An unresolved or sub-1.10 F4/W4 result can favor C4 with library-derived chain blocks; it cannot establish that a decoder-only method works, nor that fragment content adds nothing. No extra C4 arm is required for this complete-pipeline decision: narrow the interpretation and charge W4's length-law acquisition. Likewise, this design cannot determine whether F4 rescues a weak bare C4.

5. **note — Good value and appropriate prior-work placement.** This tests whether useful externally fitted map bias requires the large solved corpus, which matters more now than another `gt`-fragment knockout or a more precise W/R estimate. Four disjoint source blocks at unchanged scoring cost are a sensible improvement over one inspected prefix; historical reference reuse is the main justified saving. I see no clearly cheaper design that answers both retention and the value of carrying fragments. [Run Transferable Libraries](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf) is the closest library precedent; [DreamCoder](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf) is relevant to learning library and search policy together. The contribution is a priced data-efficiency boundary for this fitted chain/literal-window procedure, not a new library-learning technique or inherited adaptation. No essential missing precedent identified. Keep the development-bank restriction: any later transfer claim needs a fresh bank frozen with the method.

6. **note — State what would leave the acquisition choice unresolved.** The projected ×1.08–1.15 half-width is a scenario, not measured small-source variance. At rho = 0.95, ×1.15 gives approximately [0.826, 1.093], straddling the retention threshold. Such a result, combined with uncertain arithmetic savings and an unresolved F4/W4 increment, would leave the practical choice unsettled. Price resolution in additional independent acquisitions as well as scoring seeds; do not automatically extend the queue. A retained aggregate with poor individual blocks would establish average performance only.
