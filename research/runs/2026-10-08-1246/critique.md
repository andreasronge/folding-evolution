This is a tractable, worthwhile first stage toward a protected multi-composition transfer test; the researcher should resolve the operational and interpretation notes below in `plan.md`.

The counts reconcile: 3,072 collection, 2,048 C/T training and 256 G4 searches; stage 2 adds 4,352. Thus roughly two hours plus a three-hour timeout is plausible, not guaranteed: new-bank fitted-map runtime remains unmeasured. Time both fitted arms in the development-only smoke, including verification and fitting overhead. Explicitly replace 1707's **24/48 per-cell yield gate** rather than inheriting it accidentally: this bank expects only about 12 tapes in its weakest cell. Define whether 40% means pooled or per-family yield, how an empty cell is handled, and report distinct tapes per cell. Rescaling a dozen tapes to 1,600 transitions does not create 1,600 independent observations.

2. **note — Worth running; contribution is replication and a boundary test.** The important unresolved belief is whether the useful corpus-fit signal extends beyond the old bank's narrow compositions. Several protected behaviours per family address that gap more directly than another old-bank feedback iteration or a more precise old-bank interaction estimate. Stage 1 alone establishes training performance and the price of that test, not transfer. Reusing the same corpora for stage 2 makes this a reasonable staged design; I see no clearly cheaper design that supplies the same bank validation, acquisition replication and continuation decision. Preserve the deterministic, performance-blind holdout selection and freeze the complete method and scoring rule before any holdout search. The 12 probed cells remain development-only; new seeds do not restore their freshness.

3. **note — Closest technique is named correctly.** [Poli and McPhee's N-gram GP](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/9722/1/ces-479.pdf) learns instruction-triplet distributions for linear programs; this is a close precedent for learning sequential program statistics. [PIPE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) is a broader adaptive program-distribution precedent. Here the table is fitted externally once to solver tapes and then frozen for fresh evolutionary searches, rather than repeatedly updated as an EDA population model. The contribution is evidence about that procedure on these new compositions, not invention of distribution learning. No additional missing precedent is necessary to motivate this replication.

4. **note — Freeze an unambiguous endpoint, routing rule and precision expectation.** Define the speed statistic as `exp(mean(log cost_T − log cost_C))`, so C/T > 1 means C is faster. The stated branches overlap when the interval lies wholly between 1 and 1.10; give the continuation branch explicit precedence or choose a single practical threshold. An upper bound below 1.10 bounds the gain below 10%; it does not establish absence of an advantage. State the projected interval width at n=16 and the n=12 fallback using 1707's observed corpus SDs (0.27/0.16 log2), with a larger-spread scenario for this harder bank. Give a route for resolved C/T with insufficient collection yield. An interval spanning both no gain and a worthwhile gain leaves the central question unresolved; report that and price additional corpora rather than calling it a negative.

5. **note — Timeout handling must preserve the planned experimental unit.** Specify fixed, balanced BE/PA corpus blocks and seed blocks disjoint across corpora and phases. Complete paired C/T blocks in preassigned order; do not retain whichever corpora happen to finish fastest, since runtime depends on search success. If the queue stops, use only a predeclared completed prefix with its family weights, or report incomplete execution. “At least 12” alone does not specify a valid subset.

6. **note — C/T tests fitting procedures, not an isolated token-order mechanism.** With K fitted but unscored, a positive result establishes an advantage over the restricted token-likelihood fit, not that changed emitted frequencies cannot explain it. Keep that scope in stage 1. For stage 2, either freeze and price K scoring before touching holdouts, or retain the narrower C-versus-T transfer claim. Also report C/G4, T/G4 and solve rates: C beating a damaged T while neither helps G4 would not establish useful adaptation. This check can use the already proposed G4 searches.
