**What we have learned.**

- **Supply, discovery and persistence are distinct.** Root 01 found that
  random-program frequency predicts easy tasks better than difficult
  evolutionary discoveries. Cheap joins explained the tested composition
  advantage; lineage mixing obstructed rare shared forms. Natural helper
  arrival remains unresolved. These are map/operator results, not a general
  superiority claim for developmental encoding
  ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Programs found by search can teach reusable bias.** Outer selection
  learned token weights giving roughly 2× transfer. Tested contextual
  selection procedures added no resolved increment. External corpus fitting
  succeeded: context beat token fitting **2.12× [1.86, 2.41]** on then-addition
  when fresh; pooled/positional replacements and random recoding did not
  reproduce the benefit. The exact contribution of conditional supply versus
  structured variation is still open ([digest](../../digest.md)).
- **A complete acquisition recipe exists at this small scale.** Literal
  fragments add useful search bias; four source attempts, a fit, four adaptive
  attempts and a refit (A8) substantially reduced acquisition cost on the
  original sources. It helped on a fresh output shape and when rebuilt from
  complementary sources. The tenfold acquisition saving is specific to those
  sources. DG acquisition extended usefulness to addition inside a predicate,
  but its **6–10×** capped-cost advantage is against weak G4 on one bank
  ([38](../2026-10-10-0145/decision.md), [40](../2026-10-10-1536/decision.md)).
- **Acquisition history matters, and both stored components carry it.**
  DG-built A8 was about **3×** cheaper on DG than TS-built A8; reciprocal
  preference is unestablished. The new crossing reproduced the native gap.
  Bare DG tables were **3.26× [2.43, 4.30]** cheaper than bare TS tables on DG;
  DG libraries improved the TS table **3.05× [2.32, 4.07]** over no library.
  Replacement of the TS library gave **1.95× [1.43, 2.66]**, unresolved against
  the stipulated 1.5×. Thus both components merit carrying forward; neither
  is identified as the sole or dominant cause. Final bare tables were still
  acquired using intermediate libraries. The TS transplant interval
  **1.01× [0.83, 1.25]** bounds possible cost changes; it does not establish
  that transplantation is literally free
  ([41](../2026-10-10-1717/decision.md), [42](../2026-10-10-2001/decision.md)).
- **Useful per-individual inherited learning is missing.** Root 23's tested
  rule yielded worse frozen search than uniform on sum and excluded a max
  gain above about 1.06×. Ancestry helped against shuffled ancestry on max
  without establishing useful acquisition. That bounds one rule and schedule,
  not self-adaptation ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

**Current line versus different mechanisms.** Literature searched in this
review supports three distinct alternatives, not evidence that any will win
here. A8 belongs to the program-distribution learning tradition of
[Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic incremental program evolution*
(1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/), with external fitting
and literal edits rather than PIPE's particular update rule.

The selected alternative is **typed subtree GP**, following
[Montana, *Strongly Typed Genetic Programming*
(1995)](https://davidmontana.net/papers/stgp.pdf). Supplied type rules organize
construction; subtree operators move complete expressions. It needs no
source corpus or learned decoder. My hypothesis is that this generic
structural prior could replace much of A8's practical benefit, even though
the learned DG/TS contrast remains real within tape search. A8 instead learns
which sequences and literal edits to propose from prior successes. Comparing
the complete procedures answers practical value, not a pure causal effect
of learning. The candidate is a potentially stronger baseline, not a method
whose strength on these banks has already been demonstrated.

The owner's **inherited variation parameters** spread through descendants,
rather than through a corpus estimator. [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation
in evolving systems* (1998)](https://arxiv.org/abs/adap-org/9708002) demonstrates
indirect selection of encoded operator probabilities in model landscapes.
Keeping program/modifier lineages across goal changes remains a plausible
changed rule here, but that is our inference, not their transfer result.
The existing [equal-exposure plan](../../plans/heritable-bias-equal-exposure.md)
prices one usefulness cycle at 5–7 hours; a revised mechanism plus independent
frozen usefulness and transfer provisionally needs **10–14 hours**, with its
new rates unmeasured. The negative usefulness result and absent new signal
make that a weaker purchase today, despite its greater conceptual payoff.

**Callable abstractions**, as in [Ellis et al., *DreamCoder*
(PLDI 2021)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf),
could reuse computations through arguments instead of literal windows.
They require new extraction/binding semantics, and the current result reveals
portable literal content rather than an identified binding failure. Defer
that build until such a limitation or a cheaper complete test changes its
value. The paper does not establish that our windows are abstractions.
