  frequency informative about easy tasks, insufficient to predict hard
  evolutionary discoveries. Cheap joins explained the tested composition
  advantage; lineage mixing impeded rare shared forms. These findings guide
  map/operator comparisons without establishing general developmental superiority
  ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's discoveries can teach a reusable search bias.** Outer-selected
  token weights transferred roughly 2-fold; the contextual selection procedures
  tested added no resolved gain. External solver-corpus fitting worked: context
  beat token fitting 2.12× [1.86, 2.41] on then-addition when that bank was fresh.
  Pooled/positional frequency replacements and random mutation-width recoding
  did not reproduce it. The responsible conditional supply versus structured
  variation remains unidentified ([digest](../../digest.md)).

  fragment edits helped beyond the fitted table. A8's four-plus-four source
  recipe approached the original full pipeline at much lower acquisition cost,
  helped on a fresh output shape and survived replacement source compositions.
  On independent-input DG, its capped advantage over weak G4 was 6–10×.
  DG-acquired builds beat TS-acquired ones about 3× on DG; the reverse preference
  is unresolved. Both tables and libraries carry useful information within tape
  search; neither is identified as the sole or dominant cause
  ([40](../2026-10-10-1536/decision.md),
  [41](../2026-10-10-1717/decision.md),
  [42](../2026-10-10-2001/decision.md)).

  about **146 [99, 269] searches per build**. TS favors tree by about 5.7×,
