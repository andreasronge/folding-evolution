**Current line against different mechanisms.** Literature searched for this review:
[Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic incremental program evolution* (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/)
updates a program-generating distribution from the current best program. Our corpus
line similarly estimates distributions over fixed primitives, although its data collection
and frozen-reuse endpoint differ. More refitting now mostly elaborates known modest returns.

[Ellis et al., *DreamCoder* (2020 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381)
learns symbolic abstractions together with a neural search guide. The repository's
fragment candidate changes the units of variation: an externally extracted executable
block can introduce coordinated edits together. My hypothesis is that this could preserve
dependencies that token-wise variation repeatedly disrupts. It is mechanistically different
from changing token probabilities; the paper does not demonstrate that benefit here.
Root 01's cheap-join result makes matched edit-size, primitive-supply and execution-cost
controls essential. C beating K does not show that fragments are needed.

The owner's inherited-parameter route changes the credit mechanism instead.
[Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/)
studies indirect selection of encoded mutation/crossover probabilities. Descendant success
propagates those parameters without a corpus estimator. Root 23 limits one instance,
not the broader mechanism, but no measured redesign currently outranks the representation test.

**Next question for the steward.** Do C's per-position token frequencies, combined with
only the supplied G4 grammar, suffice for its search advantage—and can independent
per-position draws suffice too? This is worth the full cost because the latest K test
leaves a concrete alternative to learning new conditional dependencies.

I propagated the saved C/K tables analytically through all 32 positions. First-position
total variation is **0.0715–0.2104** across corpora, despite near-identical pooled
frequencies; mean positional variation is 0.0091–0.0168. These are descriptive table
properties, not evidence that position causes the speed gap. The plan records the source
and calculation. The residual alternative is substantial at the start of a tape.

Keep two interpretations separate: Q adds positional multipliers to G4 and **retains
previous-token dependence**; P independently samples C's positional marginals. Q succeeding
while P fails would support positional supply plus supplied grammar. Both failing while
C keeps a useful advantage would favour a target containing additional dependencies.
Success of P would favour independent positional supply. Broad or discordant results
remain unresolved. Neither target is automatically easier to evolve: an unrestricted
32-position categorical map has more free parameters than the current 25-row table.
Probability-vector learning has precedent in
[Baluja, *Population-Based Incremental Learning* (1994)](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/pub_files/pub1/baluja_shumeet_1994_2/baluja_shumeet_1994_2.pdf);
the proposed test projects frozen maps and does not test that learning algorithm.
