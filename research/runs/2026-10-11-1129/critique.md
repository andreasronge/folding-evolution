2. **note — Make R-S an executable rule before scoring.** “Unchanged ranking” is ambiguous once keys are 625-element vectors rather than syntax: the old extractor ranks by cell count, solver count, node count and canonical syntax. Specify per-solver deduplication, deterministic representative/tie selection, whether one-node subtrees enter pooling, and whether the 3–9-node emission filter runs before or after the 16-entry cap. This also makes the cheaper same-source census useful without adding search arms.

Helpers may also be acquired yet give little destination benefit. Report those outcomes explicitly. Keep the protected roster untouched and freeze both extractor recipes with it.

The local addition is a descriptive boundary check on acquiring conditional selectors, with a supplied order prior exposing the remaining performance gap.
