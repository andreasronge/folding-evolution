**What the program has learned.**

- **Map bias changes discovery, but random-program frequency is incomplete.**
  Root 01 found frequency useful for easy behaviours and insufficient for hard
  discovery. Cheap joins explained the tested compositional advantage. Lineage
  mixing blocked establishment of rare shared forms; self-mating relieved that
  barrier while retaining discovery. These are specific map/operator effects,
  not general superiority of developmental encoding
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Programs found by evolution can teach transferable search biases.** Outer
  selection acquired useful token weights, but the tested context optimizers
  added no resolved benefit. External solver fitting did: contextual tables
  beat token fits, including about 2.1× on a fresh shape. Pooled and positional
  frequency replacements and random mutation widening did not reproduce the
  gain. Literal fragments added further speed; cheap acquisition retained useful
  transfer. Partial-program and pre-solve acquisition were weaker at their
  tested scopes. None establishes inherited contextual learning
  ([digest](../../digest.md), [10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- **Family information exists, but a stronger comparator changes its value.**
  DG-acquired tape biases beat TS-acquired ones about threefold on DG, with useful
  contributions from both decoder and library; the reverse preference remained
  unresolved. Under common restarts, plain tree GP then used about a fifth fewer
  DG evaluations than A8, although A8 used fewer worker seconds. The original
  weak-prior gain is not a competitive-method claim
  ([42](../2026-10-10-2001/decision.md), [44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md)).
- **Tree libraries transfer useful content, with two distinct limits.** They
  reduced evaluations to about 0.39 of R-tree on DG and 0.25 on one fresh
  gate-sum shape. Rearranging donors lost a 5.1× advantage where a learned
  predicate matched, but retained B/C performance within the tested margin.
  Flattening labels then lost much of that B/C benefit. Thus arrangement matters
  in one setting; its necessity elsewhere and the sufficient label ingredients
  are not established ([45](../2026-10-11-0150/analysis.md),
  [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md), [47](../2026-10-11-0524/decision.md),
  [48](../2026-10-11-0652/decision.md)).
- **The latest result removes the average acquisition case on B/C.** TS sources
  made acquisition about twelve times cheaper than DG sources, but a fixed
  sum-donor prior subsequently beat those TS libraries: hand/TS cost
  **0.852 [0.762, 0.958]**, hand/R-tree **0.318 [0.291, 0.349]**. TS acquisition
  did not repay against the prior at any tested horizon through 200 searches.
  This is a development-bank substitution result, with several content features
  changed together. It neither identifies pair coverage as the cause nor erases
  predicate-matched split A or tape-context evidence. Libraries also harmed easy
  native TS targets, so default library use is not a demonstrated general policy
  ([49](../2026-10-11-0833/decision.md), [51](../2026-10-11-1019/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inheritance remains unshown.** Root 23's inherited frequency vectors
  did not improve frozen fresh search over uniform on their training tasks;
  the scaffold was much cheaper. Persistent ancestry helped relative to shuffling
  on max without establishing usefulness over uniform. This limits one procedure,
  not self-adaptation generally ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

**Current line against different mechanisms.** I searched for the papers below
in this review; they are precedents, not evidence for effects in this repository.

| Candidate | Mechanism, value and choice |
|---|---|
| Literal library acquisition on new content | External extraction transfers expanded subtrees from source solvers. Closest precedent: [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf). **Pursue a bounded first stage**, 4–6 h now, 10–16 h provisional for the complete answer. Existing arrangement-sensitive reuse makes acquisition of larger order helpers plausible; source yield and value against a strong prior are unknown. |
| Supplied structural priors | No acquisition: enumerate useful expanded helpers. **Retain as comparators**, including the successful fixed sum prior and one transparent order-helper prior. The latter knows the proposed domain; learning must be compared with that knowledge, not merely with an obsolete prior. No further B/C prior variants. |
| Learned abstractions and a search policy | [Ellis et al., *DreamCoder* (PLDI 2021)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf) derives reusable abstractions and trains a policy to guide synthesis. Parameterized reuse could generalize beyond literal input bindings; this is a different representation/acquisition mechanism. **Defer**: the current B/C advantage is already supplied for free, and a new abstraction layer needs a destination where it changes a decision. A narrow prototype plus validation and transfer would provisionally consume 12–18 h; this is unmeasured, not a price for reproducing DreamCoder. Reconsider if the new-content study exposes a specific literal-reuse limit with affordable source information. |
| Inherited variation parameters | [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/) encodes variation probabilities whose effects on descendants influence propagation. This differs from external extraction or fitting. **Defer a redesigned root-23 block**: the old 10–14 h allowance does not price a new selection rule, and the latest library result supplies no new inheritance signal. |

The alternative mechanisms are scientifically relevant, but neither currently
justifies its full implementation and confirmation cost ahead of the bounded
content test. Another statistical generator likewise needs a measured benefit
beyond the supplied priors before it earns a build.
