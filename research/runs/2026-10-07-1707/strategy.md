Use root 10's remaining experiment for a different learning signal: **can context fitted
from independently evolved training solutions improve fresh search beyond token fitting?**
The [concept plan](../../plans/solver-corpus-context.md) supplies a bounded route. This is
an external-fitting diagnostic of transferable assembly information, not another test of
map evolution. It deserves a feasibility probe; do not end the autonomous run now.

**What the program has learned.** The [core question](../../../README.md#core-question)
asks how maps bias discovery and whether that bias can evolve toward a task family. The
[digest](../../digest.md), question tree, [run ledger](../../briefs/2026-10-05-2225-ledger.md)
and recent analyses support these working conclusions:

- Frequency matters but does not determine evolutionary discovery. Folding's fixed-target
  advantage was consistent with solver supply; TAG lexicase sometimes beat the corresponding
  random-search expectation. Cheap joins enabled composition. Mixing between lineages
  caused the seeded shared-helper establishment barrier, while self-mating retained useful
  discovery. Natural B-helper arrival and single-copy establishment remain unresolved.
- Adaptation can produce useful transferable token biases. Threshold fits sped held-out
  search about 4× over uniform, with a hand-set scaffold performing comparably. Root 10's
  selected token multipliers on supplied G/G4 context sped withheld compositions about
  2–3× after program populations restarted. This is evidence for outer-loop map adaptation;
  it is not simultaneous map/program coevolution or learned assembly structure.
- Family specificity remains the important gap. [Crossed token transfer](../2026-10-06-2229/analysis.md)
  gave matched/mismatched ratios 1.02× on branch-else (BE) and 0.96× on post-addition (PA).
  Modest preferences remain possible; BE's upper bound rises to about 1.3× when individual
  maps are omitted. Only one BE holdout exists. Hand-set context expresses a crossed
  preference, but only the BE grammar demonstrably improves its own family over G4.
- Both starting programs and ongoing decoder use carry the learned gain, each adding about
  1.3× given the other, with strong sub-additivity on [training cells](../2026-10-07-0315/analysis.md)
  and the reused holdouts. This does not isolate mutation, crossover or partial-program supply.
  The BE/PA balance difference remains confounded with shape and difficulty.
- The [latest study](../2026-10-07-1137/analysis.md) improved token continuation about
  1.12–1.14× on two fresh seed blocks. In that functioning loop, mixed rank-one context
  gave C/T 0.967× [0.871, 1.073]. This bounds that procedure, not all contextual learning:
  context displaced half the token proposals; joint learning from G4 remains untested.
  Four contextual procedures have now failed to establish an additional benefit. The
  changed scoring effort, depth and total effort do not isolate why the latest loop learned.
