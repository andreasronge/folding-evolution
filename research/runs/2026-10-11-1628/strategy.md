**What the program has learned.**

- **Maps affect both arrival and retention.** Random-genotype frequency explains
  easy behaviours better than hard evolutionary discoveries. The tested joining
  advantage came from cheap joins, while lineage mixing impeded establishment
  of rare shared forms. These are scoped operator/map results, not general
  superiority of developmental encoding
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's solutions teach useful biases through external fitting.**
  Contextual fits beat token-only fits, including about 2.1× on a fresh shape;
  pooled/positional frequency replacements and random mutation widening did not
  reproduce that gain. Fragments and cheap feedback acquisition added reuse.
  DG-acquired bias beat TS-acquired bias on DG by about threefold; the reverse
  preference remained unresolved. Both table and library carried useful
  information. Outer selection learned token biases, but its tested contextual
  extensions added no resolved increment
  ([10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- **Strong comparators narrow the practical claim.** Restarted tree GP used
  about a fifth fewer DG evaluations than A8, although A8 was cheaper in worker
  seconds. Tree libraries then cut costs to 0.39× on DG and 0.25× on a fresh
  gate-sum shape. Learned arrangement mattered where the learned predicate fit;
  on other splits a supplied sum prior subsequently beat acquired libraries.
  Libraries also harmed easy native TS targets
  ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md),
  [46–47](../2026-10-11-0524/decision.md),
  [49](../2026-10-11-0833/decision.md),
  [51](../2026-10-11-1019/decision.md)).
- **Uncapped semantic extraction has a credible order-family success.** The
  frozen libraries cost **0.37 [0.33, 0.42]** of R-tree on the 24 held-out order
  cells, solved 1,152/1,152 jobs and repaid acquisition after about four searches.
  Development cost was 0.40 [0.32, 0.50]. These are held-out combinations of a
  development bank, not fresh-family transfer. Supplied order helpers remain
  **6.9× cheaper**. The coverage gradient recurred, but even the stratum with no
  destination-needed helper cost 0.74 [0.57, 0.93] of R-tree. Coverage is not a
  randomized intervention ([1530](../2026-10-11-1530/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inherited learning remains unshown.** Root 23's tested frequency
  learner produced no useful frozen gain over uniform. Root 55's probe observed
  more fixation of the useful-library selector with linkage, but its frozen
  readout versus the starting law was **0.91 [0.71, 1.20]**, unresolved. Use the
  corrected record: **290/336**, not 328/336, trajectories absorbed in episode 0.
  Absorption alone does not establish drift as its cause. These remain probe
  observations, not a confirmed useful inheritance result
  ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md),
  [55](../../questions/55-inherited-library-choice/question.md)).
