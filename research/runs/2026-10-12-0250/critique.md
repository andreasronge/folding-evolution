The design can test useful family-dependent selection among supplied repertoires at a plausible cost; the plan must distinguish uncertain headroom and control results from mechanistic failure or success.

1. **note — Feasible, with measured limits.** The headroom rows contain 862/864 solves: 142/144 for gate donors on order, 144/144 in each other arm/family. The proposed grid is 1,152 exposure trajectories plus 36,288 readouts (12,096 order, 24,192 gate, including the reduced pure-law references). About 64k worker-seconds at ten effective workers supports roughly 107 minutes, close to the stated 100 minutes and inside 210 minutes of timeouts. Exposure's 14 s/trajectory is borrowed from 1736, not measured for this two-library path. In plan.md retain the sustained timing and validation gate, report exposure episode-solve rates separately from restarted readout solves, and price the slower crossed endpoints. The preparation ceiling and deadline remain real constraints; do not omit controls to fit them.

2. **note — Worth this run; correctly bounded contribution.** This asks something 1736 did not: whether ancestry selects *which* available repertoire suits exposure, with usefulness retained after discarding programs. Root 55's content-choice reopening condition is met by the funded two-library design. The closest technique is correctly identified as self-adaptive operator choice, especially [Serpell and Smith](https://uwe-repository.worktribe.com/output/975827); [Stephens et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/adap-org/9708002) supplies the broader indirect-selection framing. The contribution is the acquired-repertoire application and reciprocal frozen readout, not a new self-adaptation principle or invention of content. A fixed-pure/mixture comparison would be cheaper but answers only whether the options differ; intact-versus-scrambled choice would mostly revisit choosing useful over degraded content. Neither is a more decisive substitute for this question. All scored cells remain development data.

3. **note — Headroom uncertainty is not saturation.** The pure-own/M probe intervals, order [0.51, 1.18] and gate [0.47, 1.05], leave substantial uncertainty despite favorable points. An own-pure/M upper bound ≥ 0.90 means adequate headroom was *not established*, not that it is absent. Report “headroom unresolved” unless the interval actually excludes the relevant saving. Also report headroom per family: a favorable pooled bound can hide an uninformative direction. Pure endpoints do not bound the performance of every interior mixture, so failure of this guard cannot override a directly resolved L/M saving. These are interpretation fixes using already planned rows.

4. **note — Preserve the control distinction.** The primary rule can declare reciprocal usefulness even if S and N match L. That establishes useful exposure-dependent endpoint laws, but does not by itself establish that persistent program–bit linkage caused the improvement. In plan.md state how L versus S/N dynamics and frozen costs qualify that claim; retain an “improvement, linkage contribution unresolved” interpretation when appropriate. No extra arms or multiplicity machinery are needed. Fixed attempted library probability also does not fix accepted insertion rates, size or helper mass: the intervention selects whole repertoires, not isolated semantics.

5. **note — Make the outcome rules unambiguous.** An interval such as [0.82, 0.88] satisfies both “useful” and “no worthwhile saving.” Specify precedence and rename the latter “20% pooled saving excluded”; it does not exclude a useful 10–18% saving or a useful single direction. Explicitly cover pooled improvement with neither directional conjunction resolved. The ±8–9% precision forecast is plausible but optimistic: the families share library pairs, and their endpoint distributions and mixture response differ from 1736. Preserve paired population/reference resampling and show a conservative precision scenario in plan.md rather than assuming independent-family averaging buys the full reduction. A broad interval spanning worthwhile saving and little benefit, especially with unresolved directional headroom, would leave the inheritance question largely unanswered. Price that outcome without automatically buying more seeds.
