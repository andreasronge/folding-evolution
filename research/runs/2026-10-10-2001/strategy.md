**What the program has learned.**

- **Supply, discovery and persistence differ.** Root 01 found that random-program
  frequency predicts easy tasks better than hard evolutionary discoveries. Cheap
  joins explained the tested composition advantage; mixing between lineages blocked
  establishment of rare shared forms. Neither result establishes a general advantage
  of developmental encoding. Natural helper arrival remains unresolved
  ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Search experience can teach reusable bias after the source programs are
  discarded.** Outer-selected token weights transferred roughly 2×. The tested
  contextual selection procedures added no resolved increment. External fitting
  succeeded: previous-token context beat token fitting **2.12× [1.86, 2.41]** on
  then-addition when fresh; tested pooled/positional replacements and random recoding
  did not reproduce it. This identifies useful acquired information, without fully
  separating program supply from structured variation ([digest](../../digest.md)).
- **Literal blocks and sparse feedback make a useful complete procedure.**
  Fragments added about 1.5× over context on the older compositions. A8—four source
  attempts per cell, fit, four adaptive attempts, refit—retained useful performance
  on fresh two-sum tasks and when rebuilt from complementary sources. The roughly
  tenfold acquisition saving applies to those original sources, not every family
  ([37](../2026-10-09-2303/decision.md), [38](../2026-10-10-0145/decision.md)).
- **Acquisition now has a resolved family-dependent consequence.** On eight
  previously unsearched double-gate (DG) cells, DG-built A8 cost about a third of
  branch-sum (TS)-built A8: **2.98× [2.07, 4.33]**, or **2.40× [1.76, 3.30]**
  with failures charged once. The reverse preference is **unresolved**, 1.29×
  [0.94, 1.78]; 95–97% solving on TS does not establish equal search costs.
  The aggregate interaction's 1.5× verdict is penalty-sensitive. Both cohorts help
  both families relative to weak G4. DG acquisition costs about twice TS acquisition,
  so DG is not automatically the economical universal choice. These are external
  fits, one family pair and capped costs
  ([1717 analysis](../2026-10-10-1717/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inherited learning remains unshown.** Root 23's tested frequency rule
  was worse than uniform on sum; a gain above about 1.06× was excluded on max.
  Ancestry helped relative to shuffled ancestry on max without establishing useful
  frozen bias. This limits one procedure, not self-adaptation generally
  ([root 23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

**Current line against different mechanisms.** Literature searched for this review:
[Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic incremental program evolution*
(1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) learns a program-generating
distribution from search. A8 is in that external-learning tradition, with its own
context estimator, literal edits and frozen reuse. Locating its family effect is a
mechanism question, not a new claim to distribution learning.

The owner's **inherited strategy parameters** are mechanistically different:
parameters spread through successful descendants rather than a corpus estimator.
[Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems*
(1998; 1997 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/adap-org/9708002) demonstrates indirect
selection of encoded variation probabilities in model landscapes, including changing
environments. Retaining linked program/modifier lineages across goal changes is a
plausible alternative to root 23's episode resets; its possible benefit here is an
inference, not evidence from that paper. The old
[equal-exposure plan](../../plans/heritable-bias-equal-exposure.md) priced a first
usefulness cycle at 5–7 hours. A revised rule plus independent frozen evaluation and
transfer needs a new complete price, provisionally 10–14 hours, and currently lacks
a measured reason to overturn the failed usefulness result. Defer it at this review.

**Learned callable abstractions** offer another route: parameterized pieces can
reuse structure with different arguments, unlike literal token windows.
[Ellis et al., *DreamCoder* (2020 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381)
combines learned symbolic abstractions with learned search guidance. That motivates
a candidate here, not an expected result. New extraction, binding and execution
semantics have no demonstrated necessity yet. Reconsider when literal reuse has an
identified binding limitation; the component test can first establish whether the
library is even the important family-dependent object.
