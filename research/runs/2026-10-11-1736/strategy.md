**What the program has learned.**

- **Maps affect arrival and retention, with strong dependence on operators.**
  Random-program frequency predicts easy behaviours better than hard discoveries.
  Cheap joins explained the tested composition advantage; mixing between
  lineages obstructed rare shared-form establishment. The old folding results
  do not establish general superiority over direct encoding or random restarts
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's solutions teach reusable bias through external fitting.**
  Solver-fitted context beat token fitting, including **2.12× [1.86, 2.41]**
  on a fresh then-addition shape. Frequency projections and random mutation
  widening did not reproduce it. Fragments and cheap feedback acquisition added
  reuse. DG-acquired bias was about threefold cheaper than TS-acquired bias on
  DG; the reverse preference remained unresolved. Both tables and libraries
  carried information. Outer selection learned token biases, while tested
  contextual extensions gave no resolved increment
  ([10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- **Useful learning is not yet a practical advantage over strong supplied
  alternatives.** Matched restarts let tree GP use about a fifth fewer DG
  evaluations than A8, although A8 stayed cheaper in worker seconds. Tree
  libraries then reduced evaluations to 0.39× on DG and 0.25× on one fresh
  gate-sum shape. Supplied sum donors subsequently beat acquired TS libraries
  on B/C; libraries also raised cost on easy native TS tasks. Acquisition and
  deployment policy therefore matter, not just ratios against G4
  ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md),
  [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md),
  [49](../2026-10-11-0833/analysis.md),
  [51](../2026-10-11-1019/analysis.md)).
- **Order libraries now have both held-out and intervention evidence.** Frozen
  R-all cost **0.37 [0.33, 0.42]** of ordinary tree GP on held-out combinations
  and repaid acquisition after about four searches; supplied order helpers
  remained **6.9× cheaper**. Rearranging only helper entries raised development
  cost **2.35× [2.07, 2.66]**, leaving R-hx at 0.90 of R-tree versus intact
  R-all at 0.38. Intact computations beat mostly projection-valued replacements
  of the same material. This does not establish superiority over other
  nontrivial computations, per-helper attribution, or fresh-bank transfer
  ([1530](../2026-10-11-1530/analysis.md),
  [1628](../2026-10-11-1628/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inherited learning remains unshown.** Root 23's tested token learner
  gave no useful frozen gain over uniform. Root 55's probe observed a small
  carrier advantage, but rapid absorption and an unresolved frozen comparison
  with the starting law: **0.91 [0.71, 1.20]**. The corrected first-episode
  absorption count is **290/336**, not 328/336; absorption does not identify
  drift as its sole cause. Its readout averaged four populations' frequencies
  before search, so it did not test deployment of one acquired population's law
  ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md),
  [55](../../questions/55-inherited-library-choice/question.md)).
