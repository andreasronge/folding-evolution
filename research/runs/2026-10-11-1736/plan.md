Implementation plan for the approved [proposal](proposal.md), with [critic notes](critique.md) incorporated. This implements an already approved design, rather than registering or redesigning a new experiment. No exposure or scoring has run for this task before this plan.

Conditions: all 24 immutable 1228 R-all libraries, six original x4-order-v1 development cells, L/S/N with six independent populations per library (432 total). P256, lexicase, crossover 0.5, two aligned elites, 16 random initial carriers. Every nonelite birth inherits its recipient bit, then redraws with probability 1/32 from Bernoulli(1/16); the resulting bit controls donors and remains with rejected edits. S shuffles before selection, N uses ordinary donors in both states. Twelve episodes of 64 evaluated populations (63 transitions each); trees reset independently, bits persist. Fixed scheduled exposure continues after first exact solve. No efficacy pilot, rate sweep, endpoint selection, protected scoring, or frequency pooling.

Each endpoint is frozen separately at q=final_f/2 and scored on six cells and two new seeds per cell, eight attempts capped at 65,536 evaluations each. Per library/cell, population repeats 0..5 use distinct two-seed blocks; all arms and q0=1/32 and R-all=0.5 share that population's block. References are stored once: 5,184 endpoint jobs plus 3,456 references, 8,640 total. Population cost is the arithmetic mean of its 12 capped jobs; arm cost averages six populations within library, then 24 libraries. Failed jobs charge 524,288 evaluations.

Seeds: SHA256-derived, task namespace `2026-10-11-1736`, separate `assignment`, `program`, `modifier`, `readout`, and `bootstrap` domains, with build/population/episode or cell/restart identities. Assignment/program/modifier streams are paired across L/S/N; scoring streams across all five laws within each endpoint block. Smoke uses a disjoint `smoke-` namespace. Audit against historical saved schedules, including 1427; freeze and log all seed lists, method hashes, library hashes, and schedules. Bootstrap uses 8,192 draws and paired library resampling with six populations nested per sampled library; the same population indices select endpoint and reference blocks across arms. Shared references are never separate independent observations.

Before admission, saved 1427 readout rows will calibrate a labelled synthetic sensitivity calculation (not new efficacy evidence). Expand the saved paired library/cell rows to the approved 6-by-2 blocks by paired empirical resampling; center the comparison at 0.95, compare its interval with 0.80, and vary independent endpoint heterogeneity explicitly. Report library-only and nested paired precision; do not claim the independent-job CV approximation as clustered precision. If a substantive precision obstacle prevents the approved design working, stop with measured infeasibility rather than enlarge it. No extra inferential gate is introduced.

Measured anchors: 1427 exposure completed 336 trajectories in 480 s on ten workers; readout completed 1,440 jobs in 533 s. Solves were ordinary 287/288, initial 288/288, L-final 287/288, S-final 286/288, R-all 288/288. Exposure solve shares were N 32% to L 54%. The new q0 success rate and recurrent low-frequency episode solve share are unknown. About 11 min exposure plus 57 min readout is a forecast; fitted low-dose 4.3 s/job is not an observation. Queue reserves 30 and 120 min (150 min summed timeouts, within the three-hour strategy limit). Preparation is bounded to 120 min. Small smoke checks correctness and sustained timing only; it does not tune efficacy.

Measurements will be emitted by the exposure/readout runner and its report: census and selected-parent frequencies; inherited/pre-mutation and post-mutation child frequencies; realized off/on transitions by recipient state; elite and rejected-edit alignment; distinct selected recipients (ancestry proxy, not effective population size); first-hit and scheduled evaluation counts; polymorphic, zero-carrier and pre-solve low-f generation counts; donor insertion/rejection by child state and solve phase; final bits/frequencies; endpoint-level capped costs, solve counts, worker seconds and cell heterogeneity; paired ratios with nested intervals; and acquisition-plus-search evaluations/worker time (one historical acquisition, extraction and one modifier trajectory per endpoint). Frequency and cost plots accompany the report. Tests will verify frozen-law limits, zero-mutation replay of committed 1427, neutral program-stream independence, neutral conditional transition rates 1/512 and 15/512, mutation/elite/revert alignment, equal exposure and first-hit preservation, seed/grid completeness and fail-closed artifact validation.

Headroom q0/R-all <1.25 is a limitation making this candidate uninformative about inheritance, not a general failure or license to change q0. Allele movement or beating only a degraded S is insufficient. Retain supplied R-all contemporaneously and the historically much cheaper supplied R-order alternative visibly. The extrapolated coefficient and distinct-parent proxy do not establish how strong a future learner must be; any negative bound concerns only this fixed procedure. This is a development-bank self-adaptation mechanism/boundary test with external content, not new library learning or transfer. Kim et al. is online reward-based operator-rate adaptation, not a separate outer evolutionary search.

Preparation precision check (before full queue admission): [script](precision_check.py), [output](precision_check.json), using 1,440 saved 1427 rows and fixed precision seed 5043370131188619381 / bootstrap seed 5558062548157006309. No new searches. The initial(.125)/ordinary(0) paired rows supply a *different-dose* noise anchor; centered at 0.95, their full empirical response spread gives nested interval [0.750, 1.164] even without extra endpoint variance. For the closer new low-dose laws, 0.25/0.5 of that response spread are explicitly assumed sensitivity cases, not measurements. At half spread and endpoint multiplier CV=0.5 the interval is [0.809, 1.102]; at full spread and CV=0.5 it is [0.725, 1.223]. CV=1 leaves 0.80 unresolved even at quarter spread. Thus the design *can* separate 0.95 from 0.80 under moderate assumed heterogeneity, but ±10% is not established and high variance can prevent the forecast stopping decision. The saved two-repeat data cannot identify new low-dose endpoint variance or establish an unavoidable precision infeasibility. Consistent with the critic's “no extra statistical gates,” proceed at the approved fixed size, preserving unresolved interpretation for broad intervals; no optimistic sensitivity case is treated as measured precision, and no size/rate change is made.
