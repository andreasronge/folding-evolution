I searched the literature for the mechanism comparison. The current line resembles
[Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic incremental program evolution*
(1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/): search informs an external update
of a program-generating distribution. Our batched fits, literal block edits and frozen
reuse differ from PIPE; neither procedure is evidence of inherited decoder evolution.

The owner's alternative puts credit through descendants. [Stephens et al.,
*Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/)
studies encoded variation probabilities whose consequences for reproduction permit
adaptation without a direct fitness reward for the parameter. This is mechanistically
different from fitting counts to solver tapes. Its relevance is high, but root 23
provides no positive frozen-usefulness signal for a revised experiment yet. A new noise
scale alone would spend the remaining window without a supported reason to expect
different acquisition.

A second distinct candidate is learning callable abstractions. [Ellis et al.,
*DreamCoder* (2020 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) grows symbolic
abstractions alongside a learned search guide. Unlike our fixed literal windows, this
changes the language in which future programs are composed. My inference is that this
could address reuse beyond recurring surface syntax, but our current results do not
demonstrate that need. Building and validating that machinery plus an informative
comparison has no credible complete price inside nine hours. Reconsider after a
transfer boundary shows what the present repertoire fails to express or reuse.
