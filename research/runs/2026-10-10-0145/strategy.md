**Current line versus different mechanisms.** I searched the literature for this
review. [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic incremental program evolution*
(1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) updates a program-generating
distribution using successful search. A8 is related external fitting, with batched
corpora, literal block edits and frozen reuse; it is not PIPE's algorithm or inherited
decoder evolution.

The owner's alternative assigns credit through descendants. [Stephens et al.,
*Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/)
studies genetically encoded mutation/crossover probabilities whose effects on future
propagation permit adaptation without a direct fitness reward for the parameter.
That differs mechanistically from estimating transition counts from solver tapes.
Root 23 gives no positive frozen-usefulness signal supporting another unchanged noise
or exposure trial. A new rule needs a specific reason to change that outcome.

[Ellis et al., *DreamCoder* (2020 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381)
offers another distinct candidate: acquiring symbolic abstractions together with a
search guide. Callable abstractions change the language available for composition;
our fragments remain literal windows in a fixed language. My inference is that this
could address reuse beyond shared surface syntax. The current results have not exposed
that need, and its complete build, validation and comparison lack a credible price
inside six hours. Keep it as a future representation candidate, not a reason to prolong
the present fragment-detail series.
