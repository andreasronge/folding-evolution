The identified roster plumbing is bounded, and exact old-default replay plus replacement-membership/fallback checks are the right safeguards.

2. **note — Worth running, with an appropriately narrow contribution.** This tests a consequential missing premise: whether to carry an acquisition recipe beyond the source cells on which it was developed, rather than carry only successful saved artifacts. It offers more information than another A8/full-F precision round or A12 on unchanged sources. A structurally different target family would test broader transfer, but needs a valid new bank and changes the target as well as potentially the sources; it is not a clearly cheaper or cleaner answer to this source question. Both rosters are correctly labelled development data. A successful result supports this complementary roster only, not arbitrary source-set robustness, a fresh-bank claim or inherited evolution.

3. **note — Prior-work framing is adequate.** [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber's PIPE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) is an appropriate model-building GP antecedent; [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico's Run Transferable Libraries](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf) covers reuse of learned program material. Here the decoder is externally fitted and the library supplies literal block edits. The contribution is a costed source-set replication of this particular frozen procedure, not a new class of learning algorithm. No additional novelty claim or literature-driven arm is needed.

4. **note — Preserve the actual uncertainty units.** Historical A8's 64 builds were averaged into 16 four-block corpus means; A8′ has 16 individual builds. The cited 0.27 log-SD is also the historical A8/full-F *contrast*, not a measured SD for A8′ versus G4. In plan.md give a simple primary-interval precision scenario including the shared G4 uncertainty and plausible single-build spread. For δ, map all four historical block-specific artifacts and evaluation keys explicitly to each new build's four scoring seeds. Pairing controls target-search randomness, not source ancestry. Call δ a historical roster comparison, and do not infer equal per-build reliability from a pooled near-one result. The primary shared-baseline bootstrap is otherwise sensible.

5. **note — Separate usefulness, attribution and economics.** UB(u) < 1.5 means this new acquisition misses the chosen usefulness bar; δ supplies the direct evidence of deterioration relative to historical A8. Likewise, poor A8′ with useful S8′ implicates the adaptive policy at this scope, but cannot identify yield versus content or decoder versus library. A 1.5× geometric penalized-cost gain does **not** imply repayment within 100 searches: repayment uses arithmetic capped costs and newly measured acquisition. Keep the margin as a practical retained-gain threshold and report the independently computed break-even, with uncertainty, rather than treating payback as guaranteed by u.

6. **note — The cheaper fallback is already present; define the uninformative outcome.** A8′ alone halves scoring and retains the primary answer, so dropping S8′ on timing grounds is reasonable; it forfeits the policy-versus-source diagnostic. Do not reduce builds to preserve that secondary arm. An interval spanning 1.5, accompanied by broad δ and S8′/A8′ intervals, leaves the carry-the-recipe decision unresolved. So does a timing stop without confirmation. Neither establishes source dependence or equality; report the resolution price and return to strategy as proposed.
