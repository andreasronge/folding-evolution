**A mechanistically different candidate.** Literature searched for this review places
23 near self-adaptation: [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/)
encode mutation/crossover probabilities and obtain indirect selection on them through
reproduction. Here the parameters instead govern token generation, and their value is tested
after freezing them and discarding their carriers. The paper motivates the mechanism, not
transfer in this repository.

The alternative is distribution learning from successful programs. [Salustowicz and
Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic incremental program evolution* (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/)
generate programs from an adaptive distribution and refine it using the best program.
Root 10's corpus fitting uses a different estimator and representation, but shares this
direct statistical learning signal. It can acquire useful context without maintaining a
modifier–program association. My inference is that this is the stronger alternative for
testing broader transfer, whereas 23 is the sharper immediate test of acquisition by
inheritance. Success of either would not establish the other's mechanism.
