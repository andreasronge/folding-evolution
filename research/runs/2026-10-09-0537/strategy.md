**Current line against different mechanisms.** Literature searched for this review:
[Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, PIPE (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/)
learns a program-generating distribution. The corpus line similarly fits probabilities
over fixed primitives. More feedback would elaborate this already productive approach
without resolving whether its generative probabilities or induced variation matter most.

[Ellis et al., *DreamCoder* (2020 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381)
learns symbolic abstractions and a search guide. The repository's
[fragment candidate](../../plans/learned-executable-fragments.md) changes the units of
variation by extracting reusable executable blocks. My hypothesis is that coordinated
edits could carry dependencies which a previous-token model misses. This is different
from fitting probabilities over existing tokens; the paper supplies no evidence that
it will beat C here. An inspection of 1246's 1,808 saved training solver tapes found
recurring five-token reducer-pair blocks across many corpora. That establishes material
to inspect, not active reuse: inert occurrences and generic syntax remain explanations.
Root 01's cheap-join result makes matched edit-size and primitive-work controls essential.

A cheaper competing mechanism is **variation coupling without changing program
probabilities**. Context-dependent permutations of allele labels preserve every lookup
row's token counts, hence every complete tape's probability under uniform alleles, but
change how edits propagate. [Stephens, *Effect of Mutation and Recombination on the
Genotype-Phenotype Map* (1999; preprint 2000)](https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0006051)
provides related work on operator effects among equivalent genotypes. The proposed
recoding is our intervention, not a result of that paper. It changes mutation and
potentially crossover neighbourhoods; it does not isolate edit size alone.

The owner's route changes the credit mechanism instead: descendants inherit variation
parameters. [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/)
studies indirect selection of encoded mutation/crossover probabilities. Root 23 limits
one token-frequency instance, not that broader mechanism.

**Next question for the steward.** Can Q approach C by changing its variation
neighbourhood while preserving its complete random-program distribution? This tests
whether the next learner should target how edits propagate before paying for a new
library of executable units. The plan specifies a bounded recoding and exact invariants;
the steward supplies the experimental design. The primary evidence must be recoded-Q
versus Q, with identical starting programs, and C remains the practical benchmark.
Merely matching C's mean edit size is not an answer.

If recoding helps and approaches C, prioritize a measured way to acquire useful variation
coupling. If it helps but leaves a large gap, retain both contributions as live candidates.
If it is bounded small or harmful, end this recoding attempt and reconsider fragments
at their full cost; failure would not prove learned conditional probabilities necessary.
Broad intervals need a resolution price. No outcome automatically earns another control.
