1. **note — Feasibility is plausible, but project runtime by arm.** The probes give G about 112 solves per 120 training searches at 65k, versus about 28 for U, and the prior holdouts gave G 49/50 and 48/50 at 524k. Also use the reviewed holdout KM medians: G 13,568/18,688 and U 83,968/148,480; the proposal's different numbers need a metric/source label.

2. **note — This is the right next contrast, with a bounded negative interpretation.** C versus frozen G tests improvement beyond the supplied prior; C versus M is the essential control for new contextual preferences; T and C-marg address frequency-only alternatives. Reusing the screened PA split is more decisive than another bank screen. I cannot identify an established cheaper optimizer that would answer this question better from the available probes. Nevertheless, those probes mutated whole tables or rows, not the proposed three-coordinate operator. Include that operator in the stage-0 smoke, record how often it actually changes the integer decoder and its paired score variability, and freeze normalization/support enforcement. A failure of sparse C mutations in 552 dimensions would diagnose this procedure, not contextual learning generally. It would leave transfer and family specificity unanswered; the proposal correctly reserves those broader questions.

3. **note — Preserve unresolved results in the outcome rules.** Row 1 currently sends an unresolved training interval to “did not improve,” bypassing row 5. Separate “no practical training gain” from “training gain unresolved”; neither establishes zero improvement. Row 2 should say “no ≥1.5× holdout gain supported within the interval resolution,” rather than treating every such outcome as demonstrated overfitting. Row 4 bounds C's incremental advantage over M; it does not establish equality or that contextual preferences are generally unnecessary. Report M versus G alongside it before calling M's transfer demonstrated. These are interpretation refinements, not reasons to redesign the experiment. Also report the fresh-training score at both 65k and 524k, derivable from stage-2 runs: improvement in the selected objective followed by failure at the larger cap is different from failure to learn that objective.

4. **note — Six trajectories is a reasonable first size, not assured power.** At the assumed between-trajectory SD of 0.3 log2, the proposed scale can distinguish a useful gain; that SD is unmeasured, and success requires both holdouts and multiple contrasts. Broad intervals or mixed holdouts would leave the leading explanation unresolved, making the reserved resolution slot appropriate. No additional multiplicity machinery is needed for this exploratory first run.

Reserve time for stage 2, use the stage-0 timings to fix the reduced schedule before learning, and avoid starting a trajectory that cannot be completed and evaluated.
