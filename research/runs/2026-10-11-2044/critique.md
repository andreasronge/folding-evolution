2. **note — Price the actual protected roster and distinguish precision from decisiveness.** Screening may leave fewer than 18 protected cells; simulate the admitted count, not an unconditional 18. With only six development cells and two repeats, explain how build, cell and search variation enter the forecast, and retain a conservative sensitivity scenario. An interval upper/lower ratio ≤1.6 does not itself ensure a decision at 0.80: an interval centred near that threshold can remain unresolved at that precision. Show expected intervals at several effects around 0.80 and identify the unresolved case explicitly. This is a planning forecast, not a gate establishing an effect or an automatic authorization to confirm.

3. **note — Correct scope and adequate probe design.** All four searched cells stay development-only; semantic/VM checks, short-alias screening, fresh seeds and freezing precede further search. Reusing all 24 acquisitions is appropriate for estimating build variability, and R-gate measures available headroom. Halving repeats would save only about five minutes of expected queue time while weakening the pricing exercise; no clearly cheaper design delivering the same measurements is apparent. As a `kind: probe`, this is approved on cost and correctness, without requiring a superiority result or a confirmatory outcome table. An unresolved R-all ratio still informs cost and variance. It leaves the substantive reuse question unanswered if precision remains poor, the bank is unexpectedly easy, or all arms largely cap; report that and return to strategy without a belief update or a second shape.

4. **note — The closest technique is correctly named.** [Keijzer, Ryan & Cattolico, Run Transferable Libraries (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf) already includes training on simpler problems to aid harder ones. The contribution here is a boundary test of this frozen, externally extracted donor recipe, with partial helper coverage and a fully supplied comparator; it is not a new transfer mechanism. Three linked terms change both composition length and input-role sharing, so any later result concerns this combined shape change. R-gate success with R-all failure would not by itself isolate missing helpers from other library-content or donor-distribution effects.
