**What we have learned.**

- Maps affect arrival and persistence, but no general folding advantage follows.
  Random frequency predicts easy behaviours better than hard evolutionary
  discoveries; cheap joins explained the tested composition advantage, and
  lineage mixing impeded establishment of rare shared forms
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- External fitting can retain useful information after source programs are
  discarded. Solver-fitted context beat token fitting, including **2.12×
  [1.86, 2.41]** on a fresh then-addition shape; marginal matching and random
  widening of variation did not reproduce it. Fragments and small feedback
  acquisitions added value. DG-acquired components showed a useful family
  preference in one direction. Outer-selected token weights helped, but the
  contextual selection procedures tested produced no resolved increment
  ([10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- Strong controls changed the practical conclusion. Restarted tree GP used
  about a fifth fewer DG evaluations than A8, though A8 used fewer worker
  seconds. Tree libraries then cut cost 2.5–4×, including a fresh composition;
  supplied sum donors beat acquired TS libraries, which harmed near-solved TS
  targets. Acquisition is neither automatically necessary nor harmless
  ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md), [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md),
  [49](../2026-10-11-0833/analysis.md), [51](../2026-10-11-1019/analysis.md)).
- Uncapped semantic libraries helped order holdouts: **0.37 [0.33, 0.42]** of
  R-tree cost. Rearranging only helper entries raised development cost **2.35×
  [2.07, 2.66]**, relative to intact entries; most replacements were trivial.
  Supplied order helpers remained 6.9× cheaper. The rectifier probe instead
  exposed a source-content problem: intended helpers seldom appeared and none
  entered its eight libraries. That is an observation about one source/recipe,
  not a general negative finding
  ([1530](../2026-10-11-1530/analysis.md), [1628](../2026-10-11-1628/analysis.md),
  [54](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/54-rectified-sum-acquisition/log.md)).
- **The newest result extends the unchanged acquisition recipe to fresh
  content.** Signed-gate R-all cost **0.577 [0.47, 0.72]** of R-tree on eighteen
  previously unsearched cells; transported order libraries showed no saving
  (**1.12 [0.94, 1.34]**, harm unresolved). All arms solved 1,728/1,728 jobs.
  But R-tree solved 97.7% on its first attempt, acquisition repaid only after
  about 66 searches, and supplied gates were another 2.3× cheaper. This is one
  construction's rebindings, not broad task-family transfer or helper causality
  ([1938](../2026-10-11-1938/analysis.md)).
- Inheritance has a narrow positive signal. Linked selection with recurrent
  mutation raised a supplied-library bit to 0.29 versus 0.06/0.05 controls.
  Frozen deployment averaged **0.805 [0.704, 0.913]** of starting cost, below
  one but unresolved at the pre-set 10% usefulness bar. Arm-average costs were
  consistent with endpoint doses; fixed R-all was 1.71× cheaper. This selects
  access to supplied content. Useful inherited token learning, content
  construction and transfer remain unshown
  ([55](../../questions/55-inherited-library-choice/question.md),
  [23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

for this review. The linked papers motivate alternatives, not predicted effects
here. Costs below include implementation, validation, agent review and scoring.

| Candidate | Decision, full cost and reconsideration |
|---|---|
| Frozen external library reuse at greater assembly demand | **Pursue, 8–11 h including conditional confirmation.** Closest to [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf): transferring acquired functional bias across runs. Saved acquisitions allow a bounded boundary test without changing the learner. |
| Inherited choice between useful libraries at equal donor rate | **Defer, provisionally 8–12 h** for calibration, changed exposure/readout and a substantive comparison. Unlike external fitting, program–modifier ancestry supplies the selection signal. Prefer order versus signed-gate content to intact versus mostly trivial scrambles; reciprocal preference and sufficient selection headroom are not yet measured. Reconsider when content choice decides a family-learning route and a complete crossed comparison is priced. This still selects supplied content. |
| Parameterized library learning | **Defer, provisionally 12–18 h with integration uncertainty.** [Bowers et al., *Top-Down Synthesis for Library Learning* (POPL 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16605) synthesizes reusable abstractions from corpus structure. This could pool input bindings rather than keep literal donors, but compression is not demonstrated search usefulness and missing source computations are not automatically recovered. Reconsider a binding/representation bottleneck plus a priced instantiation-and-transfer plan; the current cap-free recipe has just generalized. |
| Signed-gate helper rearrangement or a 55 precision top-up | **Decline this block, roughly 3–5 h or 4 h.** The former refines an explanation without choosing a new direction; the latter cannot turn a supplied dose into learned content. Reconsider only when that distinction changes an acquisition or inheritance decision. |
