Keep the experiment and its size, but make the outcome rules mutually exclusive and stop treating an unresolved component benefit as evidence that only the combination works.

The four-arm factorial is the right bounded next test under the [strategy](../2026-10-05-1759/strategy.md): fresh U/X replication plus IG/R identifies which vector intervention suffices, while retaining both tasks checks whether the answer transfers between them. It cannot separate partial-scaffold supply, junk suppression and shortcut ancestry, and the revised proposal largely respects that limit. No cheaper existing-data analysis replaces the missing interventions; an initialization/mutation split or fifth arm would expand the question unnecessarily. The short sampling addition usefully checks whether IG's success comes with increased exact-solver supply, though it cannot identify the evolutionary mechanism.

Fix these decisions before running:

- **“Carries” and “partial” overlap.** If U÷C is resolved above 1 and C÷X has interval [1.10, 1.40], C satisfies both. It is slower than X but meets the chosen sufficiency margin. Give “carries” precedence. To conclude “neither part alone suffices,” require each C÷X lower bound to exceed 1.5; a lower bound merely above 1 does not establish failure of the 1.5× criterion. A non-carrying arm whose interval straddles 1.5 remains unresolved on sufficiency, even if its improvement over U is resolved.
- **“No gain shown” does not establish “only the combination helps.”** Its predicate permits a useful component effect with an imprecise U÷C interval. Report “X improves; neither component's improvement is resolved; both are slower than X,” and park. Do not add another equivalence study just to justify a stronger conclusion.
- **Both carrying does not establish non-additivity.** Conclude only that either intervention meets the sufficiency criterion. Both can carry within a generous margin while their effects still combine. Keep interaction descriptive as promised. Apply the single-task scope/parking rule to all branches, including both carrying.

**Size and least informative outcome.** The 250-pair design is reasonable for the stated runtime and precision diagnostic; a frozen, old-data-only check allowing at most 350 is acceptable. The reported power concerns an arm exactly matching X, not all useful intermediate effects, and positive pairing is an assumption rather than a guarantee. X replicating while component intervals straddle 1.5 would leave component sufficiency unresolved; extra shortcut logs cannot rescue it. The one-run stop remains appropriate.
