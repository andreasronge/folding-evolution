**What the program has learned.**

- **The map matters, but random-program frequency is an incomplete account.**
  Root 01 predicts easy behaviours better than hard discovery. Cheap joins
  explained the tested compositional advantage; mixing between lineages blocked
  establishment of rare shared forms. These results concern particular maps
  and operators, not general superiority of developmental encoding
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's solutions teach useful transferable biases when fitted from
  outside.** Contextual fits beat token-only fits, including about 2.1× on a
  fresh shape; pooled/positional frequency replacements and random mutation
  widening did not reproduce that advantage. Fragments added speed and cheap
  acquisition retained useful transfer. Outer selection learned token weights,
  but the tested context learners added no resolved increment. DG-acquired tape
  bias was about threefold cheaper than TS-acquired bias on DG, with useful
  table and library contributions; the reverse preference remained unresolved
  ([root 10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- **Stronger baselines substantially narrow the acquisition case.** With matched
  restarts, plain tree GP used about a fifth fewer DG evaluations than A8,
  although A8 remained cheaper in worker seconds. Tree-source libraries then
  cut evaluations to 0.39× on DG and 0.25× on a fresh gate-sum shape. Learned
  arrangement mattered strongly where a predicate matched. On other splits,
  a supplied sum prior subsequently beat cheap learned libraries, whose
  acquisition did not repay against it through 200 searches. Libraries also
  harmed easy native targets ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md),
  [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md), [47](../2026-10-11-0524/decision.md),
  [49](../2026-10-11-0833/decision.md), [51](../2026-10-11-1019/decision.md)).
- **The latest result reopens a useful acquisition path, at development scope.**
  Keeping all recurrent semantic classes reduced order-task evaluations to
  **0.40 [0.32, 0.50]** of R-tree across 24 independent acquisitions and six
  development cells; the capped libraries cost **1.23 [1.07, 1.41]** of R-tree.
  Acquisition repays against R-tree after about five searches. The supplied
  order prior remains **7.3× [5.5, 9.8] cheaper** than R-all. Cap removal changes
  size, content and donor probabilities together; helper coverage is suggestive,
  not causal identification. No protected cells were scored, and the method
  was changed after this bank was seen ([1228](../2026-10-11-1228/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inherited learning remains unshown.** Root 23's frozen inherited
  frequencies did not improve fresh training search over uniform; the supplied
  scaffold was much better. Linkage helped against shuffled ancestry on max
  without establishing usefulness. This limits one inheritance procedure,
  not self-adaptation generally ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

**Current line against different mechanisms.** I searched the literature for
the papers below during this review. They motivate candidates, not predictions
of repository performance.

| Candidate | Value, full cost and choice |
|---|---|
| Frozen semantic extraction on new content | External pooling of observed source subtrees, reused as expanded donors; closest cross-run precedent is [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf). **Pursue**: 4–6 h probe, 10–14 h including conditional confirmation. Tests whether the successful recipe travels without retuning. |
| Parameterized abstraction learning | [Bowers et al., *Top-Down Synthesis for Library Learning* (POPL 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16605) synthesizes abstractions with corpus-guided matching and compression. Parameters could pool across bindings, unlike fixed observed subtrees. **Defer**: a narrow adapter, instantiation policy, validation and acquisition/transfer comparison provisionally costs 12–18 h, still unmeasured. Semantic pooling already recovered useful content without this machinery; compression alone does not establish search speed. Reconsider when a frozen-recipe boundary exposes a representational limit that parameterization specifically addresses. |
| Inherited variation probabilities | [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/) studies encoded variation probabilities selected through descendant effects. This directly addresses the owner's inherited-map aim. **Defer**: the earlier 10–14 h envelope does not price a changed rule with a useful selection signal. Reconsider under root 23's stated conditions. |
| Supplied helpers | No acquisition; the strongest practical reference on the order bank. **Retain as a comparator** for the new content too. Acquiring a fraction of their benefit is scientifically useful but cannot establish a practical advantage over them. |
