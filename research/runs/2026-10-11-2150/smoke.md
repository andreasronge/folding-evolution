The full protected confirmation is queued, not executed. Search smoke used only the first two frozen development cells, builds 0–1, one repeat, all three arms, with the approved full population/cutoff/restart/donor settings. There were zero protected searches. Synthetic completed rows in inference tests do not run evolutionary search on protected targets.

Fresh preparation ([log](smoke-prepare-portable.log), [record](smoke-prepare-portable/preparation.json)) passed in **112.067 s**. All 24 frozen cells retain their original split (6 development / 18 protected), bank digest `e3873e170b71ffcb69bea99b0d2cf4afd7062a5b6ab28ef2a9e59608f6c6d175`, method/library identities and admission evidence. The seed audit compares against **53,263 historical seeds**, releases 13,824 independent attempt seeds for the 1,728 confirmation units (20461000–20474823), and reserves 32 separate smoke attempt seeds (20480000–20480807). Each unit's eight seeds are shared only across its three arms. The old 2044 smoke seeds in the proposed namespace were explicitly avoided.

[Scoring log](smoke-score.log), [raw rows](smoke-score/score.jsonl), [report](smoke-score/report.json), [plot](smoke-score/dynamics.png): **12/12 exact D625 solves**, no caps; scoring **23.355 s**, end-to-end phase **31.178 s**. Four jobs per arm; first-attempt rates were 0/4 R-tree, 2/4 R-all and 2/4 R-gate. Maximum worker time **22.831 s**; pooled mean **7.577 worker-s/job**. These are execution and timing checks, not efficacy estimates or protected validity-rate estimates. The rendered plot was inspected and has complete labelled panels.

Small-batch effective concurrency is only **3.893**: twelve heterogeneous jobs on eight workers end with a single slow job, so its 0.514 jobs/s wall throughput is not a sustained full-grid rate. Using the prior measured full-batch effective concurrency of 7.6 with this smoke's pooled worker cost gives **86.14 min** central / **172.28 min** doubled-tail scoring. Worker cost is about 10% above the proposal's 6.9-s anchor, not a design infeasibility. With 1.87 min preparation, central expected queue time is approximately **88 min**. Outer timeouts are 900 + 11,520 = **12,420 s (207 min)**, within the approved 240-min ceiling. Internal deadlines leave 30 seconds per entry for queue handling. The finite smoke cannot bound unseen protected-cell tails.
