**What the program has learned.**

- **A map's random supply is only part of evolutionary accessibility.** Root 01
  found frequency predictive for easy behaviours, insufficient for hard discoveries.
  Cheap joins explained the tested composition advantage; lineage mixing blocked
  rare shared forms, while self-mating preserved some discovery and removed that
  establishment barrier. These are specific map/operator results, not a general
  superiority of folding or development ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Evolution's own solutions contain useful search information.** Outer selection
  learned transferable token weights, but the tested contextual learners added no
  resolved gain. External fitting did better: solver-fitted context transferred
  beyond token fitting, including about 2× on a fresh shape. Pooled/positional
  frequency replacements and random mutation widening did not reproduce it.
  Literal tape fragments added further speed. Partial-program learning helped
  less, and the tested pre-solve extractor did not recover the fragment increment
  ([digest](../../digest.md)). This establishes useful external acquisition at
  these scopes; it does not establish evolved contextual acquisition.
- **Acquisition and baseline costs matter.** Sparse A8 builds retained useful
  transfer, including after changing source compositions. On independent inputs,
  DG-acquired artifacts were about 3× cheaper than TS-acquired ones on DG; the
  reverse preference remains unresolved. Both table and library carry useful
  information. Yet common restarts made plain tree GP about a fifth cheaper than
  A8 in evaluations, with A8 still cheaper in worker seconds
  ([41](../2026-10-10-1717/analysis.md), [42](../2026-10-10-2001/analysis.md),
  [44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md)). G4 wins alone no longer justify expansion.
- **Learning improves the stronger tree procedure too.** Frozen libraries from
  tree-source solvers reduced evaluation cost to **0.39 [0.33, 0.46]** of restarted
  tree GP on DG and **0.25 [0.22, 0.29]** on one fresh gate-sum shape. Worker time
  also fell. Acquisition repaid after roughly 54–55 searches on those rosters.
  This is one source cohort and transfer to a composition of familiar parts
  ([45](../2026-10-11-0150/analysis.md), [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md)).
- **The library has two distinguishable uses.** Rearranging labels costs about
  **5.1×** where the learned predicate fits. On unmatched B/C splits it retained
  performance at the registered 1.25 margin, though an intact advantage of about
  20% remained possible. Uniform labels on those same shapes then cost
  **2.07× [1.68, 2.61]** of count-preserving rearrangement. They retained only
  about **40% of its arithmetic saving**, and were not resolved from size-matched
  random donors. Thus the label law matters for this replacement; shapes alone
  do not reproduce most of the benefit. ADD, ANY, constants and finite-library
  dependencies changed together, so the responsible component is still open
  ([47](../2026-10-11-0524/analysis.md), [48 decision](../2026-10-11-0652/decision.md)).
- **Useful inheritance remains unshown.** Root 23's inherited vectors were worse
  than uniform on sum; on max a gain above about 1.06× was excluded. Ancestry
  helped relative to shuffling on max without establishing useful acquisition.
  This limits one rule and exposure schedule, not self-adaptation generally
  ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

**Current line versus alternatives.** I searched the literature for this review.
These papers establish precedents, not expected effects in our harness.

| Candidate | Mechanism, value and decision |
|---|---|
| **Literal libraries rebuilt from different sources — pursue** | External extraction retains complete source subtrees as mutation donors. The closest precedent is [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-24855-2_63), which transfers acquired functions across GP runs. Our donors expand into ordinary primitives. A **7–10 h complete comparison** can tell whether the successful acquisition recipe survives a different source family; both useful replication and a bounded failure change its expansion case. |
| **Statistical program generation — defer** | [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *PIPE* (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) updates a program-generating distribution using selected programs. It can propose new combinations rather than retrieve literal expressions. B/C retention makes this plausible, but uniform-label failure shows it must preserve more than learned shapes. A bounded source-fit generator plus independent usefulness/transfer confirmation has a provisional **10–14 h** price, unmeasured; online feedback adds another question. First establish portability of the source signal. Reconsider after this block if it would reduce acquisition cost or retain useful bias where literal reuse fails. |
| **Inherited strategy parameters — defer** | [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/) encodes mutation/crossover probabilities whose consequences affect descendant success. That differs from both external fitting and literal extraction and directly addresses the owner's aim. The old **10–14 h** usefulness/transfer route needs a changed rule and measured signal, not another repetition. Tree-library success supplies neither. |
| Frozen-library unfamiliar-content boundary — defer one block | The existing **5–8 h** allowance could locate where DG reuse stops. It would not establish that the extractor can learn from other sources. Reconsider after the source comparison, ahead of another familiar-parts shape or local ablation; a new bank needs its own plan and semantic price. |

**What the steward should ask next.** *Can the unchanged tree-library acquisition,
rebuilt from branch-sum sources, still make fresh search cheaper than restarted
tree GP on excluded compositions, with a credible acquisition repayment?*
Follow the new [different-source plan](../../plans/tree-library-source-portability.md).
The primary destination is the fixed gate-sum B/C roster; a native TS reading
separates failure to help that destination from failure to help even related TS
tasks. Both are development banks. This changes the source family while retaining
the successful method and a measured destination, rather than changing source,
method and target simultaneously.

TS is a bounded next source, not an unfamiliar instruction set: it still shares
ADD and indexed inputs. A positive can establish acquisition portability between
these two source rosters, possibly through generic label supply. It cannot show
arbitrary-family transfer or that statistical generation is unnecessary. A
source-independent prior may still reproduce the gain. Keep that limitation
visible rather than buying all possible controls in advance.
