**What the program has learned.**

- **Map bias matters, but random supply does not explain all discovery.** Root
  01 found frequency predictive for easy behaviours, insufficient for hard ones.
  Cheap joins explained the tested composition advantage; lineage mixing blocked
  rare shared forms, while self-mating relieved establishment without eliminating
  discovery. This supports specific map/operator effects, not general superiority
  of developmental encoding ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Solutions found by evolution teach useful search biases.** Outer selection
  learned transferable token weights; the tested contextual selection procedures
  added no resolved increment. External solver fitting did: context transferred
  beyond token fitting, including about 2× on a fresh shape, and pooled/positional
  frequency replacements and random mutation widening did not reproduce it.
  Literal fragments added further speed. Partial-program fitting helped less;
  the tested pre-solve fragment extractor did not recover the exact-source
  increment on then-addition ([digest](../../digest.md)). These are external
  acquisition results, not inherited contextual learning.
- **Stronger comparators changed the practical answer.** Cheap A8 acquisition
  transferred, and DG acquisition produced a useful one-directional family
  preference, carried by both table and library. Yet common restarts made plain
  tree GP about a fifth cheaper than A8 in evaluations; A8 remained cheaper in
  worker seconds. Learning into tree search then cut evaluations to
  **0.39 [0.33, 0.46]** of R-tree on DG and **0.25 [0.22, 0.29]** on one fresh
  gate-sum shape built from familiar parts ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md),
  [45](../2026-10-11-0150/analysis.md), [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md)).
- **Specific arrangement and more general donor supply have different roles.**
  Rearrangement cost about 5.1× where the learned predicate matched, but retained
  B/C performance at a 1.25 margin. Uniform labels on those shapes then cost
  **2.07× [1.68, 2.61]** of count-preserving rearrangement. Several label features
  changed together; shape effects and the sufficient content remain unresolved
  ([47](../2026-10-11-0524/decision.md), [48](../2026-10-11-0652/decision.md)).
- **Acquisition is now cheaper, but its incremental necessity is open.** Rebuilding
  from TS sources cut B/C cost to **0.382 [0.335, 0.433]** of R-tree, beyond that
  cohort's size-matched random control. Acquisition fell from about 2.88 M to
  **0.245 M evaluations**, repaying after about **6 B/C searches**. TS versus DG
  cost **1.03 [0.86, 1.22]** is unresolved, not equal. TS libraries contain no
  comparison predicate; their shared small ADD content suggests a supplied prior
  might suffice. That inference is observational. On easy native TS targets,
  library use raised cost **1.78× [1.58, 2.00]**; its cause and a remedy remain
  untested ([49](../2026-10-11-0833/decision.md),
  [50](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/50-library-easy-target-harm/question.md)).
- **Useful inheritance is still missing.** Root 23's tested inherited vectors
  failed frozen usefulness; ancestry helped relative to shuffling on max without
  establishing improvement over uniform. This bounds one rule and exposure
  schedule, not self-adaptation generally
  ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).

**Current line against different mechanisms.** I searched for the following
papers in this review. They supply precedents, not expected effects here.

| Candidate | Mechanism, value and current decision |
|---|---|
| Literal source libraries | External extraction transfers complete subtrees as mutation donors. [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf) is the nearest cross-run library precedent; our donors expand into ordinary primitives. **Retain as the acquired reference**, with TS's measured cheap acquisition. |
| Supplied sum-donor prior | A fixed alphabet-symmetric construction supplies small sums without collecting solvers or fitting parameters. **Pursue, 4–6 h total.** Both source cohorts help B/C despite different entries, making this a consequential alternative to acquisition. It is informed by development results, not task-blind. |
| Statistical program generation | [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *PIPE* (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) updates a program-generating distribution from the best sampled programs. Distribution learning can propose new combinations rather than retrieve a finite literal library. **Defer** the provisional **10–14 h** build/usefulness/transfer route: six-search repayment weakens its acquisition-cost rationale, and the hand prior may remove much of its target benefit. Reconsider after the control if a measured learned increment or content boundary gives it something valuable to learn. |
| Inherited strategy parameters | [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998; 1997 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/adap-org/9708002) studies encoded mutation/crossover probabilities whose consequences affect lineage propagation. This differs from external fitting and literal extraction. **Defer** a new inheritance block: the existing **10–14 h** planning allowance is not a measured complete price for a changed rule. Reconsider with the signal or necessary control named by root 23. |
| Unfamiliar-content boundary | A **5–8 h provisional** complete comparison could locate the frozen library's limits, but needs a new semantic bank plan and measured price. **Defer one block** so it can compare learning with a stronger supplied prior. Do not condition writing that plan on a positive learned advantage here: either outcome can make the boundary worth buying. |

**What the steward should ask next.** *Does a simple source-free sum-donor prior
retain the useful B/C performance of the cheaply acquired TS libraries, and what
acquisition value remains after charging its cost?* Follow the new
[source-free donor plan](../../plans/source-free-tree-donors.md). This answers
whether the next learner must improve on a simple construction, not merely on
uniform or size-matched random donors. It earns its cost because either retention
or a resolved loss changes the acquisition case.

Keep the inference local. Retention would not prove equality, eliminate possible
long-horizon value from a smaller learned advantage, or erase split A's
arrangement-sensitive benefit and the tape-context evidence. Failure would reject
one supplied prior, not establish that learning is necessary. Do not add an
easy-target gating arm: question 50 becomes a priority when a mixed-difficulty
deployment is actually funded, and then needs both easy and hard destinations.
