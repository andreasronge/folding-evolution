Primary: ratio of mean capped evaluations R-hand/R-TS, cells equally weighted, paired 95% percentile block bootstrap over 24 slots (8192 fixed draws). R-hand slots are seed blocks, not independent acquisitions. Retained iff primary upper <1.25, R-hand/R-tree upper <0.80, and solve difference hand-tree lower >-0.03. Learned increment iff primary lower >1.25. Otherwise unresolved, including a failed baseline saving/reliability guard; return to strategy at the fixed size. A ratio near 1.15 with historical precision gives roughly [1.01,1.31] and can remain unresolved; do not add seeds to chase a boundary.

Retention means this fixed, development-informed sum prior retains the B/C saving within the 1.25 margin. It is source-free at execution, neither task-blind nor fresh-bank transfer.
