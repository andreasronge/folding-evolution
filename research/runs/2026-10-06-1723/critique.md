Ten independent trajectories per family are substantially more useful than another 2 + 2 pilot at these costs. More generations or a contextual arm would change the immediate question; another bank screen is not a clearly cheaper route. Stage 1 alone cannot distinguish held-out family specificity from generic transfer: even strong own-training gains and an in-sample crossed preference leave that question open until stage 2. Preserve per-holdout results and the stated scope of one screened BE composition.

3. **note — Keep the early gate operational, and make outcome labels precise.** Four selected maps below 1.1× can justify stopping an unpromising allocation, but selection optimism does not turn two trajectories per family into strong evidence of no learning. Report fresh scores and uncertainty after a stop, and call it “early low-yield stop; learning remains unresolved” unless those data support a narrower bound. Likewise N means a gain above 1.25× is excluded at the stated interval, not that improvement is absent. L and N can both hold (e.g. [1.05, 1.20]); W and B can too. 4. **note — Make the training-only sizing proxy explicit and avoid calling it conservative for every holdout.** Define whether s_off uses only off-family maps or pools both training arms: pooling includes between-arm differences, whereas off-family maps alone do not estimate both variances in the eventual matched/mismatched contrast.
