The frozen holdout comparison is feasible and informative within this bank, but it cannot deliver the fresh-bank transfer conclusion required by this task.

1. **blocking — Bank-level transfer requirement.** The task explicitly identifies `comparison-gate-v1` as a development bank. The saved `freeze.json` and [1246 analysis](../2026-10-08-1246/analysis.md) support the narrower facts that the eight targets were unsearched and the method, split and scoring roster were frozen. That is valuable protection against target-level tuning, but it does not make this a fresh bank under the stated rule. Revise around a genuinely fresh bank frozen with the method before scoring, retaining the existing fits where compatible; a renamed subset, more seeds or one added target is insufficient. Alternatively, propose this explicitly as a development-bank generalization/boundary study and justify its value before the fresh-bank test. Do not let its positive outcome close the fresh-bank transfer gap.

2. **note — Feasibility is credible, with an optimistic cap-time estimate.** Stage 1 supplied 1,808/3,072 collection solves; fresh training searches solved 936/1,024 under C, 791/1,024 under T and 174/256 under G4. These are substantial counts, not a thin-hit extrapolation. The stored projection reproduces 3,938 seconds for the proposed 4,352 searches at training difficulty. However, actual unsolved C/T searches averaged 28.9/28.3 worker-seconds, with maxima of 85.5/74.0 seconds; “about 25 s each” is not a conservative bound. At about 28.5 s and 9.57 effective workers, an all-capped full roster costs roughly 3.6 h before overhead. The six-pair prefix should still fit near that average, but completion is not guaranteed on untimed holdouts. In `plan.md`, retain the fixed balanced prefix, explicit incomplete outcome below six pairs, reporting reserve and no fallback after errors. State holdout solve counts as unknown. The allocation file already grants the budget; the proposal's unfunded wording is stale.

3. **note — Value and the best alternative.** This would distinguish a large source-cell fit effect from performance on eight withheld compositions, which matters to the core map-bias question. Reusing existing tables avoids another collection stage and is more valuable than another training replication or feedback iteration. The more decisive alternative for the stated transfer objective is scoring the same frozen procedure on a genuinely fresh bank. No obviously cheaper seed reduction offers the same precision: at log SD 0.30, 16 corpora give roughly a ×1.17 half-width, whereas the minimum 12 give about ×1.21. Keep the current roster if the narrower development-bank question is independently justified.

4. **note — Prior-work identification is sound.** [Poli and McPhee's N-gram GP](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/9722/1/ces-479.pdf) is the closest representation/model-learning precedent: it learns instruction distributions, using trigrams and iterative EDA updates. PIPE is broader ancestry, with position-specific tree probabilities, not the same previous-token model. Here the map is fitted externally once and then frozen during fresh evolutionary searches. The contribution is a controlled boundary test against a restricted token fit across compositions, not a new learning technique. No additional missing precedent is necessary to justify this run. Preserve question 25's explicit limitation: with K unscored, C/T does not isolate token order from changed emitted frequencies.

5. **note — Outcomes that leave the mechanism unresolved.** A wide interval spanning 1 and a useful gain leaves transfer unresolved, as acknowledged. More subtly, both arms mostly hitting the cap can produce a narrow ratio near 1 without showing that the source fit overfits: it bounds this capped endpoint, not uncapped search speed. Require solve counts alongside that interpretation. Also, training evaluates each corpus only on its own family, while the primary holdout pool includes both families. Therefore pooled shrinkage can reflect family mismatch as well as new compositions. Use the already planned matched/mismatched results to distinguish these; report own-family holdout/training shrinkage before attributing a small pooled effect to source-cell specificity. C beating T while both lose to G4 is relative fit superiority without demonstrated useful adaptation.

6. **note — Decision labels need precedence.** An interval wholly between 1 and 1.10 satisfies both primary branches. Carry forward stage 1's explicit precedence, while reporting both facts: a resolved positive gain and a gain bounded below 10%. This is a plan clarification, not a reason to add statistical gates.
