This bounded descriptive probe has plausible costs and can establish bank and acquisition feasibility, provided plan.md fixes the split inconsistency and keeps pilot comparisons from becoming conclusions.

1. **note — Feasibility is plausible, not measured on this alphabet.** The count is correct: 256 source searches plus 192 development searches = 448. At 11,000 worker-seconds and 9.4 effective workers, compute is about 20 minutes; 55 minutes of summed queue timeouts leaves reasonable room for screening, fitting and verification. The stated 2.5 hours of agent time makes the total about 3.4 hours at the timeout ceiling. However, 0145's 205 worker-seconds per acquisition came from 75% first-batch and 99.6% adaptive solves, not the proposed 15–35% discovery regime. That expectation implies roughly 19–45 first-batch solves across 128 attempts, or 2.4–5.6 per build; under a homogeneous independent-rate illustration, a cell's four attempts are empty with probability 18–52%. Explicitly retain the existing empty-corpus/cell/library fallbacks and report their incidence. Use the collection batches for sustained-load pricing, include fitting and exact-check tails, and stop at the existing limits rather than enlarging this probe if the extrapolation fails.

2. **note — Make the bank admission rule match the promised split.** Four sources + four development + eight protected cells requires **16**, whereas the stated obstacle is fewer than 12. In plan.md require a feasible 16-cell split satisfying all coverage and separation constraints, or explicitly choose a smaller protected roster before search. Freeze seed lists, O artifact identities, deterministic clique/split tie-breaking, alphabet and method hashes at the same time. The saved semantic result supports 24 mutually separated candidates, so this is a specification repair rather than evidence of infeasibility. Its script validates the semantic machine only: “cleared in Python” must not imply validation by the production Python executor. Complete Python/Rust/semantic-machine agreement, including short-input/type rules and legacy replay, before collection; a nine-token screen is not a 16-token minimality certificate.

3. **note — Keep O/A8 descriptive.** “Unresolved from 1, with a point ≥ 0.9” does **not** establish that “old syntax already carries the help”: it permits O to be substantially worse, and both arms might be useless against G4. Likewise, O being costlier only supports an advantage over these eight reinterpreted artifacts, not that fresh acquisition is necessary in general. Report G4/O as well as G4/A8 and O/A8, with solve counts and intervals; describe ambiguity as ambiguity. Keep O's four BE and four PA builds identifiable. The declared token reinterpretation is legitimate for this pilot, but it is not unchanged-semantic transfer from the old task language. No pilot observation should become a digest belief before confirmation.

4. **note — Price precision using both sides of the contrast.** Eight builds with two target seeds per development cell provide a rough planning estimate, not a reliable variance estimate for all protected cells. Propagate the shared G4 baseline's seed uncertainty as well as build uncertainty; increasing builds alone cannot remove that baseline uncertainty. Give the stage-2 half-width calculation as a scenario conditional on development variance, with its target-seed allocation and complete acquisition/score cost. Independent confirmation builds are required by the strategy; these pilot builds cannot substitute for them. Keep protected performance untouched, and freeze the final method with the protected bank before its first scoring.

5. **note — Scope and prior work are appropriate for a probe.** As instructed for `kind: probe`, approval rests on cost and correctness, not a demand for a confirmatory effect. Its deliverable is the feasibility and price of extending cheap acquisition to composite predicates over independent inputs. [PIPE (Salustowicz & Schmidhuber, 1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/) is an appropriate precedent for fitting a program distribution from search; A8 is external fitting, not inherited map evolution. The proposal omits the library-learning connection already recognized elsewhere in this tree: [DreamCoder (Ellis et al., 2021)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf) jointly learns abstractions and a search policy. A8 uses literal windows and a previous-token table; this stage adds a bounded feasibility observation, not a new learning principle or an isolated test of predicate placement, since the alphabet/domain also change.

6. **note — Preserve the cheap staged exit and name uninformative outcomes.** Validate the bank before paying for acquisition, then measure sources before scoring the pilot fits. No additional arm or full-F rebuild is needed for these descriptive questions. A cheaper G4-only probe would omit the fitted-path timing needed to price stage 2; a protected-target comparison belongs in that separately reviewed stage. If all arms mostly hit the cap, a ratio near one says little about their relative ability; if O/A8 is broad, it says little about the need for new-family acquisition. These outcomes still provide feasibility data, but leave the core transfer question unanswered. Report that distinction and return to strategy without relaxing the bank screen.
