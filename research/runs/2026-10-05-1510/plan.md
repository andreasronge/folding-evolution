# Implementation plan (written before experiment execution)

Implement the approved sampling experiment only: no evolutionary runs or research-tree claims. Conditions use the shared TAG alphabet (ops 0–21, 12/13 NOP), 64 cells, threshold zero, uniform tags. Every task is a predicate on four digits 0–9. Exactness requires all 10,000 inputs. Reuse the Rust TAG batch evaluator and exhaustive checker; use one frozen union of 64 balanced training cases per task for screening.

Seeds: master 202610051510; training cases 202610051511; benchmark 202610051512. Independent deterministic stage/distribution/start/iteration/batch streams derive from the master via NumPy SeedSequence, with stream identifiers and sample counts logged. Calibration, fitting, validation and transfer share no sampled tapes. Σ fitting thresholds 5/10/15, M fitting 2/5/7; original holdouts Σ 7/12 and M 3/6, substitutes in the approved order Σ 8/11 and M 4/1. Freeze eligibility and retained tasks after uniform calibration.

Sequence and caps: benchmark uniform and crude enriched vectors including exhaustive-candidate cost; uniform calibration 30M (extend to 60M when a fitting task has fewer than 20 exact hits); eligibility ≥10 hits and P(exact)≤1e-3; stop if either family has fewer than two fitting tasks or no eligible holdout. Pruning uses every calibration exact fitting solver, per-task single knockouts and a joint knockout; only ops executed in ≥5 pooled solvers and breaking none qualify (SEP/RECV excluded). Remove least-used candidates until the joint knockout is harmless. Report present/executed/broken counts, low-support ops, and mark pruning under-supported if any fitting task has fewer than 20 solvers at the cap.

Fit Σ, M and both with three independent starts each, ≤6 iterations and ≤15M samples per fit in total. Exact elites contribute equally by task via executed-cell op frequencies; <10 elites preserves that task's previous contribution. Smooth by 0.5 and constrain all probabilities to ≥0.05/22. Validate each start on 5M independent tapes against calibration; require ≥3 point ratio and 95% lower bound >1 on every fitting task. Select the validated start with greatest mean log ratio. Failure of either family fit makes the outcome inconclusive.

Fresh transfer pools: uniform, Σ-fit, M-fit, prune start at 10M, with up to four scheduled 10M looks (40M cap); both-fit and mass-exchanged Σ/M swaps are descriptive at 10M. Family estimates use the actual retained holdout set; one-member families are explicitly labelled. Fitting-task rates are also reported afresh. Log per-task and family ratios, uncertainty, weights, seeds, throughput, exact-check times and gates, and write machine-readable results and a diagnostic plot under RUN_DIR.

Critique repair: decision intervals use Bonferroni-adjusted exact Poisson rate bounds, jointly over four planned looks, four decisive family comparisons (specificity and gain × two families), and at most two members × numerator/denominator. Combining simultaneous marginal log bounds gives conservative family geometric-mean intervals, including zero counts. Ordinary 95% intervals remain descriptive. Adaptive stopping therefore cannot make an ordinary fixed-look interval justify parking. Beyond-pruning uses adjusted intervals for its own four looks but is a separate descriptive axis and never blocks or changes A/C/D. Shared tapes induce dependence across tasks; simultaneous bounds do not require independence. This deliberately uses conservative bounds instead of assuming independent member counts for combined variance.

Runtime: six-hour queue limit, with internal deadline leaving time to serialize. Budget planning measures effective screening plus exhaustive throughput under enrichment, reserves calibration/fit/validation and decisive transfer first, and cuts both/swap samples and prune extension before decisive precision. Log the frozen scale/caps before sampling each stage; budget-truncated unresolved comparisons stay U. Rebenchmark fitted vectors before allocating transfer; adaptive limits never exceed proposal caps. Smoke uses tiny caps and checks plumbing only, never supports a scientific verdict.

Interpretation (G: lower>1 and point≥3; N: upper<3; otherwise U; G has priority when both descriptions fit): infeasible calibration takes precedence; failed family validation or any U in decisive comparisons is inconclusive. A requires specificity and gain G in both families; partial A requires both G in one family and an N on specificity or gain in the other. C requires specificity N in both and a gain G in at least one. D requires gain N in both with validated fits. Every unlisted combination is mixed/unresolved, including the critique's opposed specificity/gain example. Member-level reversals are reported even if average transfer resolves. A supports a learnable, transferable frequency bias only over retained thresholds; evolutionary benefit remains open. C supports generic useful supply in this regime; D rules out worthwhile average matched transfer at the measured precision. Neither inconclusive nor mixed supports parking as C/D. Beyond-pruning N narrows the practical gain; the swaps descriptively test aggregator mass versus threshold construction.

Verification: small deterministic planted-solvers/exhaustive checks for sum and max; knockout and executed-cell accounting; probability floors and equal-task updates; zero-count and stopping-adjusted intervals; all verdict combinations and one-holdout aggregation; small end-to-end gated smoke plus synthetic all-stage plumbing smoke if uniform eligibility is too sparse. Validate the full queue with scripts/run_queue.py. Commit implementation and task artifacts on research/2026-10-05-1510, verify both worktree and research task-artifact status.

## Implementation details and smoke verification

The existing TAG evaluator is Python/NumPy, not Rust. Added a sampling-only Rust path with the same typed preserve pops, same-tag max, root-context memoization, cycle/depth guards and wrapping int64 arithmetic. Differential checks cover 500 random tapes enriched for SEP/RECV and duplicate small tags. Exactness and knockouts are checked against planted solvers for every frozen candidate threshold. The evolution engine and existing TAG evaluator are unchanged.

Executed frequencies count distinct visited tape cells, including their SEP and NOP cells. This avoids counting inert leaders/unreferenced runs and weights repeated RECV invocations only once per physical cell. The method is logged with pruning support. Training samples use replacement to retain 32 cases per label when a task has fewer than 32 distinct negatives (max>1 has only 16); the union removes repeated inputs for execution. All tasks are still checked exhaustively. Benchmark streams use seed 202610051512; other named streams derive from the master.

Decision adjustment is the conservative simultaneous-bound method stated above: marginal alpha 0.05/64 for the four decisive comparisons across four looks, and 0.05/32 for the separate pruning axis. Validation uses marginal alpha 0.05/4 for its two rate bounds and the two possible calibration looks, making its lower bound conservative for the requested 95% gate. Zero-hit cells retain an unbounded upper ratio when appropriate; there is no pseudocount in classification or the mean-log objective. Classification follows the stated G/N/U thresholds.

The initial benchmark reserves the maximum decisive design (280M tapes), with 20% runtime margin; fit/validation budgets can tighten using measured effective throughput while reserving subsequent decisive stages. Transfer scale is frozen after fresh fitted-vector throughput checks. All scales only reduce proposal caps. Prune extensions and both/swap diagnostics run after the decisive pools; an internal deadline yields an explicit inconclusive result. Individual stream records include weights, requested samples and completed samples; cumulative pool snapshots are kept separate.

Smoke verification is infrastructure evidence only. Two real 20,000-tape uniform calibration smokes had zero exact hits and correctly stopped at task eligibility; this is not an estimate of feasibility at the full cap. Synthetic counts exercise all subsequent stages, both a reciprocal A pattern and an all-zero transfer pattern that reaches the four-look cap and remains inconclusive. Planted genomes are used only by correctness tests and exhaustive-check cost benchmarks, never as fitting/calibration/transfer observations. No full experiment was executed by the researcher.
