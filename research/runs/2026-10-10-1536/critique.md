The measured cost supports a useful protected-cell confirmation of A8 acquisition, provided the plan limits the conclusion to this development bank rather than claiming fresh-bank transfer.

1. **note — Feasible, with credible acquisition risk.** [0311 analysis](../2026-10-10-0311/analysis.md) reports G4/A8/O development solves of 7/64, 55/64 and 22/64; source batches solved 22/128 and 81/128. Four of eight intermediate libraries were empty, although all eight final libraries completed. These are adequate planning observations, not guaranteed protected-cell rates. There are 768 acquisition searches plus 896 scoring searches. The proposed arithmetic checks: 16.7 k acquisition worker-seconds plus 18.5–22.6 k scoring worker-seconds gives roughly 64–71 minutes at 9.2 effective workers, within 120 minutes of timeouts. Preserve every failed acquisition and fallback, including any completely empty first batch in the larger sample; no replacing weak builds. Record the fallback and runtime-admission behavior explicitly in plan.md.

2. **note — Required scope correction.** `x4-double-gate-v1` has already been used and is a development bank under the supplied bank rule. Its previously frozen, unsearched cells support an honest protected within-bank confirmation; fresh seeds and fresh builds do not establish a fresh-bank transfer result. Replace the positive outcome label “useful transfer to the new family” with “useful protected-cell replication on x4-double-gate-v1, relative to G4 at this cap.” The contribution is confirmation beyond the four development cells for this predicate-addition setup. Do not turn it into general recipe portability, transfer of the old artifacts to a new family, or a fresh-bank result. A claim requiring fresh-bank transfer needs a genuinely fresh bank frozen with the method before scoring; renaming this split is insufficient. This is a scope note because the narrower experiment remains worth running.

3. **note — Value and alternatives.** Confirming whether independently rebuilt bias helps the protected compositions changes a relevant belief about acquisition beyond output-addition setups. The strongest immediate alternative is a same-alphabet crossed-family comparison, which would address family specificity more directly, but requires another validated family and acquisition arms. First establishing protected usefulness is a reasonable cheaper step. A new PSB2 interface is a larger investment without resolving this immediate uncertainty. The 24-build design targets the 1.5× decision rather than precise estimation of the large pilot effect; the pilot's 53–64-build precision scenario is unnecessary. Dropping O saves only about six queue minutes, so retaining it descriptively is reasonable. No clearly cheaper design buys the same build-reliability and protected-cell answer.

4. **note — Prior-work framing is appropriate.** [PIPE (Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, 1997)](https://gpbib.cs.ucl.ac.uk/gp-html/Salustowicz_97ecj.html) is a reasonable distribution-learning ancestor; its iterative best-program updates are not the same procedure as externally fitting a previous-token table and literal windows for frozen reuse. [DreamCoder (Ellis et al., 2021)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf) is the closer broad analogy for jointly learning reusable components and search bias across problems. Neither makes this a new learning principle. Its addition is a bounded replication of the inexpensive A8 recipe under a different assembly requirement. No missing prior technique is necessary to interpret that contribution. Keep O's token reinterpretation explicit: O/A8 cannot isolate family specificity.

5. **note — Outcomes that leave the important question unresolved.** An interval spanning 1.5× leaves usefulness unresolved; retain the promised resolution price. If both arms mostly cap, a ratio near one says little about uncapped difficulty or why acquisition failed. If success depends on the 2× failure charge, report penalty-sensitive capped-cost usefulness, not an ordinary time-to-solution speed-up. A pooled success accompanied by failure on the three unseen-pairing cells would confirm only the aggregate benefit, leaving new-pairing assembly unresolved. Freeze these interpretations in plan.md. The primary build-resampled interval is appropriate to fixed target cells; it does not sample uncertainty over families or source rosters. Pooled Wilson solve intervals should be labeled descriptive, since searches sharing a fitted build are not independent acquisition replicates.
