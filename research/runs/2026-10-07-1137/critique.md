The paired continuation is a feasible, appropriately bounded attempt to obtain a working learning control, with no clearly cheaper decisive alternative, provided plan.md resolves the diagnostic accounting and interpretation notes below.

1. **note — Feasibility is supported.** The arithmetic checks: 12 × 8 × 96 + 2 × 192 = 9 600 searches per trajectory; 153 600 per arm; 12 000 F1 and 16 500 full F2 searches. At the measured solve fractions, expect roughly 146 000 solves per arm and 11 856 / 16 302 in F1 / F2, respectively. These are planning expectations, not guaranteed rates after adaptation. The 0821 analysis supports the quoted 0.47 / 0.87 worker-seconds and approximately five-hour total at ten workers. Comparison noise sqrt(3.95/96) = 0.203 log2 and reliability 0.028/(0.028 + 3.95/96) = 0.405 are plausible approximations, not evidence that selection will accumulate gains. Keep the 45% gate-pass probability explicitly a judgment.

2. **note — Account for the independent selected-change diagnostic.** The reviewed runner rescored only the two surviving candidates next generation. An accepted child's actual source parent need not survive, and the other surviving candidate need not be its source parent. In plan.md, specify whether this readout covers only child/source-parent pairs that both survive (a selected subset, with coverage counts), or budgets extra evaluations of discarded source parents. Complete coverage can require up to 2 × 96 × 11 × 16 = 33 792 extra searches per arm before the final generation, about 28 minutes per arm at the proposed throughput. Those searches are absent from the five-hour estimate. Use a bounded diagnostic sample if necessary, keep diagnostic scores out of selection, and update admission reserves. Do not silently treat the retained competitor as the child's parent.

3. **note — Good procedure test, limited causal separation.** Increasing evidence fourfold while reducing generations from 20 to 12 and raising total search effort 2.4-fold tests a new procedure; success would not isolate scoring noise as the cause of 0821's result. Failure would not distinguish a plateau from insufficient depth. The midpoint and independent selected-change readouts help characterize that ambiguity. Row 1 should say that this procedure's mean gain is bounded below 1.10×, not that these starts cannot supply a positive control with this operator at any budget. A longer n=24 arm would isolate a different comparison but consume the contextual comparison's budget; joint learning from G4 answers a different question. Neither is clearly superior within this deadline.

4. **note — Size is reasonable but borderline for a small null.** With 8 + 8 starts, the approximately 0.18-log2 T/S and 0.20-log2 C/T half-widths are consistent with 0821. A true 1.15× token gain has only modest headroom above the confirmation threshold, and log2(1.15) = 0.202 is almost exactly the C/T half-width: even true equality will often remain unresolved against the 1.15× bound. This is an honest, useful five-hour attempt, not a reliably decisive small-effect study. Rows 2 and 5, and row 7 with a broad C/T interval, leave the main explanations unresolved; they still constrain this procedure. F2 provides fresh-search confirmation of the same learned maps, not independent replication of learning trajectories.

5. **note — Fix admission and outcome semantics in plan.md.** The runtime prose says a shortened context stage becomes row 3, whereas the table permits 6 + 6 or 7 + 7 starts to reach substantive rows. Choose one rule before execution; if using the pre-stated minimum, report the larger intervals and compute T/S(F2) on the same starts as C/T. Reserve fresh scoring even if no C trajectory fits. Define “bit-identical” for S using deterministic scientific fields, excluding elapsed-time fields. Row 4 establishes C outperforming T; if T/S and C/S do not resolve improvement, describe that relative advantage without claiming beneficial adaptation over S. Row 5 still bounds C/T despite the unconfirmed control; “says nothing about context” is too strong. These are interpretation and implementation notes, not reasons to redesign the primary contrast.
