**What the program has learned.** The [core question](../../../README.md#core-question)
asks both how the map biases discovery and whether that bias can adapt to a task family.
The [digest](../../digest.md), question tree, [ledger](../../briefs/2026-10-05-2225-ledger.md)
and recent decisions support a useful but bounded answer:

- Arrival frequencies matter, but do not alone predict evolutionary discovery. Folding's
  fixed-target advantage was consistent with increased solver supply; the TAG lexicase
  regime behaved differently. Cheap joins helped composition, and selected-mate versus
  self-mate crossover changed establishment. These are map-and-operator results, not a
  general developmental advantage. The natural B-helper arrival/fixation question remains
  unresolved; the completed helper line need not be reopened to acknowledge that.
- Adapted token weights transfer when program populations restart. Threshold fits helped
  held-out search about 4× over uniform, with a hand-set scaffold performing comparably.
  Root 10's weights on supplied G/G4 context improve withheld compositions about 2–3×.
  This demonstrates outer-loop adaptation, not simultaneous map/program coevolution.
- Training-family information is much less established than generic improvement.
  [Crossed transfer](../2026-10-06-2229/analysis.md) gave matched/mismatched ratios of
  1.02× on BE and 0.96× on PA. A modest preference remains possible; the BE upper bound
  rises to about 1.3× in sensitivity checks. One BE holdout cannot establish task generality.
  Hand-set context can strengthen a crossed preference, but only the BE grammar had a
  resolved own-family improvement over G4. Capacity is not evidence of learning.
- The learned map helps both through its starting programs and through its use during
  search: each adds about 1.3× given the other, with strong sub-additivity, across the
  [withheld](../2026-10-06-2331/analysis.md) and
  [training](../2026-10-07-0315/analysis.md) sets. The relative balance differs between BE
  and PA training cells, but shape and difficulty remain confounded. This does not isolate
  mutation, crossover or a particular supply of partial programs.
- Three context procedures have failed to establish an increment over token tuning.
  Crucially, [0821](../2026-10-07-0821/decision.md) also failed to establish further token
  learning: C/T 0.955× [0.833, 1.095], T/S 1.03× [0.90, 1.16]. Selected single-step mutants
  beat average mutants but did not demonstrably beat their parents. That limits the tested
  continuation; it does not establish that context is ineffective. Earlier 0811 token
  continuation did improve, on different starts and a different bank.
