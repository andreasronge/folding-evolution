## Inference and outcome meaning

Preserve the inherited conservative decision correction: marginal alpha 0.05/64 for four looks × four decisive comparisons × at most two members × two rate bounds, although this revision has one held-out member per family. Simultaneous Poisson marginal bounds tolerate shared-tape dependence; zero counts remain uncertain without pseudocounts. Specificity is matched versus mismatched fit on the same held-out task; gain is matched versus uniform. Beyond-pruning has its separate descriptive four-look correction (0.05/32), and never gates the primary verdict. Fixed-look intervals, fitting rechecks, both-fit, swaps and one-ADD thresholds are descriptive only. G means point ≥3 and lower>1; N means upper<3; otherwise U; G has priority if both criteria apply.

Infeasible calibration comes first. Failed family validation, incomplete decisive stages, or any U on either decisive axis gives inconclusive before other verdicts. For the resolved cross-product of specificity/gain classes: A requires specificity G and gain G in both families; partial A requires both G in one family with the other failing one or both; C requires specificity N in both and gain G in at least one; D requires gain N in both with validated fits; any remaining opposed combination is mixed/unresolved. This preserves all outcome cells rather than assuming the two axes move together.

A supports learnable family-specific frequency transfer on this one threshold split and motivates a subsequent evolutionary A-versus-B test. C supports generic enrichment at the chosen worthwhile 3× specificity threshold, not absence of all family information, and parks this frequency-fitting approach. D rules out worthwhile matched gain on these held-out members; report CONST_2 weights and swaps alongside it. Suppression of the withheld constant is a possible explanation, not an identified cause, and D does not prove frequency adaptation generally fails or decoder changes are necessary. Partial/mixed outcomes narrow the result to the successful family; U or failed fit validation separates neither C nor D. Only a later approved run may extend a near-boundary case. Infeasible task set parks this sampling version for strategist review, with richer constants or an owner-approved multi-G design as reopen conditions.
