This is a useful, bounded feasibility experiment before investing in map adaptation; the researcher must resolve the operational details below in `plan.md`.

1. **note — Feasibility is plausible, but partly extrapolated.** The stated 1800 searches at 6–15 seconds imply roughly 23–56 minutes on eight workers, consistent with the search allocation. At the measured sampling rate, 200M tapes take about five minutes per arm and yield roughly 200–2000 hits for cells with rates of 1–10 per million. Neither that rate nor the solve counts are established for MIN cells, seven-token sampling, or the new contextual decoder. Stage A also entails about 12.2M programs and 7.6 billion program/input evaluations; its five-minute estimate needs a benchmark. Begin with a bounded throughput calibration of enumeration, decoded sampling and capped search, including concurrent-worker performance. Reserve time for the required cell coverage and classify unfinished work as unresolved. The three-hour envelope is reasonable if these measurements support it.

2. **note — The experiment addresses the right immediate uncertainty.** Establishing a non-aliased, searchable bank is more useful now than building the outer loop. G and G-marg justify their additional cost: they test whether simple context supplies anything beyond marginal token frequencies and whether generic grammar exhausts the practical headroom. They do not yet distinguish learned family structure from overfitting or mismatched training; those require experiment 2. Freeze numerical G probabilities and the distribution used to measure its marginals before search. Interpret G/G-marg as the combined effect of context on supply and variation, not as proof of a particular mechanism. No clearly superior replacement experiment is evident.

3. **note — Make the split rule executable before running.** Three holdouts plus at least four training cells require at least seven passing cells, so the stated fallback for fewer than six cannot work. Specify eligibility over retained cells, whether seven- or eight-cell banks are allowed, and the coverage required after exclusions. Reconcile that with “every cell in the grid” passing U tractability. Fix orientation ties and apply the shorter-program screen separately for each canonical length; ADD must not fail merely because enumeration includes its own five-token solver. These are plan clarifications, not reasons to discard the feasibility design.

4. **note — Size is appropriate; simplify the decision bookkeeping.** Fifty seeds per arm/cell can establish broad tractability and large headroom differences, and the gated extra 100 are reasonable near a decision boundary. State the pooled-count equivalent of 35/50, the pooled unresolved band, and whether marginal training cells can receive the same top-up as holdouts. Apply incomplete-data and unresolved checks before assigning a negative outcome. Define how an unestimated Kaplan–Meier median is handled. There is no need for a larger confirmatory statistical apparatus in this bank-selection run.

5. **note — Validate uniform decoding against the same alphabet.** Adding MIN changes uniform token probabilities from 1/22 to 1/23, so the old direct `v2_probe` S+M sampling interval is not an appropriate equivalence target. Compare tied-uniform decoding with direct uniform `v2_rmin`, preferably using exactly corresponding token tapes and checking identical scores. Keep the old probes as runtime and discovery estimates, not a decoder correctness gate.

6. **note — Identify what would leave the central question unanswered.** An incomplete bank, a still-borderline tractability/headroom decision, or very sparse seven-token sampling would leave the relevant feasibility quantity unresolved. A null G/G-marg comparison would also leave learned transfer unanswered, even with precise search estimates. Conversely, a complete bank failure is informative about this candidate design. Project outer-loop cost from censored runs as well as solves, include decoder overhead and all adaptation controls, and retain the training solve-rate requirement when considering a cheaper 32k inner budget. Splitting an expensive adaptation into queues satisfies the review limit but does not reduce total cost; report both quantities before calling experiment 2 affordable.
