The census is useful, but multiplying terminal-population arrival rates by hand-built single-copy wins cannot distinguish why shared forms are rare in random-start evolution.

- **The proposed prediction has the wrong comparison.** In the [§32 table](../../../docs/map-bias/reviews/s32/s32_report.md), L64/self/0.3 has two first-established shared populations and two shared endings; 0.7 has neither. A post-solution transition model does not explain shared forms that establish first. Also, a 0.3 census cannot predict a pooled 0.3/0.7 endpoint. Compare post-establishment transitions within the matching 0.3 cohort, and distinguish first discovery from later replacement. Terminal snapshots cannot identify the discovery mechanism; acknowledge that remaining uncertainty when stopping.

- **A cheaper, better-matched experiment is available.** Start with §32 J's L64/self/0.3 terminal populations only. Generate children using the actual parent-selection distribution, including eligible elite parents, followed by self-crossover and mutation. Equal K per non-elite measures an unweighted neighbourhood: the [engine](../../../src/folding_evolution/chem_tape/evolve.py:713) uses lexicase over the whole population, so many non-exact individuals in solved populations contribute no offspring. Retain parent-form strata, but weight them by reproductive contribution for an arrival estimate. Exclude shared-to-shared persistence from new-arrival counts; distinguish A-only sharing from B-helper sharing. Selected-mate source populations, mutation-only arms and hand-built references can wait unless this first stage leaves a specific ambiguity.

- **Measure establishment where arrivals were measured.** If the census produces shared children from non-shared parents, save them and test single copies in their source evolved populations, inserted as ordinary offspring, with matched continuations without the insertion. Sample multiple source populations and child layouts. The proposed hand-built-versus-clonal contests instead measure another layout-specific quantity; they cannot tell whether a newborn mutant in an evolved population establishes. If no natural children appear, an evolved shared-genome insertion can be a labelled transfer control, but does not fill that gap. Report finite-horizon establishment, extinction and unresolved persistence, rather than calling every non-win a fixation failure at generation 300.

- **Size against the decision, not the clock.** At a true 1% win rate, 0/100 happens about 37% of the time; its one-sided 95% upper bound is about 3%. Thus the proposed outcome B does not establish a rate below the existing 1–3% reference. Roughly 300 zero-win trials are needed even to bound a rate below 1%, before accounting for reference uncertainty. Use a capped staged design, with an explicitly inconclusive outcome. For the census, report denominators by source population and relevant parent stratum: ten million pooled children are not ten million trials of each transition. Choose exposure from the arrival-rate bound needed to change the interpretation, and report variation across source populations.

- **What would leave us no wiser.** Zero arrivals plus zero contest wins leaves both bottlenecks possible. Agreement of the proposed product with two endings could be accidental; disagreement could reflect selection weighting, different layouts, changing populations or observation horizons, not specifically multistep paths. A one-step census already includes non-exact parents; what it misses is their changing occupancy through time. Preserve “unresolved” rather than assigning C by elimination. The narrower matched experiment can resolve late arrival versus establishment locally; explaining first discovery would require instrumented random-start runs, which need not be commissioned merely to close this line.

- **Parked questions and stopping.** No ignored reopen condition is established. [02](../../questions/01-map-bias/02-fixed-target-sampling/question.md) also requires an owner desire to settle fixed-target bias, which is not recorded; [06](../../questions/01-map-bias/06-self-mate-establishment/question.md) has no new qualifying replicate or reversal. Keep 07's stop rule and move on after this bounded test, including if the broader arrival claim remains unanswered.
