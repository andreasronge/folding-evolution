The revised experiment is a useful bounded test of late replacement, but its population weighting and insertion readout still need small changes before they distinguish arrival from establishment.

- **Keep this design; align the two estimates.** The matched cohort, actual reproduction and natural children address the [previous critique](../2026-10-04-2044/critique.md). However, 24M is total offspring exposure, whereas the proposed r is conditional on partly-shared parentage. Estimate each population's arrivals per *all* offspring, summing contributions from every non-shared parent stratum, and weight by its established non-shared duration. Keep the other terminal populations descriptive, separate from the 16-run replacement target. Estimate establishment with corresponding arrival weights: a balanced sample of distinct layouts with a 1/5 population cap does not estimate the fate of a randomly arriving child. Preserve occurrence counts and report source-specific results; do not multiply differently weighted pooled E and p. Specify what happens if arrivals come from fewer than five populations, when the cap is impossible.

- **Track descendants, not merely the shared form.** No-insertion controls estimate background establishment but cannot identify an individual inserted lineage. This matters most in the high-arrival branch, where independent shared children can establish after the inserted copy dies. Propagate an insertion-descendant flag through reproduction, including elites; the [engine already exposes parent indices](../../../src/folding_evolution/chem_tape/evolve.py). Report descendant survival, descendant exact-shared share and total shared share separately. B-helper versus A-only is a form label, not ancestry. Also retain the existing minimum of 20 exact non-elite individuals for a population verdict, or explicitly justify changing it: 50% of two exact individuals is not comparable to the historical establishment readout. Ten-generation observations give interval-censored loss times, not exact extinction times.

- **The caps are reasonable, with narrower interpretations.** Zero arrivals in 30M relevant draws gives an approximate 95% upper rate of 1e-7; zero lineage establishments in 300 trials bounds the sampled mixture's 500-generation probability near 1%. Neither establishes a bound for every source population. Five wins in 100 does not establish the ≥10% probability required by outcome A. Likewise, low arrival alone cannot exclude an additional establishment bottleneck. If natural arrivals are absent, skip the 100 foreign-genome transfer controls unless the owner wants that separate layout question; they cannot supply the missing natural-copy probability. This is the clearest saving available.

- **What could still leave the explanations unresolved.** Zero arrivals plus unmeasured natural-copy success, arrivals concentrated in one source, and mostly persistent submajority lineages all leave the bottleneck comparison open. The mid-phase check is valuable: if omitted or inconclusive, restrict conclusions to terminal populations rather than the whole established phase. Matching the best genotype alone does not establish whole-population replay fidelity; compare saved census/history too and verify the final population when possible. A focused instrumented replay of the known replacement run would be more decisive about that transition's path, but would still be one event; full-cohort replay need not be commissioned to stop this line.

- **No ignored reopen condition is established.** [02](../../questions/01-map-bias/02-fixed-target-sampling/question.md) also requires an owner desire to settle fixed-target bias, which is not recorded. [06](../../questions/01-map-bias/06-self-mate-establishment/question.md) has no new qualifying replicate or reversal. Preserve 07's stop rule, including parking with first discovery and historical occupancy unresolved.
