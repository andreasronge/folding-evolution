This is a feasible, useful development-bank test of whether a separate fragment repertoire adds enough beyond W to merit acquisition work; address the following notes in plan.md.

1. **note — Feasibility is credible; make the solve scenarios explicit.** [1548](../2026-10-08-1548/analysis.md) measured C at 1,771/2,048 solves on then-addition and 1,924/2,048 on holdouts: approximately 3,542/4,096 and 962/1,024 at this proposal's sizes. F/W target-bank rates are unknown; [0843](../2026-10-09-0843/analysis.md) supplies training rates of 93.8%/94.1%, versus C's 90.6%, not target-bank forecasts. State conservative and improved-rate scenarios, rather than assuming the training rates carry over. The runtime arithmetic checks: `(4096×7.1 + 1024×4.5)×(1+0.76+0.80)` is about 86,241 worker-seconds, or 144 minutes on ten workers and 165 with margin. The 235-minute timeout ledger fits. Preselect smoke cells/corpora spanning both source families and target difficulties, freeze scoring seeds before smoke, and use timing alone for the 16→12-seed fallback. Retain the prior rule that an incomplete roster cannot yield an efficacy decision.

2. **note — Worth running, with a specific decision at stake.** F/W directly tests whether acquiring/storing a repertoire is worth considering over a cheaper operator using the already fitted C. The strongest alternative is to isolate why W helps (local editing and suffix preservation versus chain content). That would explain W better but would not decide whether the observed extra F gain survives excluded compositions; retaining W here makes this continuation the better immediate allocation. Acquisition itself remains untested. A cheaper version could omit the within-shape reference, saving roughly 20 scoring minutes before margin; this is a modest saving, and the reference helps distinguish general failure of the whole-corpus operator from failure on then-addition. No additional B arm or fresh-bank construction is necessary for this scoped decision.

3. **note — Precision is a scenario, and the expected middle can remain indecisive.** I reran the supplied variance probe on 0843 scoring rows and reproduced pair SD 1.85, estimated between-corpus SD 0.062, and half-width ×1.072 (×1.082 at the 12-seed fallback). The decomposition pools cell variation into within-corpus noise; changing cells and switching libraries can change both variance components. Label these projections conditional on the training-noise model. At ×1.072, an observed F/W of 1.05 gives about [0.979, 1.126]; 1.08 gives [1.007, 1.158]. Both leave the repertoire decision unresolved, even though the latter resolves a small gain. A within-shape win with such a primary interval also leaves that decision open. Rule 2 earns a review, not evidence that the true increment exceeds 1.10. Keep the planned resolution price and avoid automatic top-ups.

4. **note — Correct precedent; modest contribution.** [Keijzer, Ryan & Cattolico's run-transferable libraries](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf) are the appropriate closest precedent. [Rosca's ARL](https://cdn.aaai.org/Symposia/Fall/1995/FS-95-01/FS95-01-011.pdf) and [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) are relevant broader abstraction-learning precedents. This adds a controlled boundary test of frozen literal tape blocks alongside a fitted decoder, against matched chain-generated blocks; it does not add callable abstractions or a new acquisition mechanism. No missing precedent changes this judgment.

5. **note — Preserve the actual scope and deployment cost.** Both banks are development banks: positive results support reuse on these excluded compositions, not fresh-bank transfer. The corpus interval conditions on the fixed cells. Whole-corpus libraries use four source cells instead of three-cell leave-one-out libraries, so a change from 0843 is not a clean estimate of shape attenuation. W needs no retained fragment repertoire, but its length law currently comes from F's library; document the saved schedule and distinguish “no stored repertoire” from “no library-derived design information.” For repayment, compare arithmetic savings against W as well as C, using consistent evaluation or wall-time units and separating extraction from shared corpus collection. A geometric 1.10× alone does not establish break-even.
