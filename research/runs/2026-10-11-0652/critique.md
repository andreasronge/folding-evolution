This is a feasible, bounded replacement test that can guide the next donor generator, provided its conclusions stay conditional on this construction and its inherited shapes.

1. **note — Feasibility is credible; complete the admission rule in plan.md.** The arithmetic is right: 1,728 × (6.84 + 2.25 + 2.34 + 6.84 + 5.40) ≈ 40,902 worker-seconds, or 68 minutes at ten workers. [0524](../2026-10-11-0524/analysis.md) sustained 9.98 workers; on B/C, R-tree solved 1,723/1,728 and both donor arms 1,728/1,728, with first-attempt rates about 50%, 79% and 80%. Uniform donors have no measured solve rate yet, so pricing them as R-tree is an assumption, appropriately subject to stage 0. State the calibration job count, observed solves/tails, and an explicit infeasible exit if dropping R-size and reducing repeats still exceeds 100 minutes. The quoted R-size 5.40 seconds is 0348's all-split mean, not its B/C mean (47's opening log gives 5.22); this is slightly conservative. The full 4.5-hour estimate is plausible with harness reuse.

2. **note — Worth one run, with a bounded claim.** This asks what information a competitive search bias must retain, directly relevant to the core question. It is more useful now than tightening 47's intact/permuted interval or scoring another familiar-parts bank. R-size is a reasonable descriptive comparator, but dropping it saves roughly 15 minutes without losing the primary decision. Historical controls would save more queue time but sacrifice contemporaneous pairing; that saving is small relative to agent time. A source-independent pair-sum generator is the strongest next practical alternative, but it answers whether a supplied prior competes, not whether this particular acquired information can be discarded. Do not extend this local ingredient sequence automatically.

3. **note — Treat this as replacement of label supply, not isolation of frequencies.** R-perm samples without replacement within size/length/arity/width pools; R-unif samples independently, changing finite-library dependencies as well as counts. A loss supports retaining the empirical construction for now, not “any … acquisition must keep label supply” or a necessity to learn those exact frequencies. Retention establishes sufficiency of this uniform replacement conditional on acquired skeletons, widths, sizes and multiplicities; it does not establish that the larger skeletons cause the gain. Thus “B/C-specific pairs did not matter” and “total pair supply tracks usefulness” are hypotheses, not results of 47 or of the unresolved R-size/R-tree contrast (0.860 [0.716, 1.011]). Preserve these distinctions in the outcome wording. This development-bank study cannot supply a new transfer claim.

4. **note — Prior-work positioning is broadly right, but PIPE is the prospective learner.** [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber's PIPE (1997)](https://gpbib.pmacs.upenn.edu/gp-html/Salustowicz_97ecml.html) uses an adaptive probabilistic prototype tree; this experiment implements neither its learning loop nor position-specific fitted tables. Call the contribution a donor-library replacement test informing a later probabilistic generator. The immediate lineage is subtree/library transfer in GP, already cited in [0150's proposal](../2026-10-11-0150/proposal.md), including Dinh, Chu & Nguyen (2015) and Keijzer, Ryan & Cattolico (2004). External fitting and freezing, without inheritance, are correctly identified.

5. **note — Freeze fallback weighting and interpretation before scoring.** Six repeats give two searches per realization; four with `repeat mod 3` give 2/1/1. Specify whether the estimand weights realizations equally and make the nested bootstrap match it, preserving search-seed pairing across arms. Name R-perm as the solve-difference reference, and specify reliability reporting against intact R-lib when claiming retained acquired benefit. The observed ±18% interval is encouraging, not a precision guarantee for R-unif. An interval spanning meaningful loss and retention, failed baseline guards, or retention versus R-perm with unresolved intact comparison leaves the acquisition choice unsettled; report that and the precision price. Even a decisive loss will not separate ADD dilution, ANY supply, constants and dependency changes. No extra mechanism arms are required for this replacement decision.
