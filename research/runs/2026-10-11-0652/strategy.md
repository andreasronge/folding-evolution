**What the program has learned.**

- **Bias matters, but random arrival alone does not explain discovery.** Root 01
  separated sampling advantages, cheap joins and lineage mixing. Easy behaviours'
  random frequency predicts accessibility better than hard evolutionary outcomes;
  self-mating removed the tested shared-form establishment barrier while preserving
  some discovery. Earlier folding results do not establish a general advantage of
  developmental encodings ([root 01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- **Previously evolved programs contain reusable search information.** Outer
  selection acquired useful token weights, but the tested contextual selection
  procedures added no resolved gain. External solver fitting succeeded: context
  transferred beyond token fitting, including about 2× on a fresh shape; pooled
  and positional frequency replacements and random mutation widening did not
  reproduce it. Literal fragments added further speed. Partial-program fitting
  helped less, and the tested pre-solve fragment extractor did not recover the
  solver-library increment ([digest](../../digest.md)). Useful external learning
  is established at these scopes; evolved contextual acquisition is not.
- **Cost and comparators changed the practical answer.** Sparse A8 acquisition
  transferred and survived different sources. On DG, DG-acquired artifacts beat
  TS-acquired ones about 3×, with useful information in both table and library;
  reciprocal specificity remains unresolved. But common restarts made plain tree
  GP about a fifth cheaper than A8 in evaluations. A8 remained cheaper in worker
  time. Large gains over weak G4 alone cannot justify another acquisition
  ([41](../2026-10-10-1717/analysis.md), [42](../2026-10-10-2001/analysis.md),
  [44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md)).
- **Learning can improve that stronger structural search.** Tree-source libraries
  cut restarted-tree cost to **0.390 [0.332, 0.463]** on DG and, frozen, to
  **0.250 [0.217, 0.289]** on a fresh gate-sum composition. Worker time also fell;
  gate-sum repayment was about 55 searches per acquisition. This is one source
  cohort and a new composition of familiar parts, not unfamiliar-content transfer
  ([45](../2026-10-11-0150/analysis.md), [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md)).
- **The latest result separates two uses of a library.** On the predicate-matched
  split A, label rearrangement cost **5.14× [3.68, 7.14]** relative to intact
  donors. On unmatched B/C, it cost **1.031× [0.854, 1.225]**, meeting the 1.25
  retention rule, and remained **0.360× [0.299, 0.429]** of plain tree search.
  Both donor arms solved 1,728/1,728 B/C jobs; this is not population equality.
  Rearrangement also tripled B/C pair supply: cancellation remains possible,
  and neither token frequencies alone nor a no-learning explanation is established
  ([47](../2026-10-11-0524/analysis.md)).
- **Useful inheritance remains missing.** Root 23's frozen inherited frequency
  vectors were worse than uniform on sum; on max a gain above about 1.06× was
  excluded. Ancestry helped relative to shuffling on max without establishing
  usefulness. That limits one rule and exposure schedule, not self-adaptation
  generally ([23](../../questions/23-heritable-variation-bias/question.md)).
