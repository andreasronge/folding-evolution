**What the program has learned.**

- Maps change which programs arrive and persist, but no general folding
  superiority follows. Frequency predicts easy behaviours better than hard
  evolutionary discoveries; cheap joins explained the tested composition
  advantage, and lineage mixing obstructed rare shared forms
  ([01](../../questions/01-map-bias/question.md)).
- Search can teach reusable bias through external fitting. Solver-fitted
  context beat token fitting, including **2.12× [1.86, 2.41]** on fresh
  then-addition compositions. Marginal matching and random mutation widening
  did not reproduce it. Fragments and cheap feedback acquisition added value;
  DG-acquired components carried a useful family preference in one direction.
  Outer-selected token weights helped, but the contextual learners tested
  showed no resolved increment ([10](../../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md)).
- Strong comparators changed the practical story. Matched restarts made
  ordinary tree GP about a fifth cheaper than A8 in DG evaluations, while
  A8 remained cheaper in worker seconds. Tree libraries subsequently cut
  cost 2.5–4×, including a fresh gate-sum shape, but supplied sum donors beat
  acquired TS libraries and those libraries harmed near-solved TS targets
  ([44](../2026-10-11-0024/analysis.md), [46](../2026-10-11-0348/analysis.md),
  [49](../2026-10-11-0833/analysis.md), [51](../2026-10-11-1019/analysis.md)).
- Order acquisition now has evidence beyond development scores. Frozen R-all
  cost **0.37 [0.33, 0.42]** of R-tree on held-out combinations and repaid
  acquisition after about four searches. Supplied order helpers were still
  **6.9× cheaper**. Rearranging helper entries raised development cost
  **2.35× [2.07, 2.66]**: intact computations beat mostly trivial replacements
  of matched material. This is one bank, not fresh-content transfer or a
  comparison against all nontrivial replacement content
  ([1530](../2026-10-11-1530/analysis.md), [1628](../2026-10-11-1628/analysis.md)).
- Inheritance has a real but narrow positive result. Recurrent mutation let
  program-linked selection raise a supplied-library bit to **0.29**, versus
  **0.06/0.05** in neutral/shuffled controls. Each population's frozen law
  cost **0.805 [0.704, 0.913]** of its start: below 1, but unresolved by the
  pre-stated 10% usefulness rule. It changes only library dose; fixed R-all
  is **1.71× cheaper**, without exposure. This does not show content learning,
  transfer, or success of root 23's token learner
  ([1736](../2026-10-11-1736/analysis.md)).

**The current line against different mechanisms.** I searched the literature
for this review; these papers were found and linked. They motivate candidates,
not expected effects in this repository. Costs include agent work.

| Candidate | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| Frozen external acquisition on new content | **Pursue, 8–11 h complete, ceiling 12 h.** Related to [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable Libraries* (2004)](https://www.cs.york.ac.uk/rts/docs/GECCO_2004/Conference%20proceedings/papers/3103/31030531.pdf), which carries functional bias across GP runs. Here the extractor stays fixed while sources and needed computations change. Success extends the recipe; a bounded failure limits it without another extractor rescue. |
| Inherited choice between intact R-all and R-hx at equal donor rate | **Defer, estimated 6–8 h.** [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/) studies indirect selection of encoded variation parameters. This differs from external fitting and could test content discrimination. However, most R-hx replacements are trivial: increasing the intact fraction still increases useful-helper exposure, with both repertoires supplied. Reconsider when a choice between useful competing contents would change a family-learning decision, or inherited content construction needs this control. |
| Parameterized abstraction | **Defer, estimated 12–18 h plus uncertain integration tails.** [Bowers et al., *Top-Down Synthesis for Library Learning* (POPL 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16605) synthesizes corpus abstractions for compression. Parameters could share across input bindings, unlike literal semantic pooling. No current result establishes missing parameterization as the limiting step; it also does not automatically expose computations absent from source syntax. Reconsider a demonstrated binding/representation bottleneck and a fully priced instantiation-and-search test. |
| More seeds for 55 | **Decline this block, about 4 h.** It could resolve the 10% threshold, but would still select a dose of supplied content that loses to the fixed setting. Neither outcome changes the next mechanism. |
