# Can a useful variation bias earn its own inheritance?

Concept plan for [root 55](../questions/55-inherited-library-choice/question.md),
opened by [strategy 1427](../runs/2026-10-11-1427/strategy.md). The steward supplies
the proposal, fixed sizes and decision rule. Only a first probe is allocated.
This is a mechanism calibration, not an experiment registration or a new library learner.

**Question and value.** Can program selection favor an inherited choice to use
an already useful library, and does the resulting choice improve fresh search
after the resident programs are discarded? Root 23 attempted to discover a
22-dimensional token law while also selecting it indirectly. Here we remove
content discovery: the two alternatives already exist. A global search benefit
does not guarantee a reproductive advantage to the individuals carrying its
modifier, especially when crossover spreads their useful offspring material.
That gap is the purchase.

Use the 24 frozen R-all libraries from [1228](../runs/2026-10-11-1228/analysis.md)
and the six development destinations of `x4-order-v1`. R-all cost 0.40 of R-tree
there, across all 24 builds; this motivates a selection opportunity but does
not measure it. Library content remains externally acquired. An inherited
selector would demonstrate selection among supplied variation laws, not evolution
of the library, token meanings or a developmental decoder. It cannot satisfy
the owner's prerequisite for safe code reassignment by itself.

**Bounded build.** Extend the reviewed tree path on `research/main`, starting
from `tree_gp_search.py`, the order runners and their immutable manifests.
Inspection during this strategy found a Python offspring loop with explicit
recipient/mate indices, two elites and a single half-library mutation-donor
decision. These are suitable attachment points; they are not a runtime estimate.
Reuse the current Rust evaluator, primitive accounting and tree insertion.

Each individual carries a binary modifier alongside its tree. One state uses
ordinary mutation donors; the other uses the unchanged R-all law (half library,
half ordinary). The bit does not change execution of the resident tree. Both
states start present, randomly assigned independently of identical-law initial
programs. Choose one interior starting frequency before efficacy inspection.
First test **standing variation, without modifier mutation**. This avoids another
mutation-scale search and asks whether an available useful choice can propagate.
Fix inheritance from the recipient, including crossover and rejected insertions;
copy elite bits with elite trees. No explicit reward for the bit, helper content,
proximity to R-all, or descendants' measured search cost enters parent selection.

Expose all arms to the same fixed, balanced sequence of the six development
tasks. Use equal, fixed generation counts per episode, retaining modifier
frequencies but discarding and independently reinitializing programs between
episodes. Reinitialization must not depend on the bit. Do not stop episodes
when one arm solves: root 23 showed why exposure must be specified. Record first
exact solves and separate pre-solve from maintenance trajectories. The schedule
is an imposed assay of repeated discovery, not a model of natural task switching.

Retain a program–modifier linkage control: shuffle modifiers among individuals
before parent selection, preserving that census's frequency. Also validate a
neutral-bit condition in which both states invoke the same donor law. Neutral
movement supplies a drift/bookkeeping reference; the shuffled arm tests persistent
association, not every possible cause of selection. No crossover-rate sweep or
library-content ablation belongs here.

Required implementation checks include bit/tree alignment through parents,
elites and oversize fallback; modifier-independent initialization; equal exposure;
and recovery of ordinary and R-all operator laws when all bits are fixed off or
on. Keep modifier random streams separate and retain the original search path
for replay. The present `force_cap` path does not record a first exact witness
as a solved result: continuing exposure must explicitly preserve first-hit
reporting and avoid needless post-solve verification. Do not repurpose its flag
without checking those semantics.

**First probe.** Fixed seeds, descriptive output, at most 60 minutes of summed
queue timeouts. Use a performance-blind balanced subset of the saved library
identities if all 24 cannot be priced; never choose helper-rich or fast builds.
Measure linked, shuffled and neutral trajectories, fixation/loss, actual library
draws and rejection, and frozen usefulness. Multiple modifier trajectories under
one library do not become independent library acquisitions.

For the frozen readout, predefine extraction of the final modifier frequency f
from each population. Discard all resident programs. In fresh ordinary searches,
use a global mutation-donor probability 0.5*f for that same immutable library,
with ordinary donors otherwise. This tests the mean variation law, not a claim
that a heterogeneous inherited population is equivalent to a global mixture.
Compare against the initial-frequency law, the shuffled population's final law,
R-tree and fixed R-all. The supplied R-order prior remains the strong practical
reference from 1228; do not present selecting R-all as beating it. Keep acquisition
and scoring random streams disjoint and report per-cell results.

Price frozen initial-mixture versus R-all headroom early in the probe. If both
perform alike, an allele-frequency change may be selectable without buying useful
fresh-search improvement; it would not justify a subsequent usefulness study.
Retain that observation rather than moving the initial frequency after seeing it.
Likewise, fixation alone, beating a degraded shuffled arm alone, and improved
resident fitness alone are insufficient continuation arguments.

Use the existing development roster throughout. The 24 protected order cells
and all protected rectifier cells remain untouched. No fresh-transfer or family
specificity claim follows from this assay. Save every trajectory and failure;
do not select the best endpoint or stop when the desired allele establishes.

**Full cost and exit.** One probe, **4–6 h total** including one preparation of
at most 120 minutes, validation, queue, proposal/reviews, analysis and contingency.
The 1228 scoring anchor is 2,304 ordinary searches in 836 s at sustained load;
fixed-duration modifier exposure is new and must be measured. Price complete
acquisition trajectories and frozen scoring, including full-cap tails, rather
than extrapolating early-stop timings. Return if a trustworthy implementation
does not fit that preparation or complete-stage ceiling.

A conditional independent confirmation costs provisionally **6–8 h more**:
**10–14 h for the complete candidate**, against about 24 h remaining at opening.
It is unallocated. It would use independent modifier trajectories, the whole
saved-library cohort, fresh scoring seeds and one fixed primary usefulness
comparison with a 95% interval, plus the linkage contrast needed for interpretation.
The steward must price a decision-sized comparison from pilot variation; a small
pilot need not itself pass a significance threshold. Charge historical library
acquisition, modifier exposure, extraction and deployment in arithmetic evaluations
and worker seconds. Report the comparison with simply choosing fixed R-all:
even successful modifier selection may have no practical repayment against it.

Review after the probe, or earlier on a build, validity or cost obstacle.
Continue only for a credible, affordable distinction between useful inherited
selection and linkage-free/drift explanations. Tight small effects, no useful
initial-to-final readout, or insufficient headroom end this selector procedure.
Broad uncertainty requires a resolution price. Neither a positive nor a negative
result automatically funds modifier mutation, content capture, new libraries,
more exposure schedules, or a new bank. Complete any later analysis before
**2026-10-12 14:19:15 Europe/Stockholm**.

The literature found for this direction is [Stephens et al., *Self-adaptation
in evolving systems* (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9847423/), on indirect
selection of encoded variation probabilities, and [Serpell and Smith,
*Self-Adaptation of Mutation Operator and Probability for Permutation
Representations in Genetic Algorithms* (2010)](https://uwe-repository.worktribe.com/output/975827),
on inherited operator choice. Neither establishes usefulness here. Our narrow
addition is testing a measured, externally acquired structured GP bias under
individual inheritance and then discarding programs for the frozen readout.
