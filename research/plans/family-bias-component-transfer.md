# Which acquired component carries the family advantage?

Concept plan for [root 10](../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md),
allocated by [strategy 2001](../runs/2026-10-10-2001/strategy.md). The steward supplies
the proposal, fixed size and primary decision rule. This is not an experiment
registration.

**Question and value.** Can the DG-acquired library improve a TS-acquired decoder
on DG tasks, or does the advantage require the DG decoder or its native combination?
[1717](../runs/2026-10-10-1717/analysis.md) found DG-built A8 about threefold cheaper
than TS-built A8 on DG targets. Both combine fitted context and literal block edits.
Locating a portable component chooses whether subsequent acquisition work should
target the table, the repertoire or both. A decoder-only ablation cannot establish
that the removed component works under another table.

**Reuse and bounded build.** Use reviewed `research/main` code at `b5697a1`,
particularly `family_preference_run.py`, `independent_input_run.py`,
`composition_search.py` and `fragment_operator.py`. These runners are absent from
the main checkout; reuse the research branch. The preparation artifact
`experiments/output/2026-10-10/2026-10-10-1717-family-preference-prepare/builds.json`
contains all 24 D and 24 T builds, including their table/library hashes and source
attempt keys. Recover bank/target manifests and method freezes from that run.
No collection, refitting, library re-ranking or new executor is needed.

Source inspection shows the worker receives table and fragments separately, while
the current wrapper binds them by cohort. Add explicit table-owner, library-owner
and operator-mode fields; do not use the old G4 arm label as a shortcut for disabling
blocks. Validate provenance, hash manifests, legacy replay, operator-off behavior,
fixed-table initial population equality and exact D625 verification.

Retain `v2_x4`, inputs, cap, population, selection, ordinary mutation/crossover,
support and conditional re-encoding. Literal blocks retain their existing rate,
length/start law and suffix policy. Do not reinterpret old-alphabet artifacts.
All acquisitions remain in scope, including small libraries. A read-only inventory
found D libraries of 7–32 entries and T libraries all of 32, with lengths 3–6;
there were no empty final libraries. Preserve the declared fallback anyway.

**First experiment.** Price a crossing of the two table sources with three library
choices: D, T and none. Include the native D-table/D-library and T-table/T-library
procedures as fresh-seed references. Reciprocal transplants allow both rescue and
loss to be observed; bare tables show whether libraries are needed at all.

Use all eight DG targets from 1717 as the primary development roster, and its eight
TS targets as the secondary practical boundary. Do not select targets by the size
of the previous contrast. These are now development data, not fresh transfer banks.
The primary question is the benefit of replacing T's library with D's while keeping
T's table fixed on DG. Keep its residual gap to native D, the reverse transplant and
bare-table costs visible. The steward sets one worthwhile replacement effect and
a useful retention bound before scoring; failure to resolve a difference is not
evidence of sufficiency. A 20% cost tolerance is a candidate to price, not a fixed
scientific threshold inherited from another experiment.

Match donors without performance selection. A frozen random bijection between the
24 D and 24 T builds is a bounded candidate: retain each build's native pair and
score both cross-combinations. These are independent acquisition cohorts, not
paired ancestors. Keep each build's repeated table/library uses, shared seeds and
both rosters together in the uncertainty calculation; independent paired tuples
can serve as blocks under that fixed assignment. Do not count 576 hypothetical
donor combinations, cells or search seeds as new acquisitions. Additional donor
assignments, if needed for precision, are nested measurements rather than new builds.

Use common fresh search seeds. Arms sharing a table must have identical initial
programs; different tables may intentionally induce different initial programs.
Retain independent RNG streams for ordinary search and block operations. Avoid
historical-row reuse unless exact replay and pairing are demonstrated; the full
price below includes fresh native references. New G4 scoring is unnecessary for
the primary attribution, whose controls are two useful learned procedures.

**Interpretation.** Rescue under the T table would establish a portable effect of
the D library package. Rescue approaching native D within a useful bound makes
that package a sufficient replacement at this scope. A strong bare-D advantage
with little useful transplant effect instead prioritizes the table. Benefits
confined to native combinations suggest compatibility or joint acquisition;
native table/library artifacts share source history whereas cross-pairs do not,
so this is not isolated evolutionary coadaptation or family-level epistasis.
Report effects conditional on each table, not only a pooled main effect.

Library transplantation moves content, length frequencies and diversity together;
table replacement moves both initialization and ongoing decoding. This locates
useful stored components, not pure token-order effects, semantic modules or a
particular GT join. The complete canonical double-sum predicate is longer than
these windows. Nor does transplantation explain why hard DG sources produced a
better artifact: corpus-size matching would not isolate source difficulty either.
No automatic motif knockout, marginal matching or supply census follows.

Report solve fractions, per-cell costs, build uncertainty and both one-cap and
two-cap failure charges. Near-complete TS solving still permits differences in
solved-search effort; do not label it equality or declare that only a harder bank
could resolve it. An unresolved comparison earns a precision/cost calculation.

**Complete cost and exit.** Allocate **one experiment**, root budget **33 → 34**,
through the next strategy review. Expect **5–8 hours total**: up to two hours
preparation, roughly 2–3 hours queue, and proposal/reviews, analysis and contingency.
Preparation remains at most 120 minutes; summed queue timeouts at most **4 hours**,
reduced against **2026-10-12T14:19:15 Europe/Stockholm**. This is a complete one-cycle
question, not a feasibility stage awaiting an unfunded efficacy test.

1717 scored 2,304 three-arm searches in 3,426 seconds at about 9.8 effective workers.
A costing scenario with 24 donor pairs, two seeds, sixteen targets and six arms is
4,608 searches, roughly two hours at that aggregate rate. Bare tables and hybrids
may be slower; measure representative sustained batches on the existing development
cells (at least 64 jobs), include verifier tails and reporting, and fix an informative
size before target scoring. This scenario is not a sample-size justification. There
is no spare standalone probe allowance at this review. Return on an unaffordable
or uninformative complete design rather than dropping necessary references silently.

Acquisition is sunk for executing this comparison, not for deployment. Bare final
tables still arose from an A8 acquisition that used intermediate libraries: removing
final edits does not establish library-free acquisition. A hybrid that needs both
cohorts pays both source acquisitions unless a cheaper construction is separately
demonstrated. Report arithmetic acquisition-plus-search costs in evaluations and
measured time; no assumed reuse horizon and no repayment from geometric ratios.

Return to strategy after the component result or a validity/build/cost obstruction.
No new root, new bank, new acquisition or follow-on control matrix is funded. The
closest current-learning precedent is [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, PIPE
(1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/); this is an intervention on frozen
external fits and literal libraries, not an implementation of PIPE or inheritance.
