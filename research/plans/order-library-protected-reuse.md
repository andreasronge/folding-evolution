# Does acquired order bias help on the combinations kept out of development?

Concept plan for [root 10](../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md),
allocated by [strategy 1530](../runs/2026-10-11-1530/strategy.md). One substantive
experiment; the steward supplies the proposal, fixed size and decision rule.
This is a research direction, not an experiment registration.

The uncapped semantic library R-all costs 0.40 [0.32, 0.50] of ordinary restarted
tree GP on six order development cells, but the method was changed after seeing
this bank. The next question is whether that frozen, automatically acquired
bias helps on the combinations excluded from development. This closes a specific
generalization gap before investing further in the acquisition mechanism.
It does not test acquisition on a new content family or inherited map learning.

Reuse **all 24 saved libraries from 1228**, without choosing builds by their
coverage, search performance or inheritance outcomes. Recover the source rows,
bank and method manifests from [execution 1228](../runs/2026-10-11-1228/execution.md)
and commit `60ab76697cc1e5592474b4819f4bb3761c5bbbcd`. Reconstruct each library
from its own source corpus and verify its recorded contents. No new source
collection, refitting or helper recognition enters the procedure.

Use the complete 24-cell `split.protected` roster of `x4-order-v1`, bank hash
`2fc0d88c67ae651cbf3b927ecdedcebdd60220e30307ed4f9aa2bb428803d74a`.
It contains four disjoint-pair and twenty overlapping-pair destinations. Keep
both strata and all failures. Preserve the executed admission rule: exact short
aliases excluded; the twelve approximately 84%-agreement source near-aliases
retained. No screening changes or performance-based cell replacement.

Build a scoring wrapper around `order_cap_run.py` and `order_run.py` on the
research branch. The old wrapper hard-codes development schedules and zero
protected searches; update the new wrapper's schedules, provenance checks and
reporting explicitly, while preserving the original runner for replay. Keep
the tree engine, D625 verification, primitive capacity, initializer, half-library
donor mix and eight 65,536-evaluation restart attempts fixed. Smoke and timing
searches use only the six development cells. Freeze the full method, destination
roster and scoring seeds before opening any protected search output.

Compare frozen R-all with ordinary R-tree and the unchanged supplied R-order
prior. The primary question is useful capped arithmetic search-cost reduction
against R-tree. R-order measures the remaining cost of automatic acquisition
relative to supplied domain knowledge; it must remain visible even if the
primary result is positive. Repeating the harmful capped extractor or old sum
prior would not change this decision.

Use fresh paired search seeds and preserve acquisition-build uncertainty. A
baseline run shared across builds is one observation, not multiple independent
replicates. Choose one worthwhile effect and a decision-sized fixed comparison
with a 95% interval and solve-reliability reporting before protected scoring.
The earlier 20% saving is a useful sizing reference, not a new registered rule.
Report all cells and the two overlap strata separately as well as the equal-cell
aggregate; do not turn four disjoint cells into broad task-family replication.
Coverage can explain heterogeneity descriptively, but must not select deployment
libraries or establish helper causality.

Charge each deployed library its historical acquisition, including failures,
extraction and validation, when reporting acquisition-plus-search costs.
Recompute repayment from the new destination costs in evaluations and worker
seconds; the development estimate of five searches does not transfer by decree.
If R-order remains cheaper, report no demonstrated repayment against it.

**Cost and exit.** Allocate one full experiment through the next strategy review,
expected **4–6 hours total**, including preparation/validation, queue, proposal,
reviews, analysis and contingency. Preparation at most 120 minutes and summed
queue timeouts at most two hours, subordinate to the six-hour complete ceiling
and run deadline **2026-10-12 14:19:15 Europe/Stockholm**. The measured anchor is
1228's 2,304 four-arm development jobs in 836 seconds at ten workers; its R-tree,
R-order and R-all means were 5.31, 0.27 and 2.14 worker-seconds. A costing example
of 24 builds × 24 cells × 2 repeats × 3 arms gives 3,456 jobs and about 15 minutes
at those arm-specific rates with ten effective workers. This is neither a
sample-size justification nor measured protected throughput. Allow for changed
tails, verification and lower utilization; price precision from saved development
rows without looking at protected efficacy. Return if an informative complete
comparison cannot fit, rather than substituting a tiny probe.

A material saving with preserved reliability would extend R-all's usefulness to
held-out combinations of this development bank and retain it as a reference for
future learning work. A bounded small gain or loss would end its expansion on
this family without a new explanation. Broad uncertainty needs a resolution
price at review. Neither outcome establishes a fresh-bank transfer result:
the bank already informed the method, and the same twelve helpers underlie both
rosters. Success does not automatically fund another shape, a cap/rate sweep,
or inherited content discovery. Return to strategy after this result or an
earlier provenance, precision, build or cost obstacle.

The closest precedent found in this review is [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico,
*Run Transferable Libraries—Learning Functional Bias in Problem Domains*
(2004)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-24855-2_63), which
transfers acquired functions across GP runs. Here external extraction supplies
fully expanded mutation donors, with no callable abstractions or inherited
decoder. The paper motivates the comparison; it supplies no prediction for
these protected cells.
