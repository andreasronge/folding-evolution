**Does acquiring a library buy more than a supplied sum-donor prior?**

Concept plan for [root 10](../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md),
allocated by [strategy 1019](../runs/2026-10-11-1019/strategy.md). The steward
supplies the proposal, fixed size and primary decision rule. This is a direction
and complete-cost allowance, not an experiment registration.

The [different-source result](../runs/2026-10-11-0833/decision.md) makes this
comparison consequential. TS libraries cost about a twelfth as much to acquire
as DG libraries and retain a large gate-sum B/C saving. Their different content
and unresolved performance difference suggest that small ADD-rich donors could
suffice. That is an observational hypothesis. Uniform relabelling previously
lost much of the benefit, but it did not test a deliberately useful supplied
prior. Neither result establishes that learning is necessary.

**What to build.** Extend the reviewed tree-library path on `research/main`
(latest study `28db964`), especially `tree_source_run.py`, `tree_source_report.py`,
`tree_library.py` and `tree_gp_search.py`. These runners are absent from the
current `main` checkout. Reuse the restart wrapper, compiler, exact verifier,
bank manifests, provenance and seed checks. Add one fixed donor constructor;
no new sources, learner, executor or task bank is required.

Construct one small alphabet-symmetric prior from ordinary ADD expressions over
indexed inputs and existing constants. A concrete candidate is equal mass on
two classes: all distinct-input pair sums, and all indexed-input-plus-constant
sums, uniform within each class. Canonicalize commutative duplicates and state
the treatment of operand orientation. Use every indexed input and every existing
constant symmetrically; do not select pairs or constants from target formulas.
The steward should freeze one such transparent rule before new efficacy scoring.
Do not choose among donor variants by which wins a timing batch.

The constructor must take only the alphabet and its fixed rule as inputs. Do not
copy learned entry counts, source frequencies, library sizes, predicate identities
or per-build mixtures. Matching those would make this another learned-artifact
replacement. A fixed repertoire need not share the learned libraries' entry
count or size law: the question is practical substitution, not an isolated
single-component mechanism. Audit these differences and realized insertion and
oversize-rejection rates instead of retuning them away.

Call this **source-free at execution, informed by development results**. Choosing
ADD after seeing this program's results supplies human knowledge. It is not a
task-blind baseline or independent evidence that an arbitrary task family can be
handled without learning. Keep all ordinary mutation support; half of mutation
donor draws use the fixed prior, as in the acquired-library procedure. Preserve
initialization, lexicase, crossover, 32-token capacity and the common eight-attempt
restart schedule. Validate the expanded primitives and the ordinary-path replay.

**First experiment.** On all twelve existing gate-sum B/C cells, compare this
prior with all 24 saved TS libraries and contemporaneous ordinary R-tree. Use
fresh search populations and seeds; recover the roster and libraries from 0833's
artifacts, with their hashes. This is a development-bank replacement question.
It does not require another DG cohort, split A, native TS or a fresh bank.

Preserve the acquisition-build uncertainty of the TS comparator and the paired
search blocks. The deterministic hand prior has no independent acquisition
replicates; assigning it 24 scoring blocks does not create them. Shared baseline
rows cannot be counted multiple times as independent evidence. Report arithmetic
capped evaluations, a 95% interval, solve reliability, each split and cell,
failure sensitivity, and worker time under the same sustained load.

The primary question is whether a practical acquired-library advantage remains
over this fixed alternative. A 1.25 hand/learned cost ratio is a useful sizing
reference from the latest decision, not a requirement to resolve tiny differences.
The steward fixes the margin and adequate size before confirmation. Useful
retention also requires the hand procedure to help relative to ordinary R-tree
and to retain acceptable solve reliability; two ineffective procedures do not
establish success. An unresolved comparison needs a precision and full-cost
estimate, not an equality label.

Charge TS acquisition once per deployed library, including its failed attempts,
extraction and verification. Saved acquisition is free for executing this
comparison but remains part of deployment cost. Show acquisition-plus-search
cost against both R-tree and the hand prior across reuse horizons; do not assume
the owner's horizon. Small per-search differences may still make acquisition
worthwhile at long horizons even if the fixed prior passes a retention margin.
The hand constructor has zero evolutionary acquisition, but its construction
time and human-supplied rule should remain explicit.

**Answers and next choice.** Retention would establish a cheaper way to supply
much of this B/C advantage and make it the comparator for subsequent learning.
It would not erase learned tape-context results or the arrangement-sensitive
advantage on predicate-matched split A. A worthwhile learned advantage supports
paying for the TS recipe relative to this one prior; it cannot prove that no
other hand prior suffices or identify the responsible feature. Failure must not
trigger a succession of hand-prior variants.
