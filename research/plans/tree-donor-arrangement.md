# Must a transferable donor retain the learned arrangement of its primitives?

Concept plan for [root 10](../questions/10-compositional-map-transfer/question.md),
allocated by [strategy 0524](../runs/2026-10-11-0524/strategy.md). The steward
supplies the proposal, fixed size and decision rule. This is a research direction,
not an experiment registration.

**Question and value.** On gate-sum's two predicate-unmatched splits, can donors
with the learned libraries' shapes, sizes and primitive counts, but rearranged
labels, replace the intact libraries? The frozen procedure costs about a third
of ordinary restarted tree search on these splits. Its size control does not
match input or ADD supply. Whether the specific arrangements are needed changes
the next acquisition target: distributions over labels and shapes, or intact
expressions. It does not require another bank or source collection.

This narrows the steward's proposed ingredient matrix. Pair-sums-only and
predicate-only donors change size, rejection and primitive supply together.
They can identify useful replacement procedures, but cannot alone separate
those explanations. First buy one interpretable replacement comparison.

**Bounded build.** Reuse the reviewed `research/main` implementation at
`1f02bb7`: `tree_library.py`, `tree_gp_search.py`, `tree_library_run.py` and
the gate-sum runner/reporting. These experiment modules are absent from the
current `main` checkout. Retain all 24 source acquisitions from 0150, verified
against their saved source rows; no target solver or canonical enters a donor.

A concrete candidate is a frozen randomized library built as follows. Retain
each entry's ordered arity skeleton and each node's compiled-token width. Within
each library, pool labels by entry node count, entry compiled length, node arity
and node compiled width, then permute labels among the corresponding slots.
This preserves the library's primitive counts exactly within those size classes,
each entry's node count and compiled length, and its tree validity. Binary ADD
and GT labels can exchange slots; indexed inputs can exchange leaf slots.
Constants and two-token readouts do not exchange widths. It changes which
primitives occur together and where they occur, while retaining substantial
shape information. It is **not** a context-free program generator.

The source-only [planning audit](../runs/2026-10-11-0524/donor-planning-audit.json)
checked four fixed construction realizations per build. Across 181 entries,
142–155 changed after ignoring commutative ADD child order; every build changed
in every realization. Primitive counts, arity sequences and widths were
preserved exactly. No search or efficacy measurement ran. This establishes
combinatorial room, not a validated intervention or a useful replacement.

Before scoring, validate complete tree parsing, compiler/interpreter/Rust
agreement and the exact invariants. Retain duplicate entries with multiplicity,
unchanged entries and accidental useful expressions; rejecting these would
condition the control on desired outcomes and alter its law. Describe remaining
dependencies and audit donor behaviours on D625, without selecting controls by
target performance. Do not optimize the permutation or demand that it destroys
every known motif. Any alternative construction needs equally explicit matching
and scope, rather than an unsupported claim to match all token supply.

Use several independently randomized control libraries per acquisition if needed
to estimate construction variability. Freeze their count and seed rule before
confirmation. They are nested in the acquisition, not additional independent
acquisitions. Construction selection, deduplication or choosing the worst control
would invalidate the intended comparison. Do not expand into a generator-fitting
project if a bounded matched construction cannot be validated.

**First experiment.** Compare intact R-lib, the rearranged-library procedure,
and contemporaneous ordinary R-tree on **all twelve B/C gate-sum cells**, equally
weighted, with each split reported separately. They are development data after
0348; this is a mechanism/replacement study. Split A's known predicate match is
not the primary question. No additional DG or fresh-shape matrix is necessary.

Keep the initializer, population, crossover, selection, exact verifier, 32-token
capacity and common eight-attempt restart policy unchanged. Preserve the existing
half-of-mutation-events library-draw rule and ordinary-donor fallback. The
realized insertion fraction is an outcome; do not retune to the historical
approximately 11% of children. Match initial populations and case seeds, audit
seed disjointness, preserve ordinary search RNG and record actual donor draws,
insertions and oversize rejections. Equal candidate sizes do not guarantee equal
rejection rates once populations diverge.

The primary decision is whether replacing intact entries loses a worthwhile
amount of search performance. Choose one arithmetic capped-cost contrast and
95% interval, a meaningful retention bound and adequate fixed size before
confirmation. Keep reliability and the comparison with R-tree visible so two
ineffective procedures cannot count as successful retention. A roughly 20%
tolerated cost increase is a sizing scenario for the steward, not a registered
threshold. Retain acquisition-level uncertainty, construction variation and
per-cell results. A nonsignificant difference is not evidence of retention.

**Answers and limits.** Useful retention would show that these specific learned
label arrangements are unnecessary at the chosen resolution, conditional on
retained shapes and size-conditioned primitive counts. That would justify
pricing a simpler statistical acquisition target. It would not show that raw
token frequencies alone suffice, or that the replacement is cheaper to learn:
it still borrows the learned shape/count information. A resolved useful intact
advantage establishes information beyond the matched properties and supports
retaining joint expressions for the next acquisition/boundary test. It does
not identify pair sums, predicate fragments or a causal search trajectory.
Harm to the control without a reproduced R-lib/R-tree benefit is insufficient.
Broad intervals require a resolution price; they do not establish either account.

Report evaluation and worker-time costs separately. Charge original source
searches, including failures and extraction, to a newly deployed intact library;
charge the same source acquisition plus construction to the rearranged procedure.
Saved files make execution cheap, not deployment acquisition free. Report
arithmetic acquisition-plus-search curves without assuming a reuse horizon.
The old pooled 55-search repayment is not the repayment on B/C alone.

**Complete price and exit.** One substantive experiment through the next strategy
review, expected **4–6 hours total**: roughly 1–1.5 h implementation/validation,
0.75–2 h calibration/scoring and 2–2.5 h proposal, reviews, analysis and contingency.
Preparation remains at most 120 minutes; summed queue timeouts at most three
hours. These are planning allowances, to replace with measured sustained rates
and a decision-sized precision estimate before admission.

0348 completed 5,184 three-arm searches in 2,317 seconds, plus 275 seconds
preparation. A costing example retaining 24 builds, twelve cells and four
repeats is 1,152 jobs per arm, 3,456 total. Control realizations can be balanced
within a fixed scoring allocation, but enough repeats are needed to retain both
construction and search uncertainty. This is not a prescribed sample size.
The altered controls may have slower tails: price them, reporting and verification
on development data instead of importing the pooled old rate as a guarantee.

The library precedent is [Keijzer, Ryan and Cattolico, *Run Transferable
Libraries* (2004)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-24855-2_63).
The alternative acquisition family is [Salustowicz and Schmidhuber, *Probabilistic
Incremental Program Evolution* (1997)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10021756/),
which learns a program-generating distribution from selected programs. Both
papers were found in this review's literature search. This frozen replacement
test implements neither PIPE updates nor inherited decoder evolution.
